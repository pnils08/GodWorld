# Engine.206 build card review

**Disposition (engine-sheet, 2026-10-04 02:35):** all 8 findings verified against the code and folded — the card's Cut was rewritten (sports plan Task 6 §Cut (revised)): the bars ride the economy's SPORTS_WEEK ripple instead of filing their own, transit emits one ripple per franchise home week at `venue[0]`, the seed builder lets a birth lead over a carryover, 20-ID cap and mixed-cluster accepted and written down, pointers corrected.

**Verdict: HOLD.** The cause-type routing is real, but the proposed grouping cannot guarantee one seed for one sports week. Revise the card and its acceptance gates before cutting code. This is a current-source review, not a bench, deployment, or live-Sheet verification; the card's C110–C118 and live C107–C109 observations were not re-read here.

## Findings

1. **BLOCKER — `economic-event|hood` is broader than the sports event.** The card promises a venue bar ripple and `SPORTS_WEEK` as one seed (`docs/plans/2026-09-11-sports-as-a-lived-system.md:464,472`). The builder keys *every* ripple by causeType and normalized hood, without CauseId, effectType, or source (`phase07-evening-media/buildContractSeeds.js:435-443`). A business closure is already `economic-event` in that hood (`phase05-citizens/applyBusinessDynamics.js:642-651`); economic carryovers also use it (`phase06-analysis/economicRippleEngine.js:263-292`). The resulting seed can combine a closure, bars, and one or more franchise weeks. **Fix:** specify an event-identity boundary or explicitly accept and test the mixed cluster; test concurrent same-hood closure and carryover, not just the isolated pair.

2. **BLOCKER — even the isolated pair is conditional, and the lead is the bars.** Phase 5 bars run before Phase 6 economy, then Phase 7 seeds (`phase01-config/godWorldEngine2.js:598,652,712`), while the builder takes the first ripple as lead (`phase07-evening-media/buildContractSeeds.js:449-452`). With no earlier closure, the proposed bar ripple supplies `What`, `Trend`, and SeedID, including a single-Cycle trend for a three-Cycle economic ripple (`phase07-evening-media/buildContractSeeds.js:554-572`; `phase06-analysis/economicRippleEngine.js:72-74`). More decisively, `SPORTS_WEEK` is absent at `|signed| < .15`, citywide when home-game share is below .5, and placed at only `venue[0]` when home-majority (`phase06-analysis/economicRippleEngine.js:466-483`). Bars can still respond to a smaller signed week, any home venue, and away nightlife (`utilities/sportsWeekRecord.js:270-281`). A legacy two-hood venue therefore has bars in both hoods but an economic ripple only in the first. **Fix:** condition the merge promise on the actual economic gate and primary hood, or change event/geography semantics deliberately; test below-.15, partial-home, two-venue, and earlier-closure cases.

3. **HIGH — the home-week unit gate demands a citywide row that can never appear for a pure home week.** The card says a home week produces a venue ripple *plus* a citywide ripple for the rest (`docs/plans/2026-09-11-sports-as-a-lived-system.md:472`). With `venueShare = 1`, `sportsBarTerm_` gives a nonvenue bar zero unless another franchise contributes an away term (`utilities/sportsWeekRecord.js:275-279`). The business pass records only nonzero sports terms (`phase05-citizens/applyBusinessDynamics.js:585-607`); its existing home-loss fixture confirms the off-venue bar is untouched (`phase05-citizens/applyBusinessDynamics.test.js:345-361`). **Fix:** expect a citywide row only when off-venue bars actually moved; use a separate mixed home/away fixture for that branch.

4. **HIGH — one merged magnitude and label misstate two different effects.** The proposed bar magnitude is mean Growth_Rate percentage points (`docs/plans/2026-09-11-sports-as-a-lived-system.md:464`; `phase05-citizens/applyBusinessDynamics.js:699-715`); the economic ripple magnitude is `round(15 × signed)` impact points (`phase06-analysis/economicRippleEngine.js:480-482`). The seed builder adds them numerically, selects major/texture from that sum, and takes the lead's effectType and duration (`phase07-evening-media/buildContractSeeds.js:458-462,483-487,554-573`). Opposite signs can cancel and a bar-first merged seed reads `game-week-bars`/single-cycle while carrying a three-Cycle economy cause. **Fix:** define a common magnitude and representation, or keep separate effects with explicit linkage; assert the displayed What, Magnitude, Trend, and Class under opposite signs.

5. **HIGH — the claimed complete Businesses list is capped at 20 IDs per ripple.** `recordRipple_` persists only the first 20 target IDs and pushes only those 20 into `S.rippleEvents` (`utilities/rippleLedger.js:22,53-56,74-91`). The builder collects businesses only from those target IDs (`phase07-evening-media/buildContractSeeds.js:464-475,498-509`). One venue hood with more than 20 affected bars will show a mean/count for all bars but name at most 20. **Fix:** design bounded sharding or an explicit truncation contract and test over-cap hoods with the real ripple writer; an isolated stub that receives every ID cannot prove deck completeness.

6. **HIGH — transit's promotion and causal magnitude gates need correction.** `gameDayLoadByHood_` includes a venue whenever `h > 0`, even if `unsigned × venueShare` is zero, and takes the maximum for shared hoods (`phase02-world-state/updateTransitMetrics.js:682-693`). Stations apply `0.3 × max(load of served hoods)`, not one additive lift per hood (`phase02-world-state/updateTransitMetrics.js:373-383`); corridor traffic uses a different `.25` factor (`phase02-world-state/updateTransitMetrics.js:515-519,87-90`). A ripple for every returned hood can therefore claim a zero or duplicate rider effect. The card's “full home week = .30” is a synthetic load of 1; derived unsigned uses `1-exp(-g/3)` times stakes and franchise weight, so finite recorded weeks remain below that (`utilities/sportsWeekRecord.js:162-164,205-214`). A single `.3 × load >= .05` is already major, and three same-type/hood effects are major regardless of magnitude (`phase07-evening-media/buildContractSeeds.js:27-29,90-95`); all seed classes are written to the deck (`phase10-persistence/saveV3Seeds.js:111-143`). **Fix:** require positive measured lift, state whether the ripple represents a hood load or an actual station change, and bound expected row/class counts over ordinary home weeks and coincident disruptions. Major class alone is not proof of article promotion.

7. **MEDIUM — another causeType reader changes citizen matching.** Moving bars from `sports` to `economic-event` switches the seed builder's preferred LifeHistory event pattern from game/playoff/fan terms to work/job/shop/customer terms (`phase07-evening-media/buildContractSeeds.js:139-149,172-181,198-224`). The card names the domain map and Node desk map but misses this semantic reader (`docs/plans/2026-09-11-sports-as-a-lived-system.md:457,466`). The Node world summary also groups ledger prose by CauseType (`scripts/buildWorldSummary.js:252-272`) before routing each ledger row to desk signals (`scripts/buildWorldSummary.js:1284-1293`). **Fix:** add matching/prose assertions for the new economic bar cause and both Node views; decide whether the sports causal detail survives a business-domain seed. `routePatternSeeds.js` excludes the moved rows by its allowlist (`scripts/engine-auditor/routePatternSeeds.js:69-98`); repository search found no scheduled caller, but its manual `--apply` remains a write path (`scripts/engine-auditor/routePatternSeeds.js:755-791`).

8. **LOW — correct source pointers and the duplicate-run premise.** The card cites `buildContractSeeds.js:463` for the cluster key; that line deduplicates cause prose, while the key is at `:440` (`phase07-evening-media/buildContractSeeds.js:440,461-463`). `economicRippleEngine.js:474` is the threshold; creation and placement are `:475-483` (`phase06-analysis/economicRippleEngine.js:473-483`). The economy file's “runs twice” comment is stale (`phase06-analysis/economicRippleEngine.js:264`): Phase 6 owns the scheduled call and Phase 8 removed its economic registry entry (`phase01-config/godWorldEngine2.js:652,751`; `phase08-v3-chicago/v3Integration.js:85-103`). Its birth/carryover guards remain, but the proposed same-Cycle merge must be evaluated with one scheduled economic pass. **Fix:** update pointers and base tests on current phase order, not the stale comment.

## Verified boundaries

- `CONTRACT_SEED_DOMAIN` maps `economic-event` to ECONOMIC and `transit-event` to CIVIC; desk mapping then sends those to business and civic (`phase07-evening-media/buildContractSeeds.js:34-55,58-67,532-559`). `recordRipple_` carries CauseType and target geography into the ledger and `S.rippleEvents`, with no Domain field (`utilities/rippleLedger.js:17-20,59-92`). The card's basic causeType strategy is valid.
- The existing gridlock ripple is citywide, `transit-event`, magnitude `.02`, and requires `avgTraffic >= 78` plus a major event or game day (`phase02-world-state/updateTransitMetrics.js:265,300-314`). Ordinary game-day station and corridor modifiers currently have no corresponding ripple (`phase02-world-state/updateTransitMetrics.js:377-383,512-520`). The proposed G2 carrier addresses a real gap.
- The parent-hood choice in the card is necessary and can match the sports venue key: business dynamics resolves child areas before `sportsBarTerm_` (`phase05-citizens/applyBusinessDynamics.js:550-552,585-590`), while `sportsWeek.venue` comes from the canonical franchise venue list (`phase02-world-state/applySportsSeason.js:351-355`). Grouping by the raw Business_Ledger child label would break that join. The seed backdrop indexes raw business Neighborhood separately, but exact BIZ_ID targets resolve through `bizById` (`phase07-evening-media/buildContractSeeds.js:311-320,498-509`).
