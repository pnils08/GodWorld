# Baylight District, in their own words

## Ernesto Quintero — 3B/1B , Oakland A's

> Man, this cycle's been a lot to process. First the Mesa trade—that one stings, even if I get why Paulson made it. Carmen had that fire in him, you know? That first start against the Cubs was something else. But this game's about depth and the long haul, and bringing in Giancarlo... I've seen the kid's glove work in spring training. That A potential is real. Kevin Clark's been grinding, and now he's got real competition. That's baseball. And Mark Aitken... look, I've played behind that man for two seasons. He's the heart of this clubhouse, the guy who shows up early, signs every autograph, takes the rookies under his wing. The Angels sniffing around him? That's a gut punch. But I've also seen what he means to this city. Oakland raised him. I'm hoping he remembers that when the offers come in. Me? I'm just trying to stay ready. The mood around the complex is tense, but that's when the real work starts. I'm putting in the extra cage time, staying late on the infield work. When the season rolls around, I want them to know they can count on me at first or third, wherever they need me.
