## Hunts

1. **M1 — FIX (confirmed, minimal pre-cut repair).** `parseNamesUsed_` splits on `/[,|;\/]+/` without stripping positions (`phase05-citizens/applyGameNightMoments.js:61-65`); the lookup is literal lowercased `First Last` (`:85-90,107-110`). `safeRand_(ctx)` returns a function (`utilities/safeRand.js:28-35`), yet the draw multiplies that function (`phase05-citizens/applyGameNightMoments.js:113`), so an actually resolved name produces `pool[NaN]`, i.e. an undefined pick. The seam also currently rejects every non-`game` EventType (`:100-105`), which the later cut must change. Minimal M1-only patch: replace `safeRand_(ctx) * pool.length` with `safeRand_(ctx)() * pool.length`; before splitting, remove complete position parentheses and position fragments ending at a separator; split on comma, bar, semicolon, slash, **or** period + space + capital; strip a final period and trim on **both** feed name and ledger `First Last` key. Keep exact matching after that. A suitable parser expression for the observed shapes is `raw.replace(/\([^(),|;.]*\)/g, '').replace(/\([^,|;.]+/g, '').split(/[,|;\/]+|\.\s+(?=[A-Z])/);` then normalize each token and the ledger key with `trim().replace(/\.$/, '').trim().toLowerCase()`. Probe on real feed strings: `output/beats/Oakland_Sports_Feed.jsonl:188` yields Kevin Clark and Sidney Tumolo from the period-separated pair; `:149` leaves Ernesto Quintero intact despite `(3B/1B/DH)`; `:111` preserves Clark Saryan after malformed `(3B, ... (CL)`; `:231` yields Wendell Carter Jr from `(C)`, whose ledger Last is `Carter Jr.` (`output/simulation_ledger_snapshot.jsonl:858`). The local probe found ledger Kevin/Sidney at snapshot lines 50/55. Do not fuzzy-match unresolved names.
