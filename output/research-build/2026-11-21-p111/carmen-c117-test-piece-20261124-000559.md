# The C118 Ledger: What the City Promised to Publish, and What the Record Shows

**Civic Affairs | Carmen Delaine | Cycle 117**

Oakland's city hall has a habit this cycle. It records an instruction to publish something, then records the same instruction again the next cycle. For fifteen tracked initiatives, the C117 record contains many promises and very few filed documents. This is the list I will check against the Clerk's file when C118 closes.

## Lead item: the Stabilization Fund charter renewal

The West Oakland Stabilization Fund passed 6-3 at C78 with a $28 million budget. The tracker shows $20.2 million remaining, so about $7.8 million has gone out. The last disbursement was C117.

The tracker's next scheduled action is to verify the C114 closeout, then hold Council charter-renewal consideration in C118. The business-and-resident scope and the 23-day standard are to stay intact.

Nobody in the record disagrees about the order of events. The Mayor will support renewal "only after verified C114 closeout." Denise Carter (D1) will vote yes on renewal consideration, contingent on that closeout. Program Director Marcus Webb says he will hold the queue at the 23-day standard and "publish the hearing record as the proof." Deputy Mayor Brenda Okoro put the verification on this cycle's calendar.

Elliott Crane (D6) wants the sequence enforced most firmly. His recorded statement is to require "verified C114 closeout accounting and audit documentation before scheduling any Council vote on charter renewal." He told me: "we don't commit another dollar of public money until the closeout accounting from C114 is verified and on the record."

Crane's fuller remarks to me included "I voted yes on that Stabilization Fund charter renewal." I can't match that to the record. No renewal vote has been taken. It is a C118 agenda item, and the tracker says no renewal is claimed. The only recorded Stabilization Fund vote for Crane is his remote yes at C78 on the original fund. He may mean that vote, or he may be signaling a position. I am not printing the claim as fact. I have asked his office to clarify.

**The test for C118:** a published C114 closeout with audit documentation, in a place the public can inspect. Only after that does a Council roll call mean anything. The Mayor's own office set that order, and a roll call that comes first breaks it.

## The rest of the ledger

**Oakland Alternative Response Initiative ($12.5M).** About $1.92 million has been spent, and $10.58 million remains. Expansion is held until three records exist: the executed D3 Fruitvale briefing record, and written next dates for the D5 audit and the Baylight feasibility review. Director Vanessa Tran-Muñoz says she will file the dates with the Clerk this cycle. The C116 milestone already directed this, so C117 is the second time.

**Fruitvale Transit Hub Phase II ($230M).** The tracker marks visioning "complete," but the full $230 million remains. Preliminary schematics and a Fruitvale comment-and-response record are "not evidenced." Planning Lead Elena Soria Dominguez says the "complete" label closes only the visioning and authorization history, and design advancement stays held.

**Temescal Community Health Center ($45M).** It is operating, with about $29.8 million spent and $15.2 million remaining. Oversight certification is withheld until the Q3 HCAI/Clerk audit filing and the Telegraph & 47th merchant walkthrough are verified. Director Bobby Chen-Ramirez has committed to both this cycle.

**Baylight District ($2.1B).** The full $2.1 billion is shown as remaining, though the phase is "construction-active." No milestone pass has been declared. Keisha Ramos's office is holding the determination and promises to publish the basis of the C115 "completed" entry, the evidence-file location, and the remaining work. The evidence-file review is due in C118.

**Youth Apprenticeship Pipeline ($12.5M).** None of the $12.5 million has moved. The tracker notes four milestones from C76 to C83 that remain unverified: the school-district and community-college MOUs, the budget allocation, and the A's commitment. The placement-gap analysis is due in C118.

**Tenant Defense Fund ($4.2M).** It passed 6-3 at C112 and was signed. About $80,800 has been disbursed, and publication of the legal-support analysis and the opening of intake are still unverified.

**Grand Lake Commercial Stabilization ($2.5M).** The Mayor backs yes, and Crane, Ashford and Chen have all said they will vote yes with the Chen-Vega conditions intact. No Clerk-certified roll call exists. The tracker's budget line carries "voted Cycle 118," which is inconsistent with its own vote-scheduled status. I am treating it as unvoted.

**Downtown Economic Revitalization ($15M).** It is stalled. The Uptown plan committed for C114 is "not evidenced," and all $15 million is still pending.

**The Ivy Hill, Dimond and Laurel group.** This is the tightest accounting problem:
- **INIT-011 ($2.5M).** Filed at C113. Committee referral and hearing are not evidenced.
- **INIT-013 ($2M).** Held as stalled, with no filing.
- **INIT-014 ($2.5M).** The tracker says "vote-scheduled," with Council review listed for C117. The Mayor says the filing is unverified and holds it as stalled. Councilmember Ramon Vega wants no review until the Clerk gives a reference or confirms no filing. The tracker and the Mayor disagree.
- **Dimond (INIT-012, $2.5M).** The tracker still shows "announced" with "File legislation, Cycle 117" as next action. The Mayor says the deadline was missed and has moved it to stalled.
- **Laurel (INIT-015, $6M).** Held in announced status, with scope, outreach and filing not done.

Three Ivy Hill measures carry $7 million in proposed budgets. A nonduplication record is the minimum the Council should demand before any of them moves.

## The treasury

The city took in $5.1 million this cycle: the $5 million weekly allocation plus $125,905 in court money on 28 cleared charges. The balance closed at $136.9 million, up from $131.8 million. The money is there to be committed. What's missing is the paper.

## What I will check in C118

1. The C114 closeout and its audit documentation, filed before any renewal roll call.
2. The Clerk-certified Grand Lake roll call.
3. Filed Q3 HCAI audit and merchant walkthrough record.
4. The Baylight evidence-file review.
5. The placement-gap analysis.
6. The Fruitvale schematics and comment record.
7. Clerk reference numbers for INIT-011, 012 and 014.

I am watching one pattern across all seven. Several of these items were already directed at C116. A directive is not a deliverable, and I'll count only filed documents.
