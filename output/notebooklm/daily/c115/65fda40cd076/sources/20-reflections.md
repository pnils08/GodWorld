# Uptown, in their own words

## Omar Amici — Construction laborer

> is shifting away from the people who've been here all along. It's like the neighborhood I knew is being priced out.

## Priya Narang — EMS DIRECTOR

> The A's winning season still brings a lot of energy to Uptown. I started a jar on my counter to save up for season tickets next year. It's not just about the games, though - when they're doing well, the whole neighborhood feels it. The bars and galleries stay packed, and you can feel the energy.

## Toto Foto — Theater Director

> I love walking through Uptown on a busy Saturday like that. The market was buzzing, and it felt like everyone was out enjoying the day. I saw some familiar faces, exchanged a few hellos. The energy's always good there.

## Caroline Cabrera — After-School Program Director

> I still see that numbers don't tell the whole story here in Uptown. The changes we're going through - with rents going up and families having to stretch to stay - it's not just about statistics. I've seen it firsthand with the kids in my after-school program and the people I talk to around the neighborhood. It's about real lives.
