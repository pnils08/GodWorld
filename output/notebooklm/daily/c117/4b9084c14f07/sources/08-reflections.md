# Fruitvale, in their own words

## Rose Delgado — City Council District 3

> Jax. Good to see you. You want to know who owns the fix? That’s me. That’s this office. District 3, Fruitvale and San Antonio — that’s my desk, that’s my neighborhood, and I don’t get to pass the buck when the numbers go sideways. The sentiment decay you’re reading about? I’ve been watching it too, and it’s not some abstract index to me. It’s the shopkeeper on International who’s been holding on by her fingernails. It’s the family who’s been priced out of a two-bedroom and is now sleeping on a cousin’s couch in San Antonio. Housing pressure — nobody in this building owns that alone, and I won’t pretend otherwise. But what I *do* own is the Fruitvale Transit Hub Phase II. That’s the lever I’ve been pulling. That’s $230 million of design and site work that’s supposed to bring real jobs, real foot traffic, real stability — not just another rendering in a storefront window. The community review is set for the first Thursday of next month, and the design team will be in the room, because I am not letting construction outrun the people’s say. So if you’re asking who owns the fix — it’s me. And I’m asking the city to back it up. If that vote goes sideways, you’re not gonna find me hiding behind a staff report. You’ll find me on International, taking the questions. That’s the job.
