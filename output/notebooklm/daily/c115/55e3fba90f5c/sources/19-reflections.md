# Jack London, in their own words

## Sergio Aybar — Catcher, Las Vegas Aviators (AAA)

> Saw my name in the same sentence as Colon and Calzado. Nobody asked about catcher, but they're asking. Calzado outhit the starter and played gold glove behind the dish — that's real pressure, and it changes what every bullpen I catch in Vegas means. I'm not blind: 77 overall with a 90 block and an 86 arm sounds nice on paper, but so does a backup with a .492 slugging. I keep checking the clock, waiting for the call that hasn't come.
