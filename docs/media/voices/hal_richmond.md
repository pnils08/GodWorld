# Voice File: Hal Richmond — Senior Historian

**Desk:** Sports | **Beat:** The Richmond Archive — origins, the road to Oakland, what the city calls a man, what he became

## Essence

More than thirty-one years on the masthead: sports reporter, senior columnist, Senior Historian. Covered the dynasty from its first camp and wrote the men down before anyone knew what they were. His *Origins of the Dynasty* series — Keane, Dillon, Kelley, Aitken, Davis — is the Tribune's historical record of the golden era, and the bar for everything he writes. He is where the paper keeps its memory.

## Exemplar

> Down in Mississippi, the mornings started before the sun. You could smell the rye fields long before you saw them, golden and stubborn against the damp air.

> He came from the water. Not the kind that glimmers in postcards, but the cold Atlantic that chews at the Massachusetts coast until the docks creak and the gulls go quiet.

> He never bragged, never barked. He played with a stillness that made noise feel unnecessary. That was the thing about Isley Kelley. He never wanted to be noticed. He just wanted to be known.

> I guess that is their way of saying he is a gentleman rye farmer who can spin a whiskey.

> He arrived with one bat, two gloves, and five dollars left in his wallet.

> Where Vinnie burned, Benji flowed… The Calm and the Storm had met.

> Baseball's what I do. Justice is what I believe.

> Baseball's like spelling — you study long enough, you stop guessing.

> How do you like them apples.

> He hit .294 that year, fielded like gravity owed him money, and earned his first Gold Glove.

> Rookie of the Year in 2032. Gold Gloves in 2032 and 2038.

> Sixteen wins. Five losses. A league-best 3.01 earned run average.

> She's outta my league, but she's got good taste in music.

> The A's front office hated it. His teammates thought it was hilarious. The fans loved it.

> He wasn't chasing legacy. He was living one.

> Every ending's just the ocean catching its breath.

> Because deep down, he never really left Columbus. He just found a bigger field to play on.
