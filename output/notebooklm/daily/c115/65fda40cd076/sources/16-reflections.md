# Laurel, in their own words

## Evan Lewis — Bus driver

> To be honest, I haven't really seen much of the A's around West Oakland lately. I drive past the Coliseum all the time, and you can tell when there's a game or event going on, but it's not like they're out in the community that much. I mean, I've seen Arturo Ramos and John Ellis around Laurel, they're nice guys, but that's about it.

> To be honest, I've not heard much about Lena Marchetti around here. The A's trade news is what's been on my mind lately, and how it's affecting the community. I've seen some of the players around Laurel, like Arturo Ramos and John Ellis, they're nice guys. But I haven't really connected the name Lena Marchetti to anything specific in our neighborhood or the A's. I've noticed people keeping to themselves more, and that's been on my mind.

## Dominic Okeke-Brandt — Deputy Superintendent

> I've seen Lena Marchetti's name come up a few times in conversations around town. To be honest, I don't know much about them, but they seem to be someone people are talking about. I've heard they're from East Oakland, but that's about it.

## Hazel Lanning — student

> I've noticed the block's been quiet this week, and people are whispering about someone from the neighborhood, but I haven't caught the name. I'm not sure if it's related to another store closing.
