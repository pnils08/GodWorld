# Chinatown, in their own words

## Marcus Osei — Deputy Mayor (Econ Dev)

> What's rattling me is seeing how many folks in Chinatown are struggling, and it's not just us. Downtown Oakland's got its own issues. If 13 people there are making headlines, that's 13 too many. We need to make sure our economic development plans are actually helping people on the ground, not just the ones with the loudest voices.

## Manju Abebe — Plumber

> Marcus and I go back longer than most people know, but we don't talk about it much. He's the Deputy Mayor now, and I'm a plumber who fixes his neighbor's sinks. We're not enemies or anything—he's always been straight with me when our paths cross, which isn't often anymore. But there's a distance that wasn't there before, like we're both aware of the different worlds we live in now and neither of us wants to pretend otherwise. I respect him, and I think he respects me, but we're not the kind of friends who call each other up. That's just the truth of it.
