# engine.290 — Transit Rides the Sim Plan

**Builder's words (2026-10-14):** "tie the transit system to the sim so it peaks and slows with the sim, make sure it is staffed enough with having at least 1 employee as conductor, engineer, etc."

**Builder's words (2026-10-15):** "align the transit system with the sim , same clocks, avoid hard coded math where possible, tie is which hoods are where people work , where the hot spots are , and this is using sim reality not real world. Transit ledgers , hood ledgers , business ledgers , sports feed , etc. to determine ridership , you can have a chaos car that creates off chance transit issues , delays , the ECL ledger to create transit related events . I believe this is just trains not sure buses are involved here ."

**Goal:** The eight BART stations and ten corridors read the sim's own clock, workforce and hot spots for their numbers, feel their own crew, take an off-chance bad week from a chaos car, and put commute events into citizens' lives through the content ledger. Trains only; AC Transit per-line stays held (builder 2026-09-09).

## Design

Seven moves, all inside existing files. No new phase file. Every new number is a World_Config dial in an **engine.290 seed list of its own** in `engine94SheetContract.js` (engine.133 pattern; engine.94's fourteen keys untouched); code reads `ctx.config.<key>` with the default as fallback. Dials: `transitCityRiders` 60000 · `transitCityTraffic` 50 · `transitFreewayMult` 1.2 · `transitResidentWeight` 0.5 · `transitCatchmentShare` 0.3 · `transitRetailWeight` 0.2 · `transitWeekendMult` 0.6 · `transitMajorHolidayMult` 0.4 · `transitHolidayHoodLift` 0.25 · `transitFirstFridayLift` 0.15 · `transitCongestionOnTimeMult` 0.25 · `transitStaffingGovernor` 0 · `transitNoCrewOnTimeDrop` 0.10 · `transitNoCrewRidershipMult` 0.85 · `transitNoDispatchOnTimeDrop` 0.05 · `transitChaosWeight` 0.5.

1. **Write boundary** — a station row writes OTP and a blank TrafficIndex; a corridor row writes TrafficIndex and a blank OTP. Decide by `m.station !== ''`, never by truthiness. Both writers. `beatSlices.test.js` fixtures updated to blanks in the same commit.
2. **Same clocks** — `dayType` = `S.isWeekend ? 'weekend' : 'weekday'`; the existing weekend/holiday branches in `calculateRidershipModifier_`, `calculateRidershipModLocal_` and `calculateCorridorTraffic_` are rewritten to one rule in one place: weekend → ×`transitWeekendMult`; holiday cut only when `S.holidayPriority === 'major'` → ×`transitMajorHolidayMult` (ridership) and the corridor's existing ×0.5 becomes the same dial; a minor/oakland holiday is a Factors tag with no cut. `S.holidayNeighborhood` lifts the primary station(s) of that hood and the surface corridor through it by `transitHolidayHoodLift`. `S.isFirstFriday` lifts the stations whose catchment includes KONO / Uptown / Downtown and Telegraph + Broadway by `transitFirstFridayLift`. The private weekend `rng()` goes. **Catchment table edit (authored, with its reason):** 19th St `corridors` adds `Uptown`, `KONO` — its own `character` says "uptown arts district" and no station serves either hood today (codex §2).
3. **Ridership from the ledgers — dimensionless, two scales never mixed.** Hood weight `w(h) = (α·r(h) + (1−α)·k(h)) × (1 + ρ(h))`, where `r(h) = Adults(h) / ΣAdults` (hood-scale residents), `k(h) = workers(h) / Σworkers` with `workers(h) = S.commuteInbound[h]` (ledger-scale; the inbound tally already carries the diagonal — es Task 3 changelog), `α = transitResidentWeight`, and `ρ(h) = transitRetailWeight × clamp(retailVitality(h)/cityMedian − 1, −0.5, +0.5)` applied as a multiplier `× (1 + ρ)` (a bounded hot-spot lift; missing → 0 — es Task 3 changelog). Child-area names in a station's lists fold through `resolveHoodOrChild_` (`canonNeighborhoodLoader.js:441`) before weighting — Coliseum, Elmhurst → East Oakland; North Oakland → Temescal; Montclair → Piedmont Ave (agy §2) — and each hood counts once per station. **Primary vs catchment:** a station whose `neighborhood === h` is primary for h; a station listing h only in `corridors` is secondary. If h has primaries, `(1 − s)·w(h)` splits among them and `s·w(h)` among secondaries (`s = transitCatchmentShare`); with no primary, all of `w(h)` splits among secondaries. Station share = its load / Σ loads (sums to 1 by construction; no `base: table` mixing); base = `transitCityRiders × share`. The old adults-ratio and inbound multipliers in `calculateStationMetrics_` are deleted (they are the share now). Corridors: base = `transitCityTraffic × mean(S.neighborhoodDynamics[h].traffic over the corridor's hoods) / cityMean(traffic)` (a missing hood counts 1.0), × `transitFreewayMult` when `freeway`. Crowding from the sim: `S.cityDynamicsCapacity.transitCongestion` replaces the `context.events > 2 → −0.03` on-time hardcode as `−transitCongestion × transitCongestionOnTimeMult`. The authored `baseRidership` / `baseTrafficIndex` stay in the table only for the no-demographics-tab fallback (Factors `base: table`); the live path tags `base: ledger`. **Rebase cycle:** the first cycle on the new base carries `rebased` in every Factors string (`previousCycleState.transitBaseVersion !== 2`), **and `buildTransitSlice.js` learns it**: when any row's Factors contains `rebased`, deltas print as "rebased this cycle — not comparable", the lead-mover label is skipped and the total comparison is suppressed; `beatSlices.test.js` covers it (codex §3).
4. **The crew is felt** — Phase 2 counts Active `ctx.ledger` rows at BIZ-00014 by role class from `RoleType`: conductor (`/conductor/i`), operator (`/train operator|train engineer|rail operator/i`), dispatcher (`/dispatch/i`), maintenance (`/track|maintenance|systems tech/i`), stationManager (`/station manager/i`). Publishes `S.transitStaffing = { conductor, operator, dispatcher, maintenance, stationManager, total, short: [] }`. Governor, armed by `transitStaffingGovernor` (default **0** until the Intake mints land and are read back — see Sequencing): no conductor *and* no operator → OTP −`transitNoCrewOnTimeDrop` and ridership ×`transitNoCrewRidershipMult`; no dispatcher → OTP −`transitNoDispatchOnTimeDrop` and the rain/fog penalty doubles; no maintenance → the chaos train's weight doubles (move 5). Independent of the governor, the **named cause**: a ledger row with `EmployerBizId === 'BIZ-00014'`, a matched role class, and `Status` in the care/custody set `{hospitalized, critical, serious-condition, injured, recovering, detained}` (retired and deceased are not crew) → Factors `crew short: <Name> (<role>)` on every station row and `S.transitState.disruptionCause` names it when a disruption fires. **This is a one-cycle lag by construction** (care/custody writes land in Phase 4/5 and persist at Phase 10; Phase 2 reads them next cycle) — the acceptance says so (codex §4).
5. **The chaos train** — one new `VEHICLE_CONFIGS` entry: `bart_train` / "BART train", scopes `['neighborhood','citizen']`, `baseFrequencyWeight` 0.5, `hoodFilter: 'transit-served'` (the canon hoods that have a primary station). Outcomes: `signal_failure` (medium, Friction, subject, `delay` 0.06), `stalled_train` (high, Friction, `delay` 0.10, narrativeSeed), `door_fault_holds_the_line` (low, `delay` 0.03), `platform_overcrowding` (low, `delay` 0.02), `medical_hold_on_board` (medium, Friction, `delay` 0.05 — no death token; the patient is off-camera), `clean_run` (low positive, `delay` 0, small weight). Non-citizen weights sum to 1; every citizen-scope outcome carries a real `lifeHistoryTag`. `pickTargetByScope_` passes the vehicle through: with a `hoodFilter`, `pickNeighborhoodTarget_` draws from the filtered list (still one draw; other vehicles unaffected) and `pickCitizenTarget_` draws from working citizens living in a served hood (still one draw). `runChaosEvent_` writes `S.chaosTransit = { hits: [{ hood, station, outcome, delay, citizen? }], cycle }` — **collision rule:** many hits in a cycle are all kept; Phase 2 applies the **max** delay per station and lists every outcome in Factors; `clean_run` never cancels a delay. Carried on `previousCycleState.chaosTransit` (finalizeCycleState) and consumed only when `previousCycleState.cycle === currentCycle − 1`; the serving station takes `−delay` on OTP and Factors says `BART train: stalled train in Fruitvale (last week)`. The carry replaces transit's `breakdownRoll` — the dice live in one engine. `validateAllChaosConfigs_` is extended: `delay` numeric in [0, 0.2] on every `bart_train` outcome, `hoodFilter` a known key. `transitChaosWeight` scales the vehicle's `baseFrequencyWeight` where the config is loaded for the cycle (`loadChaosCarsConfig_` / `runChaosCarsEngine_`), not in `chaosLoopWeight_`. Carry size is checked on the bench (blob < 9 KB with the carry present).
6. **ECL transit events — same odds, text from the sheet.** The existing gate at `generateCitizensEvents.js:2826-2828` (`disruptionOngoing && working && chanceHit(0.30 affected / 0.12 elsewhere)`) is computed once per citizen in the `condScopes` block (`:2851-2883`) and published as `condScopes.transitdisrupted = true` only when the gate passed; the inline pool at `:2829-2833` is deleted. So the flag means "this citizen drew a commute moment this cycle" — today's exposure (including the 0.12 citywide) is preserved exactly, and the ledger rows under `transitdisrupted` enter the general pool at their own Weight (1.15 to match today). `transitdisrupted` is registered in `CONTENT_LEDGER_DSL_FIELDS` as a flag (loader) — two files, both in one commit. The four inline lines become Event_Content_Ledger rows (`Kind=line`, `PoolKey=occupation.transit`, Tags `source:occupation, transit:disruption`, Conditions `transitdisrupted`), plus authored rows: delays, crowding, the platform wait, and the crew's own week keyed by exact title through the existing `occupation` scope (`Conditions: occupation=BART Train Conductor`, one row per title). A chaos-train week sets `S.transitState.affectedHoods` to the chaos hood, so it draws these rows too. Test: loader accepts the flag; a working citizen whose gate passed is eligible, a retiree is not; the expected number of draws across a fixture of 100 working citizens in an affected hood matches today's 0.30 within the fixture's rng.
7. **Hiring plumbing** — `data/economic_parameters.json` adds `Train Conductor` (68–98k, median 84k), `Train Operator` (76–112k, median 94k), `Transit Maintenance Technician` (64–96k, median 80k) under Transit & Infrastructure (`Transit Dispatcher` exists at :381). `data/employer_mapping.json` keywordRules and `MINT_KEYWORD_RULES` (`processAdvancementIntake.js:1558+`) add `Train Conductor`, `Train Operator`, `Rail Dispatcher`, `Transit Dispatcher`, `Track Maintenance` → `BIZ-00014`, placed **before** `Transit Hub` (first substring wins; `BART`-prefixed titles already land, codex §7). `DEMOGRAPHIC_VOICE_ROLES` adds `BART Train Conductor`, `BART Train Operator`, `BART Rail Dispatcher`. Engine hiring: the settle step's `Transit Operator Trainee` resolves to `Train Operator` when the employer is BIZ-00014 (one employer-aware line in the settle map); the role **floor** is met by the builder's mints, engine hiring only grows headcount — the plan does not claim otherwise. Acceptance 8 uses a fixture with room at BIZ-00014; with no room the picker falls to field/hood/UNTRACKED exactly as today. **No citizen is moved** (builder 2026-10-11).
