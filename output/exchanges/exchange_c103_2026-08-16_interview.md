# Exchange — interview (Cycle 103)

- participants: Anthony (Bay Tribune) -> POP-00527 Mike Paulson
- trigger: edition c102 NAMES INDEX ("Head of Basketball Operations, Oakland Oaks; General Manager, Oakland Athletics")

---

**Anthony:** "How did you balance the dual roles of GM for the A's and running the Oaks — what’s the rhythm of managing two teams in one city?"

**Mike Paulson:** "Honestly, it's all about finding the right overlap – the A's are my priority during the season, but the Oaks keep me grounded in the community year-round. Early mornings at the ballpark, late afternoons down by the estuary checking in with the Oaks staff... keeps me moving, keeps me connected. And the rhythm? It's Oakland time – never too fast, never too slow, just right for getting things done." (glances at watch, shifts weight) "Sorry, gotta run – Desmond's got a hamstring tweak I need to check on."

**Anthony:** "Desmond’s hamstring — is that indicative of something bigger? Are you seeing more injuries across the board with the Oaks?"

**Mike Paulson:** "Desmond’s just a flare-up, nothing major – we’re being cautious with him. But yeah, I’ve noticed more soft tissue stuff across the Oaks lately. Could be the turf at the stadium, could be the grind of back-to-back road trips. We’re looking into it, maybe adjusting warm-up routines or recovery protocols." (pauses, looks out toward the estuary) "It’s a balance though – push too hard, and you lose players; take it too easy, and you lose games."
