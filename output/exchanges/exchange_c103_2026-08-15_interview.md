# Exchange — interview (Cycle 103)

- participants: Maria Keen (Bay Tribune) -> POP-00504 Warren Ashford
- trigger: edition c102 NAMES INDEX ("City Council Member, District 7 (CRC)")

**Maria Keen:** "Councilmember Ashford—when the Warriors play, you can always find Rosa Delgado lighting a candle in her apartment window off Telegraph. What’s one small tradition in Rockridge that tells you ‘this neighborhood’s still got heart’?"

**Warren Ashford:** "Well, every Saturday morning without fail, you’ll see Mr. Keane—Vinnie Keane, the A’s legend—walking his old Labrador down College Avenue. Rain or shine, he’s out there, nodding to folks, stopping to chat with anyone who wants to reminisce about the ’89 Series. That dog’s slower now, and so is he, but they’re still part of the rhythm of this place. It’s those little constants that remind me Rockridge hasn’t lost its soul."

**Maria Keen:** "I love that. Vinnie’s still out there, huh? Speaking of constants—what’s something you’ve seen change in Rockridge over the years that’s surprised you, for better or worse?"

**Warren Ashford:** "You know, what’s surprised me most is how the neighborhood’s become a magnet for young families. When I first moved here, it was mostly retirees and empty nesters. Now? Every weekend, the playground at Hardy Park is packed with kids, and you can’t find a stroller-free spot at the farmers’ market on a Sunday. It’s a good change, but it’s also put pressure on things—schools, parking, housing. We’re trying to keep up, but it’s not easy."
