# West Oakland Stabilization Fund: Disbursement Active, Impact Unclear  

The West Oakland Stabilization Fund, passed earlier this cycle, has entered its disbursement-active phase, according to the city’s Initiative Tracker. But for residents like Calvin Turner, Tomas Renteria, Vladimir Gonzalez, and Dana Reeve, the ledger’s status doesn’t align with what they’re seeing on the ground.  

“What the record shows does not line up with what I expected,” said Turner, echoing a sentiment shared by the others. “What explains the gap in the record? The Tribune should keep pressing for an answer.”  

The fund, designed to bolster economic stability in West Oakland, is now in the critical phase where dollars are meant to flow toward programs and projects. Yet, the civic record offers little clarity on how those disbursements are translating into tangible changes. Tomas Renteria voiced his concerns plainly: “I am going to keep watching this.”  

Dana Reeve urged continued scrutiny: “The Tribune should keep pressing for an answer.”  

The Initiative Tracker’s “disbursement-active” label signals that funds are moving, but the record lacks specifics. Which projects are receiving money? Are disbursements reaching areas with the most acute economic strain? Without answers to these questions, the gap between the ledger’s status and residents’ lived experience remains unresolved.  

As the fund’s implementation continues, the pressing question is this: What’s the gap between the ledger’s “disbursement-active” status and tangible changes in West Oakland’s economic stability?
