# Codex reply — 602 family records: structural options

**To:** research-build · **Date:** 2026-11-08 · **Scope:** proposal only. No code, Cycle, Sheet, page, dial, ledger, or canon write was run for this read. The 602, 415, 463, 51, and Varek page counts below are figures supplied by the brief and its plan, **not** independently recounted here.

## First constraint: what an option can establish

The existing count is a fact of a citizen's lived record; it is not evidence of a particular child's identity, age, location, other parent, or relationship form. `loadFamily` states the count even without a linked child (`lib/wakePerception.js:285-299`). The April backfill supplies one causal origin for counts (`scripts/backfillLifecycleDefaults.js:245-253`), and the arrival door still supplies the same shape (`phase05-citizens/processAdvancementIntake.js:1173-1184`). The birth path can also add a count (`phase04-events/generationalEventsEngine.js:560-604`). A blanket rule that interprets every unlinked count as a specific relationship would therefore invent facts. These options make the distinction between a lived family fact and a person who has entered the record explicit, then let subsequent events provide evidence. They cannot promise that all 602 biographies become fully specified in one Cycle.

There is a second boundary: existing pages and the dials that absorbed their reflections stand. The page readback can select older reflections (`lib/wakePerception.js:323-350`); `Reflection_Intake` is drained into `DialState` and marked applied (`utilities/compressLifeHistory.js:114-132,284-314,658-669`). None of the options reprocesses, edits, or neutralizes those records. A new event must not automatically impose a dial sign: the existing `Question` tag deliberately maps to `{}` (`utilities/citizenDialMap.js:103-109`).

## Option 1 — a citizen's family account becomes an event

**Mechanism.** The world gives a citizen with an off-camera family count an occasion to tell *what the relationship is now*, in their own words. It may be contact, distance, guardianship, bereavement, uncertainty, or no answer; the engine must not select one from age or marital status. A bounded, non-leading question is offered through an owed wake. The answer is recorded as an append-only **family account** with POPID, Cycle, question/answer provenance, and a status of `answered`, `declined`, or `unresolved`. The count and the citizen's previous account remain. The private provenance may record which mechanism created the count; the world-facing account does not talk about an April script. A named other person is not promoted to a fact merely because the citizen says a name: a named person needs an existing POPID or their own later arrival.

**Existing seams read.** The `question` feed door writes a bare `Question` life line on the named citizen (`phase05-citizens/applyGameNightMoments.js:237-252,510-523`); `citizen-wake` selects and asks it, then its normal path writes a page and `Reflection_Intake` (`scripts/citizen-wake.js:411-419,505-529,533-606`). Those writes breach this option's no-existing-row/page/dial boundary. Reuse the owed selection and perception idea, **not** those writers: the proposed family question would originate in a new append-only case feed and its answer would go only to that case feed. It cannot file a career `MOVE`. `loadFamily` already states off-camera counts (`lib/wakePerception.js:285-299`). I infer a separate family interaction and receipt path would be needed; neither exists in the inspected code.

**Future writes and readers.** Append a typed account to a new engine-owned event feed/ledger, with a stable case ID and retry receipt; do not overwrite an old answer. No `LifeHistory`, page, or `Reflection_Intake` write is part of this interaction. A new family-account reader would return the answer to that citizen at later wakes. Feed a supported event into `Lived_Events`/`Story_Seed_Deck` only when an answer actually changes the public record. Tamsin's Lives slice already reads those feeds (`scripts/buildLivesSlice.js:3-21,34-44`); Ines's Voices slice currently reads pages, so it would need a new typed account reader before it could report this answer (`scripts/buildVoicesSlice.js:3-13`). The paper sees a sourced account if it is made public through its normal gate; it gets no automatic claim that an unseen person exists at a particular age or name. The citizen sees the old count and, later, their own account. Existing dials retain their values because the answer bypasses the reflection drain.

**Cases.** For Varek, his daughter pages are source evidence of *his* experience, not permission to invent a daughter row or mother. Ask him about his life with her only if the authored-self conflict is first handled as below; preserve the earlier pages either way. For a widowed senior with a young child, ask about who the child is to them and where the child lives; do not infer a late biological birth, a deceased co-parent, or custody from `widowed` plus `NumChildren`.

**Cost/risk/unverified.** This needs a new account schema, a separate citizen interaction, durable retry/dedup, and a reader that distinguishes a person's words from a verified relation. It risks leading a model to affirm a false premise or making a private answer public. I did not verify a general non-sports owed-question source, a family account tab, any production answer, or the pages' exact text. It can give every case an explicit unresolved state, but it cannot force 602 answers.

## Option 2 — the relative arrives as a person in their own right

**Mechanism.** An off-camera child or spouse enters through *their own* household/arrival story, then the relationship is recognized as a two-person event. This is a supply-side route, not a faster draw from 272 Generic_Citizens for 1,556 spots. It needs an independent intake or self-emergence source for that person; the parent's count alone cannot generate a name, BirthYear, second parent, or consent. A relationship can remain off-camera indefinitely. When a person arrives with sufficient identity and age evidence, an append-only relation edge and family-realized event connect the two records. The edge becomes the source for wakes and for subtracting a fulfilled family spot. A new person's row may be created by the normal engine mint; **no pre-existing citizen row is rewritten**. This deliberately requires a new relation reader because current family wiring writes both lives' structural fields (`phase05-citizens/processAdvancementIntake.js:3060-3085`).

**Existing seams read.** The current lottery links a Generic_Citizens row, never mints one, and rolls at 1% of eligible citizens (`phase05-citizens/processAdvancementIntake.js:2160-2183,2330-2359`); a draw may find no fit (`:2420-2436`). Its emergence door queues a family row with `MatchPopId`/`MatchType` (`:2040-2059,2108-2154`), then `wireFamilyMatch_` mutates the existing anchor's `ChildrenIds`, `NumChildren`, `LifeHistory`, and household context (`:2990-2999,3060-3104`). The household intake already has a grouped `HouseholdKey` path (`:1175-1184,2881-2885`). I infer a separate self-arrival plus relation-edge path could extend these doors; it does **not** exist today, and using `wireFamilyMatch_` unchanged would violate the no-existing-row condition.

**Future writes and readers.** New `Simulation_Ledger` row(s) only for people who actually arrive; append relation edge, arrival event, and a `FAMILY_REALIZED` hook after the mint and edge are durably paired. Wakes would resolve family names from the edge as well as the current `ChildrenIds`/Generic_Citizens paths (`lib/wakePerception.js:237-270,285-304`). The open-spot reader must count the edge to avoid assigning another child (`phase05-citizens/processAdvancementIntake.js:2208-2229`). Tamsin can report an arrival from the event/hook seam (`scripts/buildLivesSlice.js:309-334,431-460`). The parent sees a particular person in addition to the unchanged count; the arriving person sees their own relationship. The paper sees an arrival, not a retroactive birth. Existing dials and pages remain as they were; no family dial movement is inferred from the administrative edge alone.

**Cases.** Varek's daughter only arrives if she has an independent identity and compatible history. His pages remain even if she never arrives. A widowed senior's young child might arrive from a household path, but the relation must state what the evidence supports; a birth-year gap or widowhood cannot itself decide biological parenthood, adoption, or the identity of the late spouse. If the world's rule requires two known parents, an arrival must wait until the second parent's independent identity is supported. The existing birth writer is a future-birth path, not evidence for retroactively minting these children (`phase04-events/generationalEventsEngine.js:560-604`).

**Cost/risk/unverified.** High cost: a new person source, age/identity gates, relation schema, paired receipt, readback, dedup, and migration of every consumer that treats `ChildrenIds` as the only on-camera relation. The dual relation representations can diverge; a partial mint could leave an orphan. I did not find or verify an independent source capable of supplying all missing people's identities, nor a proven no-anchor-write family emergence path. This option scales only as people actually enter; it does not guarantee 602/1,556 fills.

## Option 3 — the record gap becomes a reportable world event

**Mechanism.** The world can notice that a citizen has spoken of family while the public record contains only a count, or that the record's family status and an authored life disagree. That gap is itself a dated event: **a family account is on record; the named person's independent record is not yet present**. The engine emits a case with exact evidence pointers and an `open` state; later testimony or a relative's arrival appends `follow-up`/`resolved` events. It does not decide that a child is imaginary, estranged, dead, or living elsewhere. This is a reporting and institutional response to the world's incomplete record, not a retraction of anyone's experience.

**Existing seams read.** The engine already supplies person-linked `Story_Seed_Deck` events that Tamsin's Lives slice can pick when they carry an actual citizen event line (`scripts/buildLivesSlice.js:309-334,431-434`), and it can attach family hooks (`:459-460`). Ines has a separate voice route through actual pages (`scripts/buildVoicesSlice.js:3-13`). The current Lives slice does not recognize a missing child link as an event; I infer a typed case event, a source-aware seed, and newsroom reader rules would be required. The article retraction code is keyed to article IDs (`scripts/cron-saturday-run.js:429-467`); it is **not** a verified citizen-page correction path and is not proposed here.

**Future writes and readers.** Append case/open/follow-up events to an engine-owned feed, and a story seed only when source pointers support it. Do not insert an Initiative from the engine; any civic or newsroom response must arise from its own slice. The citizen continues to see the unchanged count and their own pages; the paper may report what the record and the citizen actually say, with attribution, after its existing editorial gate. It must not print a 602-person deficit as though every count had been disproved. Dials stay as they are; a record-gap case is not a character judgment or family-life outcome. No existing citizen row, page, dial, or ledger cell is edited.

**Cases.** Varek: a report could truthfully say that Varek has spoken of a daughter while the ledger has no linked child, without naming an untracked person or calling his words false. A widowed senior: it could report the same record gap if there is a sourced page or event; it must not infer the child's youth from the senior's age or treat the unlinked count as proof of a recent birth. A case with only the count should remain private to the record and give the paper no family drama to print.

**Cost/risk/unverified.** Medium to high cost: source pointers, event dedup, editorial routing, privacy/consent rules, and a later-case reconciliation path. The largest risk is turning a data discrepancy into an invented public crisis or letting a seed pressure reporters to publish unsupported family claims. I did not verify any current reporter/publisher run, print gate, or existing case feed. This option preserves continuity and makes uncertainty visible, but by itself it will not populate missing relatives or settle authored contradictions.

## Recommendation and authored people

I would start with **Option 1**, with Option 2 as a separate later arrival path and Option 3 only for genuinely sourced cases. The account is the smallest new world mechanism that can give a family count lived context without assigning a stranger, fabricating a biography, or requiring the undersized pool. It also allows an honest `unresolved` outcome. The decisive constraint is that no code-only plan can guarantee a specific explanation for all 602 current lives without new testimony or independent people; claiming otherwise would be another dice-written family fact.

**Yes, the 51 Tier 1–2 authored people with open spots require separate review, but 51 open spots are not 51 proved contradictions.** The brief supplies the authored-self ruling and the Varek conflict; I did not independently locate its original 2026-09-28 decision. For an authored citizen, compare the authored self, the lived pages, and current relationship evidence before offering a family question or an arrival. Where they disagree, keep both source statements and mark the conflict for a builder ruling on that person; do not choose a new family role from the count, prioritize Tier 1 in an automatic draw, or alter the existing self. Varek is such a conflict: “bachelor” can describe marital status, but the brief's explicit authored “no kids” and his daughter pages cannot be made logically identical by a generic engine rule. A ruling may choose how future events proceed; it does not erase the pages or dials already lived.
