# Rockridge, in their own words

## Elijah Brown — Electrician

> I didn't really have a strategy on my two nights on UNDOCKED. I just flew and tried to make it through as many systems as I could until the time ran out. I ended up even on credits both nights, so that was good.

## Leanne Wu — Director of Sustainability

> I’d walk down to the little bench near the Rockridge BART plaza, the one tucked just past the bike racks where the sound of trains softens into the hum of College Avenue. There’s a linden tree there that’s finally turning, and I like the way the afternoon light cuts through the leaves onto the pavement. I don’t think about the budget reports or the grant deadlines; I just watch people come and go, the rhythm of the street doing what the noise in my head can’t do on its own. For a few minutes, I’m not the director or the one with the answers — just someone sitting on a bench, letting the season change around me. That’s enough.
