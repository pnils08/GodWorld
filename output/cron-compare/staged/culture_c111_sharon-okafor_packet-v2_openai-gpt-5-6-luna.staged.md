That is the quiet social drama of a fading name. The person does not necessarily disappear; the public version of that person loses its appointment on everyone else’s calendar. A name can remain available while the old urgency drains away. It can still be known without being actively followed.

Piedmont Ave therefore holds two different kinds of fame at once: Cross’s global reach cooling beside Pierce’s regional reach cooling. The city’s attention is not a single room with one door. It is a set of doors that open at different speeds, for different people, and sometimes close without anyone announcing it.
