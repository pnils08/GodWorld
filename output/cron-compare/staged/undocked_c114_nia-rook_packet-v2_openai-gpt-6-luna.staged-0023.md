# UNDOCKED: Luis Taz takes the lead on an even run

Luis Taz ends Wednesday night at the top of the UNDOCKED board: rank one, 37.96 achievement points, and a current streak of one cycle led. The twist is in the finish: zero credits gained or lost. It’s a lead built on a night of moving, mining and staying even—not on a windfall.

In a Fruitvale watch room, that kind of episode gives the hallway something to argue over. Was the real achievement the long route, the haul, or simply finishing without the balance tipping? I like the shape of the run: Taz crossed Nova Terra and Sirius, continued toward Lacaille 9352, then threaded through Grumium, Celaeno, Lanternwick, Diadem and more, flying until the show’s time ran out. The full route also included Intercrus, Gsc 0044, Atlas, Cedarhold, Achernar and The Experiment.

The haul was specific: nine copper ore, two antimatter containment cells and four iron ore. Taz put the strategy plainly: “My goal was to come out even, and I managed to do that, so it was a good run.” That’s a tidy line for a night that kept changing systems. But “even” describes the credits, not the whole performance; the board says Taz leads, and the episode record doesn’t assign achievement points to individual actions.

That missing breakdown is the fun question, not a license to pretend we know. The travel makes the episode feel like a test of persistence; the mined materials give it tangible stakes; the balanced credits make the finish feel controlled. Which one mattered most to the score? The available record doesn’t say.

The earlier approved show history available for Taz turns up no prior episode. That doesn’t prove this was Taz’s first time in the chair; it just means this run arrives without an earlier recorded Taz arc in that history. So for now, the board supplies the headline and the episode supplies the texture: a pilot listed as Janitor, a wide sweep of systems, a mixed haul, and no credit gain or loss. One cycle led is a beginning, not a dynasty.

The next question is whether Taz can make this lead feel earned again—or whether the next chair turns an even run into a different kind of story.
