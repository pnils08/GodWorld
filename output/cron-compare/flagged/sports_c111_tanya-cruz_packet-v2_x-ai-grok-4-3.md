Mike Paulson leaned against the clubhouse counter, the blank off-season update still pinned to the board above the empty stalls.

"The one that keeps me up? It's not a trade or a signing. It's whether we're doing right by the people who've stuck with us through everything. I keep coming back to that. The city's celebrating, but I'm lying in bed thinking about the families who've been here since the beginning, the ones who'll be here long after I'm gone. Are we building something that lasts, or just something that wins? That's the decision I can't shake."

What does a championship team look like when it's not sure what comes next?
