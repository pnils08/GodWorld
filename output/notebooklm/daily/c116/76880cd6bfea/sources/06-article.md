# UNDOCKED: Marguerite Kimball-Ostrow lets the route lead

By Nia Rook, The Bay Tribune

Marguerite Kimball-Ostrow’s Wednesday night on UNDOCKED had a clear shape: a long route, no credit swing, and no sign that she was flying to make the board her story. Luis Taz remains #1, with 45.19 achievement points and a current streak of three cycles led. So the night’s question isn’t whether Marguerite took the lead. It’s what a run looks like when the pilot follows curiosity instead of a ledger.

She went through Antares, Gsc 0041, Shaula, Alnair, Wolf 1061, Gsc 0031, Gsc 0021, Gliese 876, Felis, Biham, Gsc 0035, Jasmine, Mirach, Azha, Groombridge 1618, Silverbend, Alnilam and Jishui. The credits came out even. She flew until the show’s time ran out.

That’s a lot of ground for a night that doesn’t end with a windfall or a setback. I like the little tension in it: the route keeps unfolding, but the balance refuses a dramatic final tilt. At a Piedmont Ave watch-room table, you can imagine the argument splitting two ways. Is the satisfying part the distance, or the refusal to turn every stop into a transaction? No need to pretend the room has a consensus. UNDOCKED is better when the chair leaves space for both readings.

Marguerite put it plainly: “I didn't really have a strategy on Wednesday night. I just flew through the systems one by one, and it happened to come out even on credits. I wasn't really focusing on that; I was just trying to explore and see where the show would take me.”

That admission gives the route its center. This wasn’t a carefully announced plan paying off; it was a pilot letting the next system arrive, then the next, until time called it. The even finish is part of the story, but not the whole story. Marguerite says she wasn’t focused on credits. The show’s own pleasure here is watching what occupies the chair when the scoreboard isn’t steering the person in it.

The paper’s C115 coverage had Taz at the top with 45.19 points, two cycles led and a streak of two. This cycle’s standings keep him at #1 and extend that run to three cycles. That’s the board’s continuity, not evidence that Marguerite is closing the gap. Her episode offers a different kind of hook: an exploratory night that ends where it began on credits, with the route doing the talking.

I’m always going to watch the chair. This time, the memorable thing is how little Marguerite asks it to prove. She keeps moving, system by system, and lets the show’s time—not a declared strategy—close the night.
