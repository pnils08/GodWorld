# A Place of His Own on Fruitvale, and the Water Heater Came First

Manjit Singh's grandmother's tea set was the first thing out of its box in the new place. Singh, a faith leader in Fruitvale, has moved into a rental of his own in the neighborhood. He said the tea set came before the kitchen things and before the bed. "The first thing I did in my new place was unpack my grandmother's old tea set; my friends from the community helped me move," he said.

The Household Ledger records the move this cycle as a renter moving into a place of his own, in Fruitvale. Tribune readers may know Singh from the Culture pages. In C99, Maria Keen found him at the edge of a prep station in the langar kitchen of the Gurdwara Singh Sabha on Fruitvale Avenue. The city's faith records list him as the gurdwara's leader. This week he was carrying boxes with friends from that community.

## The weekend that didn't happen

Singh's own account of the first days in a rented place is short. He had a weekend trip in view. He also had a water heater that needed fixing. He picked the water heater.

"I decided to prioritize getting the water heater fixed over a weekend trip because I wanted to settle in comfortably," he said.

The city's life record for the cycle has the same moment: he weighed the trip against the heater and picked the heater. Anyone who has moved into a first place of their own knows the choice. A trip can be taken any weekend. A cold shower in a new home is something you have to live with.

## The noise, and the neighborhood

The record has two more details from the same stretch. The first is about sound. Singh had braced for the pile drivers, the heavy construction noise that has become part of the backdrop in parts of the city. He then noticed he had already started tuning it out. The record doesn't say where the noise was coming from, and I haven't tried to place it. It does say that by the time he noticed, he had stopped hearing it.

The second detail is that he caught himself bragging a little about the neighborhood lately. In C11 he had been recorded feeling "quietly lucky to call Fruitvale home." Now he lives on a Fruitvale street under his own lease, and the bragging has started.

## Elsewhere on the move

Fruitvale was not the only neighborhood with a household move this cycle. Kris Bubic, the retired former A's player behind Bubic Burgers, has gone from West Oakland to Lake Merritt. "I moved to Lake Merritt because I wanted a change of scenery and a bit more space," Bubic said. "I'm taking the memories of my time in West Oakland with me, especially the community and the A's games."

For Singh, the tea set is unpacked and the water heater is the other priority he named. The ledger doesn't record what he put in the first cup.
