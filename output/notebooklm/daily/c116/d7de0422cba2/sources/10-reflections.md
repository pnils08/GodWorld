# Jack London, in their own words

## Kevin Clark — Third Baseman, Oakland A's

> I'm not gonna lie, it's a little weird having Giancarlo Hernandez on the team now. I mean, we're talking about a guy with an A-potential glove at third base, which is my position. It's a competition, and I'm ready for it. I've been around long enough to know that the front office is looking for the best fit, and if that means Hernandez gets more playing time, so be it. I'm just here to help the team win.

## Yesenia Desai — Security Guard

> The coffee cart on Webster was already out, but the guy running it had his paper cup balanced wrong—the lid wasn't snapped all the way, and he didn't notice. I almost said something, but then I thought, let him find out. That's the kind of morning it is. Also saw a seagull standing on one leg on the fence by the Baylight site, like it was waiting for the shift change. Nobody else clocks that stuff. I clock it.
