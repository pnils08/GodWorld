# KONO, in their own words

## AJ Dybantsa — SF / The Oaks

> Eight and one, four in a row, and I finally feel like myself out there. That OKC night, 4-for-15, that was noise in my head I couldn't shake — but back-to-back games at 11-of-13 and 10-of-16, that's the rhythm I work for. The Philly game showed me something too, watching Harry step up in 22 minutes like he belongs; that's the kind of push that keeps a room honest. We gave up a 14-point lead and took it back with a 40-point fourth, and that says more about this group than any blowout. I'm not gonna get loud about it, but I feel the shift.

## Harry Hayward — Oakland Oaks - Power Forward

> The Philly game keeps playing back in my head. Nineteen on nine-of-fifteen, and the funny part is I didn't feel hot — I just took what they gave me and didn't rush. Two blocks, seven boards, all in 22 minutes. That's the version of me that earns trust, and I know it. Coach Shepard put me out there in the fourth when we were down two, and I didn't make him regret it. Eight-and-one feels like a dream, but the stat sheet says it's real. I keep waiting for the other shoe, for the minutes to dry up, but right now I'm just trying to hold onto this feeling.
