# Temescal Health Center Construction Sparks Mixed Reactions

By **Dr. Lila Mezran**

The Temescal Community Health Center, now under construction, has become a topic of neighborhood conversation. Approved earlier this cycle, the project promises to bring much-needed health services to the area, though its ongoing build phase has drawn a spectrum of reactions from residents.

Melton Neilon, a sourdough baker in Temescal, acknowledged the noise and disruption but expressed optimism about the long-term benefits. "I’ve heard folks talking about the construction timeline, and while it’s a bit noisy, I think it’s worth it for the health center we’ll get in the end," Neilon said.

Kevin Nelson, a line cook in the neighborhood, echoed a mix of anticipation and concern. "I’ve been hearing about the health center construction, and honestly, it’s a mix of excitement and concern—excited for what it’ll bring to the neighborhood, but worried about how accessible things will be during the build," Nelson remarked.

Omar Cleo, another local line cook, focused on the immediate impact. "I’ve heard folks talking about the health center construction, and honestly, I’m just hoping it doesn’t disrupt the flow of the neighborhood too much—people need to get to work, and we’ve got lives to live," Cleo said.

The construction timeline and its implications for access remain unestablished, leaving residents to speculate on how the project will unfold in the coming cycles. What exactly "construction" means for health access in Temescal remains a question for the next record.
