### 3.1 Flagged Draft Analysis — Elliot Marbury (Sports)

#### Reported Content
Elliot Marbury submitted an analytical sports draft titled *"Henry Rivas's 1.6 Walks per Nine Is the Lowest on His Dossier. It Rests on 136.1 Innings."* The submission analyzed Oakland A's pitcher Henry Rivas's 2041 strikeout and walk rates across his eight-season dossier (claiming 136.1 IP, 24 BB, 1.6 BB/9, 2.64 ERA in 2041). 

To contextualize Rivas's command, Marbury evaluated a "peer band" of two other Oakland starting pitchers from the 2041 season:
1. **Arturo Ramos:** Reported accurately from the dossier as 212.2 IP, 41 BB, 1.7 BB/9, 4.44 K/BB, 2.41 ERA, and a 23-1 record, demonstrating that Ramos achieved high command over a full workload sample.
2. **Benji Dillon:** Evaluated as a comparison point, but reported with the following specific statistical line:
   * **Innings Pitched:** 150.1 IP
   * **Walks Issued:** 65 BB
   * **Walk Rate:** 3.9 BB/9
   * **Strikeout-to-Walk Ratio:** 2.03 K/BB
   * **Earned Run Average:** 4.43 ERA

#### Gate Desk Summary & Flag Reason
The editorial gate flagged Marbury's draft with a **HIGH SEVERITY** error and pulled it from the publication queue. 

* **Gate Summary Reason:** Direct contradiction of canonical recorded sports state regarding Benji Dillon's 2041 season line.

Cross-examination of Marbury's claims against canonical C116 Sports state data confirms a direct factual failure on Benji Dillon's numbers. While his reported figures for Arturo Ramos were factually correct, his reported metrics for Dillon deviated significantly from the ground truth database.
