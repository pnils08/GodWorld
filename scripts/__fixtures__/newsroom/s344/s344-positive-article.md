# Bus Comes When It Comes

Rafael Pilgrim waits at the Fruitvale Transit Hub Phase II stop the way he always does — same slab, same lean, rain finding the crack in the shelter no matter where he stands. The hub is still in visioning. Nine cycles of it. The sign still says coming soon.

He does not look at the tracker. He looks at the tunnel of weather between the bus and the pole.

"I take the bus every day and they've had those coming soon signs up for years now."

That is the whole scene: a rider, a cracked panel, a sign that has not moved. Nobody named here has said which desk owns the next phase, or when visioning stops being the answer.

At what cycle count does coming soon become something you stop believing?
