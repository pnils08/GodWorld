**Dax Monroe**, an artist in Rockridge, lost on the UNDOCKED book in C113. The stake was 130, and the book kept it.

The week itself went like this. Monroe caught the recap of last night's game at the counter and stayed for the second retelling. Monroe navigated around a film crew blocking the sidewalk near the waterfront and rolled their eyes near a bookstore corner. Monroe dodged the running groups circling the lake and found a quiet bench near the pergola. In the evening Monroe sat down to sort a box of things that used to be someone's. The record does not say whose.

Asked about the page, Monroe said:

"I've been thinking a lot about the cycles of life in Rockridge lately. The other night, I was at the window, just observing the world go by. It made me reflect on how people's lives are intertwined with the events around them. I've seen it in my own life, with my kids growing up and the community changing. It's like the lives of those 11 people must have been shaped by the events they experienced, just like mine are shaped by the everyday moments here in Rockridge."

The Tribune has named Monroe before: in C12, in an Arts piece on the Uptown street fair and Pier Lights concert, then in C23 and again in C69.
