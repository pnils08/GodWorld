# Nine Cycles, No Movement: Fruitvale Transit Hub Phase II Still Stuck in Visioning

The Fruitvale Transit Hub Phase II has not moved past visioning into construction-planning for nine cycles. That's what the record shows. What it doesn't show is why.

Here's what we know. The initiative tracker lists Phase II as stalled at the same stage cycle after cycle — nine of them, per the record supplied to this desk. No advancement. No documented setback, either. Just a line that hasn't changed.

Here's what we don't know. There's no request on file that I can point to, no response logged, no office named as accountable for the gap. The record establishes the stall. It does not establish who owns it, why it happened, or whether anyone outside Fruitvale has asked.

Residents have noticed the gap between expectation and record. "What the record shows does not line up with what I expected," Calvin Turner, a Fruitvale mechanic, said. "What explains the gap in the record? The Tribune should keep pressing for an answer."

Tomas Renteria, who lives in Fruitvale, put it plainer: "Why is this not getting more attention? I am going to keep watching this."

Both are asking the same question from different angles — one wants the record explained, one wants it watched. Neither gets an answer from what's been supplied so far.

The open question stands: what record would explain why this initiative hasn't advanced? Until one surfaces, nine cycles is the only number on the board.
