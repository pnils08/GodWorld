# Piedmont Ave, in their own words

## Benji Dillon — Pitcher, Oakland A's Legend

> Mark's one of us. He grew up swinging a bat in East Oakland, he lives in Jack London with his wife and kids, and he's the one who started Aitken Little Leaguers — free baseball for kids who might not get it otherwise. That's not a press release, that's his life. So when he signs one year in Colorado, I don't read it as him leaving Oakland. I read it as him leaving a door open. The money's the same as what he made here, so it was never about being priced out. It's about a guy who gave this city everything, including a World Series homer, and still wants to play. I know what that feels like. The clubhouse won't be the same without him in it, and the neighborhood won't either. But that's the shape of it — not a goodbye, just a 'see you later' that might be longer than any of us want.
