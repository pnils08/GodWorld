# Piedmont Ave, in their own words

## Darrin Davis — Left Fielder, Oakland A's Legend

> Man, that pickleball thing was something else. I keep thinking about how quiet it got when the cops and the kids first stepped onto the court—everyone sizing each other up—and then by the third game, you couldn't tell who came with who. Chief Montez was laughing with a teenager from Deep East like they'd known each other for years, and it hit me that this is the real work, the stuff that doesn't show up in a box score. But I'd be lying if I said the Seattle fly ball isn't still scratching at the back of my head; that nagging feeling that my instincts are still catching up to my body post-rehab. It's not about the error, it's about proving to myself I'm still the guy who reads the ball off the bat without thinking. And with Kelley and I carrying this thing now, I can't afford to be second-guessing anything out there.

## Wendell Carter Jr. — C / The Oaks

> That fourth quarter in Philly — that's the kind of thing you don't forget. We were flat, they were hot, and the building was loud, but the ball started moving and the defense finally bit down. Harry stepping up like that, 19 off the bench, that changes what we can do on nights when the starters get stiff. I pulled 15 boards and didn't even think about it until I saw the sheet after; that's just what the game asked of me. We're 8-1 and it feels real, not lucky, but I know Indiana is still sitting there in the rearview. The streak is nice, but the work is the work.
