# Engine.214 Step 8a — adversarial PLAN review

**Scope:** commit `39ecafa8`, plan lines 189–196, against current source. Source review only: no code change, test run, Sheet read, bench fire, or deployment. The C112 observation below comes from the existing execution log, not a fresh live read.

**Verdict: HOLD.** The proposed success line is unavailable on the C112 baseline, the unconditional repairs line is an invented closure that contradicts the plan's own test, and a negative spot line can add *positive* neighborhood sentiment. Fix the causal rules and the proof contract before building this cut. The BusinessDynamics → CitizenEvents ordering and checkpoint tail are sound.

## 1. Business state and `win` reachability — HOLD

- `d = bizDriftOne_` is computed inside the current Cycle's Business_Ledger loop before closure exclusion or open-row collection; `streak` uses this Cycle's calculated growth and `win` uses this Cycle's `prosperous` test (`phase05-citizens/applyBusinessDynamics.js:619`, `:643-650`, `:483-485`, `:445-449`). Thus collecting after `d` and after the `closesNow` branch yields current-Cycle values. Collecting from `ps` or the pre-drift row would be a one-Cycle lag (`:578-580`). The state is available before both CitizenEvents entry points (`phase01-config/godWorldEngine2.js:641-657`, `:2358-2374`).
- `win` is **not the business's sales or growth window**. It increments only if its hood's `S.neighborhoodState[raw business hood].retailVitality >= bizSuccessVitalityHigh` **and** the mayor's sheet approval `>= bizSuccessApprovalHigh`; otherwise it resets to zero (`applyBusinessDynamics.js:592`, `:611-613`, `:445-449`). The seeded gates are 9 and 85 (`phase01-config/engine94SheetContract.js:62-63`). C112's existing live execution log says mayor 69 and zero businesses under success pressure (`output/execution_log_c112.txt:144`). For a C113 fire reset from the C112 baseline, the business reader reads the Civic_Office_Ledger sheet, while the preceding approval phase queues its new value as an intent; it does not expose that intent through this reader (`applyBusinessDynamics.js:287-304`; `phase05-citizens/updateCivicApprovalRatings.js:829`, `:906-915`; `phase01-config/godWorldEngine2.js:637-641`). Therefore `win > 0` is unreachable in that paired C113 fire if the baseline sheet still holds 69, regardless of the business's own success. Longer-term live reachability requires a measured approval and hood-vitality window; the unit case with approval 95 proves only a synthetic path (`phase05-citizens/applyBusinessDynamics.test.js:64-68`). Do not call zero success lines a bench pass.
- Business spot grouping folds the hood, but the drift's vitality lookup uses `ns[hood]` on the **raw** Business_Ledger cell (`applyBusinessDynamics.js:562-565`, `:577-578`, `:592`). A child-area business can enter the parent's spot list while receiving no parent vitality, so its `win` stays zero. Either fold the drift input as a separately reviewed behavior change or document/test this asymmetry; do not claim the spot's row reports parent-hood prosperity.

## 2. Rendezvous selection and RNG — SHIP-WITH-FIXES

- A hash score of a canonical `POPID|BIZ_ID` pair with a fixed tie rule is deterministic and consumes no `ctx.rng`; the *line* is still selected by the existing `roll()` weighted picker (`phase05-citizens/generateCitizensEvents.js:1187-1196`, `:3414-3418`). This fits the existing separation between identity-stable selection and Cycle dice; no proposed `Math.random` call is needed (`utilities/safeRand.js:15-35`; `.githooks/pre-commit:63-128`). The pre-commit hook shown has no hash/`ctx.rng` rule; a hash does not itself trip it. If reusing `seedUnit_`, note it exposes only 10,000 scores, so equal scores need an explicit BIZ_ID tie break (`phase05-citizens/generationalWealthEngine.js:881`).
- The stability claim needs correction. With an unchanged candidate set, a citizen keeps the winner; removing a **losing** spot changes nothing, removing the **winning** spot must change that citizen, and adding a new spot can win and move a subset. The plan's “after another spot is removed” test does not specify winner versus loser, and “a closure or a new opening only moves the citizens whose spot it was” cannot describe a new opening with no prior assignees (`docs/plans/2026-10-10-engine-214-mood-per-hood.md:192`, `:194`). Test both removals and addition, fixed ordering/ties, and replay from identical identities. Hash choice is a stable association, not evidence that the citizen has visited or favors that business.

## 3. Pool competition and outabout sign — HOLD

- The spot pool is one more set of entries in the same weighted array, after a participation gate and beside QoL, weather, previous-evening, neighborhood state, local chaos, hood texture, and Event_Content_Ledger entries (`generateCitizensEvents.js:2674-2715`, `:2717-2761`, `:3180-3202`). The generator then applies eligibility/filtering, trait/dial/continuity modifiers, recent-text suppression, and 1–4 draws; it balances ECL PoolKeys without reducing their total mass against hardcoded lines (`:3230-3362`, `:3374-3418`, `:50-85`). A low-weight repairs entry or two declining entries can be offered yet almost never drawn; large streak-scaled mass can conversely dominate ordinary life. The plan specifies no target or bound for either outcome (`docs/plans/2026-10-10-engine-214-mood-per-hood.md:193-195`). Require per-hood eligible citizens, candidate winners by state, offered spot mass / total mass, picked spot lines, and participation/other-pool shares across paired and multi-Cycle samples. A single zero-hit C113 is unresolved, not proof that the branch works.
- Cutting the decay pair removes two `state:trajectory-down` candidates with combined weight `2.1 × (1 + 4·mi)`, up to 10.5, for every participating citizen in a decay hood (`generateCitizensEvents.js:1696-1718`). The planned C112 shape has 7 decay hoods but only 11/180 shrinking businesses, 6 in Ivy Hill (`docs/plans/2026-10-10-engine-214-mood-per-hood.md:188`). A citizen assigned an open, steady spot receives no decline candidate. Thus the outabout-down mass can collapse in decay hoods; keeping the growth pair leaves trajectory-up mass untouched (`generateCitizensEvents.js:1709-1718`, `:1055-1057`). Retail-low, weather, housing and other tags may still supply down events, so an exact net count cannot be inferred from source alone. Compare A/B **per hood** graded Outabout-Up-S and Outabout-Down-S lines by source/fact, plus offered mass; a citywide up/down total can hide the loss. The builder's growth-pair call in plan line 190 remains open.
- “Any spot → closed for repairs this week” has no supporting Business_Ledger state and can recur weekly; the plan simultaneously demands “all-steady hood → no down spot line” (`docs/plans/2026-10-10-engine-214-mood-per-hood.md:193-194`). Negative growth/streak also does not establish a cut in hours or half a menu (`applyBusinessDynamics.js:478-484`, `:695-699`). `win` does not establish a line out the door (finding 1). These are causal claims that need a real row/event field or narrower wording. Remove the unconditional repairs line until there is a repairs cause and resolution; test a steady hood with no invented decline.
- Reusing `source:nbhdState` + `state:retail-low` grades the citizen's outabout **down**, but `primaryFromTags` routes that entry to `Neighborhood` (only `state:retail` routes to `Lifestyle`), and `recordPulse_` uses the ungraded `lineKind` on the first draw (`generateCitizensEvents.js:1013-1017`, `:1055-1057`, `:3563-3575`, `:3681-3688`). `PULSE_MAP.Neighborhood` adds **+1 sentiment**, and the Phase-8 writer folds that pulse into the hood (`utilities/neighborhoodPulseMap.js:28-31`, `:119-133`; `phase08-v3-chicago/v3NeighborhoodWriter.js:524-530`). This is a sign contradiction in the proposed down line. If wording contains “shop closed,” the pulse text rule also subtracts vitality despite a repair line having no closure fact (`neighborhoodPulseMap.js:75-80`, `:105-110`). Resolve and test the pulse semantics as part of the cut.

## 5. Hood fold — SHIP-WITH-FIXES

The citizen's raw Neighborhood cell is passed into `neighborhoodStatePool_`, which indexes `S.neighborhoodState[neighborhood]` directly (`generateCitizensEvents.js:2465-2466`, `:1683-1686`, `:2711-2715`). `resolveHoodOrChild_` maps a recognized child to the canonical parent and returns null for unmapped/City-wide (`phase01-config/canonNeighborhoodLoader.js:435-452`); the business momentum helper uses that fold (`applyBusinessDynamics.js:557-565`). A child-area citizen can therefore get a parent spot while the adjacent state pool sees no row. Passing the folded hood only to the new pool fixes the spot match, but the emitted log and `recordPulse_` still use the raw hood (`generateCitizensEvents.js:3664-3676`, `:3688`; `neighborhoodPulseMap.js:119-131`), while Phase 8 reads pulse by canonical parent (`v3NeighborhoodWriter.js:524-530`). Decide the attribution key deliberately and test a child-area emitted line through log and pulse, not merely the pool lookup. Unknown raw cells should remain unavailable, never inherit a random parent.
