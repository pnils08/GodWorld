# Uptown, in their own words

## Samantha Viteri — INTERNAL AFFAIRS DIVISION LEAD

> Look, I can't walk you through specific case files — that's not how this works. But when a classification gets challenged, it lands on my desk because somebody on the street or in the ranks felt the paperwork didn't match what actually happened. That's the part people forget: we don't start with the file, we start with the doubt.
