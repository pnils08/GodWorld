Instead, they summoned 21-year-old Cuban right-hander **Carmen Mesa (POP-00081)** from the Double-A Midland RockHounds [1-4].
* **The Dominant Fill-In:** In his major-league debut against the Chicago Cubs, Mesa tossed a complete-game shutout with twelve strikeouts [5, 6]. Across his first two starts (15.1 innings), he struck out twenty-two batters while allowing just one earned run [2, 5].
* **The Option Down (Cycle 98):** When Rivas was medically cleared in Cycle 98 and tossed seven shutout innings against the Kansas City Royals, **Mesa was optioned back down to the minor leagues (Midland/Triple-A)** [190, 398–399]. As *The Bay Tribune* noted, optioning Mesa was a reward for holding the rotation slot steady, keeping him first in line for roster expansion rather than relinquishing his contract rights [7].
