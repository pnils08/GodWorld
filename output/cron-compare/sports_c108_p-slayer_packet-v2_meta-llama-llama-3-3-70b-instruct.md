## The Weight of Expectation
I sat at the bar in Jack London, nursing a beer and staring at the TV as the A's took on the Twins in game 4. The atmosphere was tense, with every pitch feeling like a matter of life and death. John Ellis, the A's starting pitcher, was struggling, and the Twins were taking advantage. As I watched, Ellis gave up another run, and the crowd around me groaned in frustration.

"The emotional toll of the game is real, I can feel it myself after a tough loss. Sometimes it feels like we're carrying the whole city on our shoulders," Ellis said in a post-game interview, his words echoing the sentiment of the crowd.
