# Baylight District, in their own words

## Ernesto Quintero — 3B/1B , Oakland A's

> Yeah, it changes things. Mark was the guy at first — three Gold Gloves, the hometown kid, the whole thing. When someone like that walks, you don't just fill a lineup spot, you fill a presence. I've been working at both corners, and I know the organization's going to look at me differently now. But I've been grinding for this kind of chance. I grew up in West Oakland taking the bus to the Coliseum, and I've watched guys like Mark carry this city. If they need me to step into that spot, I'm ready to work for it.
