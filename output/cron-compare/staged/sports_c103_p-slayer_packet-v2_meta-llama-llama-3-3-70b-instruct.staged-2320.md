## A's Fans Left Scratching Heads After Pablo Almanzar's No-Hitter

I was sitting at the bar in Downtown, nursing a beer and trying to process what I had just witnessed. Andre Turner, a die-hard A's fan, turned to me and said, "I'm not sure what to make of this Pablo Almanzar guy throwing a no-hitter, it feels like something's off, like it's too good to be true. I think the Tribune should ask if he's really as good as everyone's making him out to be." His words echoed my own thoughts - was this a fluke or the real deal?

As I looked around the bar, I saw a mix of excitement and skepticism. Some fans were high-fiving and cheering, while others were scratching their heads, wondering if this was just a one-time thing. The handoff feeling was palpable - would the A's be able to keep the momentum going, or would they fall back into their old ways?

I thought back to the game, where Pablo Almanzar had thrown a no-hitter, with 9IP, 0H, 1BB, and 10Ks. Vinnie Keane had also had a great game, with 2-3, a home run, and 3 RBIs. The team's record now stood at 124-34, with a 15-game winning streak. But despite these impressive stats, I couldn't shake off the feeling that something was off.

Some fans might say that we're just being pessimistic, that we should be celebrating this win and not questioning it. But I say, we have every right to be skeptical. We've been burned before by players who have promised the world and delivered nothing. So, yes, I'll believe it when I see it. I'll believe that Pablo Almanzar is the real deal when he proves it to me, not just once, but consistently.

As I finished my beer and left the bar, I couldn't help but feel a sense of dare - dare the A's to keep this momentum going, dare Pablo Almanzar to prove himself again, and dare the fans to keep believing. The question on everyone's mind was, would the A's be able to maintain their winning streak with Pablo Almanzar on the mound? Only time would tell.
