West Oakland master electrician **Kevin Kim** lost two subcontracts to Livermore firms despite meeting all bonding and certification requirements [15]. A spot-count by columnist Jax Caldera at the *Anchor & Rail* construction bar revealed that **only 4 out of 30 workers (13%) wearing Baylight badges were actual Oakland residents** [5, 6, 18].

This contains billionaire **Elias Varek's** "triple overlap" as Oakland Oaks owner, Civis Systems founder (\$60M annual revenue), waterfront equity holder, and naming-rights holder for the Athletics' new stadium (*Civis Systems Field — The Town*) [19-22].
