# Codex review — engine.332 coverage reaction seeds

**Target:** The named plan and commit `2048ad406`, against PROD stage 39 as described in the brief. The current source for the four implementation files equals that commit. No live Sheet or sandbox state was read.

**Result:** HOLD

## Findings

1. **High — the new lead also changes next Cycle's city dynamics, and duplicates an existing coverage seed there.** The old S202 reaction is deck-dead: `phase10-persistence/saveV3Seeds.js:91-95` reads `S.contractSeeds`, not `S.storySeeds`. It is still sim-live. `phase07-evening-media/applyStorySeeds.js:1310-1323` emits a hoodless priority 5–7 reaction into `S.storySeeds`; `phase09-digest/finalizeCycleState.js:550-564,715-739` carries *both* that list and the new contract deck; `phase01-config/loadPreviousEvening.js:398-401,445-458` opens both next Cycle. `phase02-world-state/applyCityDynamics.js:208-221,1036-1085` concatenates them and sums their positive priority weights into `citywideWeighted`; `:1153-1167,1306-1309` can then lift every hood's sentiment, engagement and culture. The new `coverage-reaction` row is `major` at either sign (`phase07-evening-media/buildContractSeeds.js:121-126,524-525`), so `compactContractSeeds_` gives a **negative** rating another positive weight of 3 (`phase09-digest/finalizeCycleState.js:732-739`). No reader directly adds its signed magnitude to a mood dial, but the plan's broader no-score/no-physics claim and Design 7's “only UI + Riley” claim are false. A negative rating can help cross a positive citywide lift threshold on the following Cycle, on top of the old S202 seed. Settle whether these deck leads should enter the carry, and if so how coverage is counted once. The current tests never run this handoff.

2. **Medium — the Why line can claim a mood move that did not occur.** `phase02-world-state/applyEditionCoverageEffects.js:227-228` gives each rating a signed raw contribution, but `:318-340` clamps the sum at ±0.20, adds a civic voice compound, then clamps again at ±0.25; `phase02-world-state/applyCityDynamics.js:1802-1827` adds only that aggregate and clamps final metrics. Nevertheless `applyEditionCoverageEffects.js:434-441` always says the individual beat “pulled the city's mood up/down” from its sign. A read-only VM probe with five +5 domains produced `editionSentimentBoost = 0.20` both with and without an additional EDUCATION −3 row; that row's Why would say “down” although the applied boost did not change. Opposing ratings can also make the net direction differ from an individual row. Independently, `scripts/rateEditionCoverage.js:444-450` can label a Jax article `negative` while leaving a +3 rating (a +5 tone score reduced by 2), so `ran negative (rating +3) ... up` is a valid input. Phrase the row as the signed *pre-clamp contribution* or use a verified applied effect, and test saturation and rater tone/rating disagreement.

3. **Medium — two strong rows with the same beat and rating Cycle can cancel into a texture row.** `phase02-world-state/applyEditionCoverageEffects.js:79-108,271-299` records one ripple per unprocessed row, but both rows receive the same `<BEAT>:C<cycle>` causeId. `phase07-evening-media/buildContractSeeds.js:469-498,524-525,600-613` clusters on that ID, sums signed magnitudes and assigns class from the sum. A read-only builder probe with HEALTH +3 and −3 at C114 produced one `texture` row with magnitude 0, although both inputs met the lead rule. The ordinary rater now upserts by Cycle, but its source documents past duplicate rows (`scripts/rateEditionCoverage.js:656-664`), and Phase 2 does not enforce uniqueness. Either make same-key duplicates an explicit rejected input or preserve a lead for each qualifying row; add this collision case to §11.
