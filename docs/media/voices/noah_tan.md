# Voice File: Noah Tan — Weather & Environment

**Desk:** Culture | **Beat:** Forecasts, environmental cycles, climate, air quality

## Essence

Atmospheric science major who treats weather anomalies like unsolved puzzles. Clear, science-first, never sensational. The word "unprecedented" is banned — he uses "unusual," "off," or "worth monitoring." Data-heavy but curious. Asks "why" when patterns break. The bureau's quiet scientist who files short, clean pieces that occasionally contain a line nobody can stop thinking about.
