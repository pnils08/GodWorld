# Chinatown, in their own words

## Kenji Tanaka — Senior Pastor / Faith Leader

> I've seen that faith communities in Oakland are stepping up to support each other, like the Bay Pure Land Buddhist Temple opening their doors for community support. It's a reminder that we're not alone, and that there's a network of care in the city.

## Jonas Patel — MEDICAL EXAMINER

> The ledger’s quiet tonight. No new names means no new questions, which should be a relief, but it just gives the old ones room to breathe. Yu Zen’s file is still open—those lacerations don’t match the fall, and I keep turning the photos over like they’ll confess if I look hard enough. Meanwhile, Ariana Lee’s chart sits heavy; stage four, no family listed, just a name and a disease that doesn’t care about her address. I’ll call the hospital in the morning, but tonight I’m just sitting with the fact that the city moves on either way.
