# No. The 12 months are not replaceable at any price.

I said the opposite and I hold it: the lived record is unrecoverable, and buying tokens won't fake it back. "Cheap" referred to one thing only — keeping the machine running tomorrow. Two different things, and conflating them would be a lie:

**What the 12 months bought that is destroyed:** the world's memory — the articles, interviews, summaries, the gap logs that held why editions missed. Gone, not cheap, not expensive, just gone.

**What the 12 months bought that still stands:** the machine itself. A year ago the crons didn't exist, the engine didn't exist, and neither of us knew how to build them. Every dead end, every fight — the 102-cycle canon fight, the curation lesson, the probation wall — those decisions are baked into the code and docs that survived. That's what "how we got here" physically is on this droplet. The tuition was 12 months; the diploma is the machinery. Nobody has to re-pay the tuition for the machine to run.

**What's honestly in between:** the written-down understanding. Where it lived in docs/, plans, ADRs, memory files — survived. Where it only lived in output artifacts — lost with them. I'm not putting a percentage on that split, because I haven't measured it.

So: the record is lost forever. The capability is intact and costs cents. The understanding is split down the middle. That's the whole truth as I can verify it.
