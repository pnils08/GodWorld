# Elias Varek and Mike Paulson, talking

**Elias Varek:** Mike. Good to see you out here. I was just thinking about you — the Aitken news and what it means for that infield of yours.

**Mike Paulson:** Elias. Yeah, it's been a long couple of days, I won't lie. Losing Mark stings more than any box score can show, but we've got a decision to make at third and two kids who deserve a real shot.

**Elias Varek:** The kids earned it. Kevin's bat speaks for itself, and Giancarlo's glove is the kind of thing that wins you games in September. You've got a good problem, Mike — most GMs would kill for that kind of depth.

**Mike Paulson:** That's the thing, isn't it? We've been waiting for a problem like this for years. Kevin's got the stick and Giancarlo's got the leather, and somewhere in between we've got to figure out what wins us ballgames.

**Elias Varek:** That's the right question to be asking. You're not picking a starter, you're building a lineup that wins a pennant. And you've got the runway to let it sort itself out in spring training — that's a luxury, trust me.

**Mike Paulson:** Spring training's only a luxury if we use it right. I've seen too many clubs let those six weeks decide a guy's whole future, when the real answers come in July and August. We'll give both of them a fair look, and the one who forces the issue keeps the job.
