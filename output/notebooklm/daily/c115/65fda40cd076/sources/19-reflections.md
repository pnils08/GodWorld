# Temescal, in their own words

## Ross Cabral — Catcher, Las Vegas Aviators (AAA)

> It's the way my phone vibrates now. Every time, I think it's someone from the A's telling me the whole thing was a clerical error, that I'm supposed to be in Mesa, Arizona, not on a flight to Vegas. And then it's just my wife asking if I want eggs. That's the grating part—the waiting for a phone call that isn't coming, the feeling that I'm a guy who got moved for a reason I can't fully pin down. Does it matter? No. I know that. I'm a catcher, I'm getting paid seven figures, and I'm still in the organization's system even if it's a different city. But I'm worn down because it's one more thing I couldn't control, one more reminder that I'm a piece on a board, not a player in a story. Maybe I just need to get to the bakery and let the sourdough smell reset my head for an hour before I pack.
