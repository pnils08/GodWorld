### **3. West Oakland Emergency Stabilization Fund (INIT-001): Cleared Backlogs & The Looming C95 Audit**

#### **Direct Published Facts & Prior Events**
*   **Prior Stagnation:** Authorized at \$28 million in Cycle 78 (passing 6–3), the anti-displacement fund spent over a year in administrative limbo, executing only 18 disbursements out of 280 targeted applications [4, 25-27]. *   **Webb's Acceleration and the Signature Backlog:** Fund Director **Marcus Webb** successfully accelerated processing to a rate of 50-plus cases per week, reviewing 285 of 295 applications and approving 221, deploying \$3.87 million and clearing 108 West Oakland households [27, 30, 32]. *   **The Political Backlash:** Councilmember **Warren Ashford** (D7, CRC) immediately seized on this lag, declaring that the remaining 83 households constitute an **"executive-branch chokepoint that warrants weekly reporting and an external audit cadence"** [29, 31]. 

#### **Analytical Inferences & Unresolved Storylines**
*   While the administrative processing of applications has run smoothly under Webb, the political consolidation of the economic development portfolio has created a persistent executive signature bottleneck [28].
