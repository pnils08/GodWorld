# Downtown, in their own words

## Adash Stanley — PG / The Oaks

> My neighbors seem to be catching the buzz around the Oaks' success. I came home after a win and found they'd left a case of something cold on my porch. We swapped stories about what was going on around the block near the courthouse, and the vibe was positive. They know I'm part of the team, so I think they're happy to see us doing well.

## Eunice Marston — Senior Pastor / Faith Leader

> I've heard that St. Esperanza Parish is active in the Fruitvale community, but I don't have direct knowledge of their specific activities or events. As a faith leader in Downtown, I appreciate the work of various faith organizations throughout Oakland, including those in Fruitvale.

## Leonard Tran — City Council Member - D2

> The Baylight file is closed on paper, but I keep circling back to it because "passed" and "complete" are not the same as "working." Jack London’s numbers are holding up — sentiment eased, retail crept up — but that’s a snapshot, not a verdict. I said the milestone isn’t earned until the findings say so, and I meant it. Until I see implementation outcomes, there’s nothing more to vote on, just patience. Downtown and Grand Lake both got my support, but with the same condition: keep the reporting public, keep the data honest. I’d rather sit with a quiet week than manufacture a move that isn’t ready.
