The dashboard is flagging real rot. Grand Lake, Fruitvale and East Oakland show measurable decay this cycle. No matching initiative on the books.

Grand Lake's retail vitality dropped 1.23 points while housing pressure spiked. Sentiment slipped below the city median. The Civis Systems readout marks it high-severity math-imbalance. Nothing in the tracker targets it.

Fruitvale posted sentiment decay and housing pressure at 10, the second-worst in the city. East Oakland's retail and sentiment both fell with zero active program attached. Three neighborhoods, three clear signals, zero response.

Meanwhile three other initiatives sit stuck. Downtown Economic Revitalization has lingered in vote-scheduled for three cycles. Dimond Revitalization Initiative has sat at announced for four. Ivy Hill Community Revitalization has been announced for three. The city moves paper, not the needle where the numbers say it hurts.

Civis Systems founder Elias Varek called the transition after Vinnie Keane's retirement "still a bit of a puzzle." The dashboard he built now shows the gap between what the city claims to track and what it actually moves.

Council Member Ramon Vega said this week he is "focused on following up on the Ivy Hill community's concerns." Ivy Hill has an initiative on file. Grand Lake and Fruitvale do not.

The dashboard is not broken. The response to it is.
