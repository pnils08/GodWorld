# Downtown, in their own words

## Tao Lee — Community Organizer

> Quiet night. I keep thinking about Mesa, how he threw that shutout and still couldn't get a real shot here. That's the part that sticks. He lived in Lake Merritt, he was one of us, and the front office just moved him along like he was inventory. I get the business of it, but the kid deserved better than a $780K seat on the bench while the rotation stayed as is. Hernandez though — his glove is the real deal, and I've seen enough baseball to know defense wins in October. If he can hit even a little, that's a solid pickup. But I wonder if this means Aitken is gone, and that's a bigger loss than anyone's saying out loud. Mostly I just hope the young guys coming up feel what this city gives them. We show up, we care, we remember their names. That's not nothing.
