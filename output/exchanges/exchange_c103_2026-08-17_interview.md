# Exchange — interview (Cycle 103)

- participants: Maria Keen (Bay Tribune) -> POP-00034 Avery Santana
- trigger: edition c102 NAMES INDEX ("Mayor of Oakland")

---

**Maria Keen:** **Maria:** Mayor Santana, you grew up in Rockridge. When you’re walking these streets now, what’s the one corner that still makes you pause—like, *really* stop—and remember who you were before all this?

**Avery Santana:** *Laughs, adjusting sunglasses* Oh, easy—the corner of College and Claremont, right by that old oak tree. Used to sit there after school waiting for the 51 bus, scribbling city budget ideas in a notebook like some kid playing mayor. *Pauses, taps the tree trunk* Still stands there, still watching. Funny how some things stay solid while everything else moves.

**Maria Keen:** **Maria:** That notebook—was it just dreams, or did you already have a list of what you’d fix first if you ever got the keys to City Hall?

**Avery Santana:** *Grins, shoving hands in pockets* Oh, it was a mess of crayon maps and angry notes about potholes on Broadway. *Mimics scribbling* “Fix this NOW—Avery, Future Mayor.” *Laughs* Kid me would lose it seeing Baylight’s cranes from that same spot today.
