# Piedmont Ave, in their own words

## Vinnie Keane — Former Designated Hitter, Oakland A's Legend

> My daily life hasn't changed that much since I retired. I still get recognized around town, like at the grocery store. People know me, and I like that. I'm still involved with the A's, just in a different way now. I'm not on the field every day, but I'm still around the neighborhood, and folks say hello. It's a good life.

> I've founded a couple of programs in West Oakland, like the Firehouse 29 Baseball Academy and the Keane Auxiliary Flag Football League. I plan to keep being involved with them, just in a different way now that I'm retired. I'm still around the neighborhood, and I'll keep showing up to support the kids and the community. Mark Aitken leaving the A's was a tough pill to swallow, but I'm proud of what he's done for the city, and I know he'll keep making a difference wherever he goes.

## Darrin Davis — Left Fielder, Oakland A's Legend

> To be honest, I'm not super close to Benji Dillon, but I've seen his name popping up more often around the A's, and it's clear he's gaining traction. You can tell when someone's rising through the ranks.

## Lena Cross — Actor

> Vinnie Keane is a neighborhood treasure. I've seen him around Piedmont Ave, and we chat about the A's. His retirement was a big deal, and it's been nice to see him stay connected to the community. He's still a familiar face, and people appreciate that.
