# Grand Lake, in their own words

## Elijah Davis — Barista

> Honestly? I'm hoping the morning rush is smooth and I don't burn the first batch of oat milk. That sounds small, but it sets the whole day right. And I'm dreading... I guess I'm dreading the moment someone brings up Mark Aitken leaving. I get it, it's big news, everyone's got feelings. But I've got a regular, older guy, comes in every day for a black coffee and the paper. He's been an A's fan since the seventies. I just don't want to see that look in his eyes today, you know? The city's been through enough.
