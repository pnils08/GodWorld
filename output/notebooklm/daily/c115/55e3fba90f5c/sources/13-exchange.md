# Clarissa Dane and Vinnie Keane, talking

**Clarissa Dane:** Vinnie, look around Grand Lake. The storefronts that survived the last five years are the ones with landlords who held the line on rent. This stabilization fund keeps those businesses on their feet instead of watching another vacant window gather dust. I want to see the same commitment in West Oakland, and this initiative sets that precedent.

**Vinnie Keane:** Clarissa, I hear you on the storefronts — I've watched three different businesses turn over on Lakeshore since I moved here, and that dust on the windows is a hard thing to see. Stabilization sounds good on paper, but I want to know it's not just patching one block while another neighborhood waits its turn. If this fund actually holds the line for the little guys, I'm with you.

**Clarissa Dane:** Every dollar in this fund goes to rent stabilization, not red tape. I've sat across from small business owners in my own district who've watched their margins get eaten alive by annual hikes they couldn't predict. This is about giving the folks in Grand Lake the same floor we're fighting for in West Oakland — a fair shake, not a handout.

**Vinnie Keane:** Fair shake, not a handout — that lands with me, Clarissa. I've seen what an unpredictable rent hike does to a family-run spot; it's the difference between a place that's been on the corner for twenty years and a For Lease sign. If this fund actually gets dollars into their hands without a pile of paperwork slowing it down, that's a precedent worth backing.
