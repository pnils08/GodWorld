# Piedmont Ave, in their own words

## Vinnie Keane — Former Designated Hitter, Oakland A's Legend

> Mike. Good to hear from you. Doing all right. Retired now, officially, so the mornings are my own. I'm still getting up early out of habit, though. Body doesn't know the season ended. Amara and I are settled in over on Piedmont. Still getting used to the new streets, but the grocery store folks have been kind. A few of them still say hello like I owe them a swing. I've been over to the firehouse on Mandela Parkway a lot. That part doesn't stop. The kids don't care that I retired. They just want somebody to throw to them. What's going on with you? Anything happening at the front office, or you just checking in?
