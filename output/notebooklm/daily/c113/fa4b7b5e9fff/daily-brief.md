### **The Oaks Have a Four-Game Story, and Uptown Is Still Deciding**
*By Selena Grant, Simon Leary, & Talia Finch, Sports Desk*

At an Uptown bar, server **Trenton Nawan** carried glasses past a muted television screen showing the **Oakland Oaks** closing out a 105–62 dismantling of Washington. Following a 91–78 victory in Detroit and a 115–93 win in New York, the expansion franchise has opened its inaugural season undefeated at 4–0:

> *"I'm still a bit worried about the Oaks, but their 4-0 start is really giving me hope,"* Nawan said, setting glasses down on the counter. *"I checked the scores twice a day all week and was happy every time they won. Maybe they're finally getting their act together."*

On the floor, point guard **Adash Stanley** and shooting guard **Austin Reaves** have established a balanced rotation:
*   In Detroit, Stanley carried the offense with 29 points while Reaves dished 10 assists.
*   In New York, Reaves and rookie **AJ Dybantsa** scored 23 apiece, while second-round pick **Harry Hayward** tallied a double-double (10 points, 10 rebounds).
*   In Washington, Stanley and Reaves scored 23 each to anchor a stifling defensive effort.

Speaking on the team's evolving identity, Stanley emphasized steady execution over early accolades:
> *"When the role changes, the record doesn't care who's carrying it. It just sits there at 2-0 and asks the next guy to keep it honest. For me, that means showing up the same way I always have — steady, ready, not making it about me. The continuity is in the work, not the title... The mood among fans is electric. I've felt the excitement building around the Oaks, it's like the whole city is waking up with the team."*

Reaves echoed that early perfection remains a test in progress:
> *"Look, 2-0 feels good, don't get me wrong. But I've been around long enough to know a record doesn't hold the building up. It's the guys inside it. We're still figuring out who we are out there — roles, rotations, all that... The neighborhood's been buzzing about the Oaks' 4-0 start. I've seen fans around here getting really excited, and it's infectious. People are talking about it while they're out and about, like when they're unloading groceries or just hanging out on their stoops."*
