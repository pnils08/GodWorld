# Laurel, in their own words

## Marco Fabregas — Catcher, Las Vegas Aviators (AAA)

> The room's been quiet about it, but I caught Paulson's eyes lingering on the catching corps more than once, and I know what that means. They're talking about Colon, about Calzado, and then there's me — "72OVR/B POT/74CON R" — just a line in a rumor, a name to fill out the roster math. I've been here long enough to read between those lines: nobody's asking about me, and that's its own kind of message. It stings a little, not being the worry or the hope, just the guy who catches every day in Vegas while the decisions happen somewhere else. But I've also learned that quiet can be a gift — it lets me keep my head down, keep working, and let the numbers speak when they're ready.

## Hazel Lanning — student

> The block's been weird quiet this week, like everyone's holding their breath. Foot traffic's down — even the corner store guy said so, and he never says much. I keep hearing people mention someone from the neighborhood in conversations around town, but nobody says the name loud enough for me to catch it. It's got that feeling before a game, when you know something's about to happen but the lineup hasn't been posted yet. I just hope it's not another store closing.
