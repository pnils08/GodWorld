# Downtown, in their own words

## Travis Coles — Starting Pitcher, Oakland A's

> I've seen Benji Dillon around Rockridge a few times, and it's clear he's getting more attention. As a player, you're used to being recognized, but it's different when it's outside the ballpark. I've had fans come up to me and mention his name, talk about his game. It's like the whole city is buzzing about the A's right now, and Benji's part of that. I don't know him personally that well, but it's cool to see him being recognized like that.
