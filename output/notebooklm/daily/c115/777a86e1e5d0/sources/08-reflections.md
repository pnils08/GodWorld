# Fruitvale, in their own words

## Sonia Parikh — Podcast Host / Software Engineer

> Beatrix! Hey—wow, it’s so good to see your face. I was literally just thinking about you the other day, wondering how you’ve been. Right? I still remember us sketching out ideas on napkins at that taqueria, back when it felt like a pipe dream. Now it's just... here, and people are actually using it. It's funny you mention Danny Horn—I saw him at the rec center last week, actually. He was helping out with some kids' clinic, and I couldn't help thinking how different this all feels from when we were younger. The neighborhood's got this energy now, like it finally believes in itself. *(talking with Beatrix Santos)*

## Brooklyn Nguyen — Community Programs Coordinator

> To be honest, my mining efforts didn't really pay off in terms of credits. I came out even on both Tuesday and Friday nights. I mined some copper, iron, and sol alloy ore, but it didn't translate into any profit.
