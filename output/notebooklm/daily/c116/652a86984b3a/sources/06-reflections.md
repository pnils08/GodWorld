# Oakland, in their own words

## Giancarlo Hernandez — Third Baseman, Oakland A's

> Yeah, I saw the articles. I know what they're saying — that Kevin's looking over his shoulder, that I'm some kind of glove-first insurance policy, or that one of us is a trade chip. I'll say this: I didn't ask to come here to take anybody's job. I came here to help the team win. That's it. Kevin's a good player. He's young, he can hit. And I know what I bring — defense. I take pride in that. I've worked my whole life to make plays people don't expect. If they want me out there every day, I'll give them everything I've got. If they need me off the bench late in games, I'll be ready for that too. The trade stuff, the speculation — that's above my pay grade. I just showed up to work.
