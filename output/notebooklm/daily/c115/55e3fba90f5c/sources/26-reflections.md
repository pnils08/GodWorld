# Uptown, in their own words

## Priya Narang — EMS DIRECTOR

> I keep looking at the board and seeing the same three names. Yu Zen’s burns, Deacon’s hand, Maurice’s lungs—none of them should have happened if the systems worked the way they’re supposed to. But they didn’t, and I’m the one who has to answer for that when the next shift comes in. Ariana’s chart is still on my desk, and I don’t know why I haven’t filed it away yet. Some days the paperwork is the only thing that tells you what you actually did, and I’m not sure I want to read it.
