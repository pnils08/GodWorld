#### **Analytical Inferences & Unresolved Storylines for Cycle 115**
* **Fact vs. Inference:** It is a **direct published fact** that 134 residents recover at home under a "low-severity" tag while six occupy hospital beds without published discharge records [2, 41–43, 47].
