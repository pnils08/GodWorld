# Oaks’ Cold Streak Is Now a Test of the Build

Wilson Shepard answered from the press conference about rhythm, then about morale. The Oaks’ numbers made both questions unavoidable: one C109 result put the club at 0-2 with a six-game losing streak; another moved it to 0-3 and seven straight losses. The preseason label softens the record, but it does not erase the problem facing an expansion franchise still trying to establish what its shape is.

“I've been feeling like we're still finding our rhythm, you know? The guys are putting in the work, and I'm confident we'll tighten up and be ready for the real deal,” Shepard said. “As for morale, I think the team's doing okay, considering. We're not getting the results we want, but we're not letting it get us down either.”

The board read is less reassuring than the language. In the first result, Wendell Carter Jr. finished with 8 points and 8 rebounds. In the second, he had 12 points and 7 rebounds. The center’s line improved, but the result did not. That is the distinction the Oaks have to confront: individual production is appearing without a dependable team conclusion.

Austin Reaves supplied the clearest scoring response in the second game, with 24 points and 4 assists. Dillon Brooks added 21 points and 8 rebounds. Those are credible contributions from two players expected to give an expansion roster structure. They still ended in another loss.

“Honestly, it's tough to see the team struggling like this. I feel like we're still finding our footing, and it's hard not to worry when we're not seeing the wins. But I trust that we'll get it together, we just need to keep working and supporting each other,” Reaves said.

Brooks was more direct about the emotional cost. “Honestly, I've noticed the team's morale has been a bit down lately, but I think we're all just trying to stay focused and work through this tough stretch. As someone who lives in Uptown, I've seen how much the community loves and supports The Oaks, and I'm confident we'll get back on track soon.”

Ernesto Quintero described the same stretch without the cushion of a player’s box score. “I've seen the team's morale take a hit with this cold streak, it's tough to keep the energy up when we're not winning. I feel like we're all trying to stay focused and support each other, but it's hard not to get discouraged. I'm trying to stay steady and keep my head in the game, but it's definitely affecting us.”

That leaves Shepard with the central question of the build. The Oaks can point to Reaves’ 24 points, Brooks’ 21 and Carter’s changing production, but the franchise’s stability remains uncertain. Can the next result show a repeatable rotation and a first victory, rather than another strong individual line inside a longer streak?
