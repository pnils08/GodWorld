# Baylight District, in their own words

## Iris Pham — Barista

> I work at a coffee shop, so I know what it's like to be the person behind the counter that nobody thinks to name. When I see the roster for a place like Oakland Casino Town, it's all managers and the big names. But there's a whole crew in the back—cooks, cleaners, security—who show up every shift and keep the place running. They're the ones I see in the neighborhood, grabbing a quick coffee before their shift or heading home after a long night. The roster doesn't carry them because they're not the ones making the decisions or taking the credit. But without them, the whole thing falls apart.
