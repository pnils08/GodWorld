# Exchange — debate (Cycle 111)

- participants: POP-00034 Avery Santana | POP-00007 Hal Richmond
- topic: West Oakland Tenant Defense Fund (INIT-010) — vote at cycle 112 (initiative)

---

**Avery Santana:** Hal, I’m not going to pretend this is a line-item debate. We’ve got families in West Oakland getting eviction notices because they can’t afford a lawyer — that’s the whole fight. This $4.2 million isn’t a handout, it’s a shield, and it keeps people in their homes while we fix the bigger picture. I’m for it, and I’ll say that from any podium in this city.

**Hal Richmond:** Avery, you're not wrong that folks are getting squeezed—I see the notices taped to doors two blocks from my place. But I've watched this city pour money into programs that look good on paper and vanish when the landlord's lawyer shows up with a better argument. If this fund's got teeth, real ones, then I'm with you. But I need to know it's not just a Band-Aid while rents keep climbing.

**Avery Santana:** I hear you, Hal, and I've seen those same notices. That's why this fund isn't just about court appearances — it's about buying families enough time to fight back and stay put while we push the bigger rent stabilization work through. If we do this right, the eviction defense becomes the wedge, not the whole tool. I want your eyes on the disbursement rules, because teeth only work if we sharpen them together.

**Hal Richmond:** Then we're on the same page about sharpening. I've seen too many good intentions get buried under paperwork and fine print—especially when the money finally lands. You want me to look at the rules? I'll do more than look, I'll bring the street-level view: who's actually getting served, which landlords are playing games, and whether this fund reaches the folks who don't know their rights yet. Let's make sure the shield doesn't just cover the loudest voices.
