### 1. Ref check (§Design step 4, `docs/plans/2026-11-26-city-feed.md:79-80`)

- **Co-presence and predicate swaps pass `"contains the cycle and the person"`:** `toolCanonRecall` (`scripts/cron-desk-writer.js:975-1015`) returns up to 6 520-char multi-sentence chunks from `bay-tribune` (editions, supplementals, daily pieces); `toolSearchWorld` (`scripts/cron-desk-writer.js:574-577`) returns 6 `world-data` snippets. Because an edition or multi-story article names dozens of residents, figures, and events in one document, checking only that `canon doc contains the cycle and the person` (`plan:80`) passes when a model attaches Person B's metric/action to Person A in the same C114 edition, or invents a causal link (`"X caused Y"`) between two facts in the same doc. Because `City_Feed` feeds ~14 daily seats whose gate-passed pieces enter canon (`plan:126`), any swapped figure or false causal link written into `City_Feed.Notes` becomes an approved `FACT` in W1/W3 (`livedExperiencePacket.js:380-386`) and enters published canon.
- **Unchecked `sheet_read` polarity and `ask_worker` / `citizen_lookup` blind spots (`plan:79-80`):** `"sheet row exists and holds the figure"` passes if a number appears anywhere in the row (e.g., matching `5` employees or `91` attendance while swapping the metric label or direction). Meanwhile, Step 3 allows `ask_worker` and `citizen_lookup`, `Refs` (`plan:70`) lists only `canon doc id, sheet tab + row, or staged file`, and Step 4 defines no verification rule for `ask_worker` or `citizen_lookup` outputs at all. Also note `toolSheetRead` (`scripts/cron-desk-writer.js:829-831`) blocks reading `Hospital_Ledger`, `Health_Cause_Queue`, `Casino_Ledger`, `City_Treasury`, `Judicial_Ledger`, `Cycle_Weather`, `LifeHistory_Log`, and `Employment_Roster` unless widened.
- **Cheapest check that catches right-doc / wrong-claim & swapped figures:** Require every `Refs` item to cite the **exact returned unit** (`docId + chunk/sentence index` or `tab!row`), not a whole edition doc, and enforce deterministic **same-unit numeric + entity + anti-causal binding** (reusing the `significantNumbers` / single-unit pattern in `scripts/livedExperiencePacketV2.js:617-629`): every significant number, cycle token, and proper name in the history sentence must co-occur verbatim inside that single cited chunk/sentence/row, and any causal connector (`because`, `caused`, `due to`, `led to`, `triggered`) is rejected unless present in the cited unit itself.

### 3. The trigger (`docs/plans/2026-11-26-city-feed.md:84` vs `scripts/cron-civic-run.js:4370-4486`)

`cron-civic-run.js` week state is **not** a complete or consistent freshness guarantee for both `output/beats/*.jsonl` and `output/desk_signal_c{cycle}.json`:

- **Hole 1 — `engineFireInfo` never checks `desk_signal_c{cycle}.json` (`scripts/cron-civic-run.js:4390-4396`):** `engineFireInfo` only checks `world_summary_c{cycle}.md`, `engine_audit_c{cycle}.json`, and `beats/meta.json` (`beatsDumpReady` at `:4370-4373`). Although `scripts/runCycle.js:157` checks `desk_signal_c{cycle}.json` in Step 5, a manual/partial `world_summary` write or missing/stale `desk_signal` still lets `state.engineFiredAt` go non-null.
- **Race 2 — `output/beats/*.jsonl` is pre-apply once `chain applied` is true (`scripts/runCycle.js:162-167` vs `scripts/cron-civic-run.js:4617-4624`):** Step 5.56 runs `dumpBeatTabs` *before* the civic chain applies tracker changes (`applyTrackerUpdates.js --apply`). Nothing re-runs `dumpBeatTabs` after `--apply`. Thus, at the exact moment the plan's trigger (`engineFiredAt set, chain applied, beats meta.json at the Cycle`) fires, `output/beats/Initiative_Tracker.jsonl` is **older than the live Sheet** while `cityFeedWriter`'s `sheet_read` reads the post-apply live Sheet.
- **Race 3 — `apply.status === 'done'` does not wait for `directive` or `runCycle` completion (`scripts/cron-civic-run.js:4425-4428, 4474-4484, 4646-4657`):** In `runTick`, `directive` runs post-apply and can sit in `partial` (`resumeDirectiveCommit`) while `close_c{cycle}.json` already has `applied: true` (including cutoff applies under `decideApply` at `:4511-4513` when verdicts were missing). Also, `dumpBeatTabs.js:143-150` carries a monotonic stamp (G-PF35): if `beats/meta.json` advances ahead of a skipped week, `beatsDumpReady` and `week_state` must be checked for the exact same `cycle`.
