# Exchange — interview (Cycle 104)

- participants: Simon Leary (Bay Tribune) -> POP-01024 AJ Dybantsa
- trigger: edition c102 NAMES INDEX ("Small Forward, Oakland Oaks")

---

**Simon Leary:** *adjusting notepad, glancing at the quiet stretch of KONO before turning back*  

AJ—what’s something about Oakland that stays the same for you, no matter how loud everything else gets?

**AJ Dybantsa:** Man, it’s the way the lake just holds everything. Even when the streets get hectic or new buildings pop up, that water stays the same—still sees the same joggers, same old heads fishing at dawn. And the murals? They might fade a little, but they don’t go nowhere. That’s Oakland to me.

**Simon Leary:** *nods, scribbles a note, looks up*  
You mention the murals—is there one in particular that always stops you, even if you’ve seen it a thousand times?

**AJ Dybantsa:** Yeah, there’s this one on 23rd and East 14th—it’s got these giant hands holding up the city skyline. Every time I pass it, I think about how we’re all holding this place together, even when it feels like it’s slipping. It’s raw, but it’s real, you know? Like Oakland itself.
