## 2. Comprehensive Audit of Published Coverage

### 2.1 Sports Beat Summary
* **Mariano Rosales Contract Analysis (Anthony Raines):** Raines examined closer Mariano Rosales' five-year, $88 million contract extension ($17.6 million AAV, running 2042–2046 with free agency in 2047), which replaces his 2041 one-year, $10.1 million contract. The deal rewards a 2041 season where Rosales went 8-3 with 42 saves, a 1.84 ERA, 1.7 WAR, and an All-Star selection (striking out 82 and walking 14 in 63.2 innings). Raines contextualized his 2041 true baseline metrics (11.6 K/9, 2.0 BB/9, 5.86 K/BB) against historical volatility: in 2039, Rosales posted a 2.09 ERA over 77.2 IP (8.9 K/9, 3.0 BB/9, 2.96 K/BB). In 2040 with Baltimore, his ERA more than doubled to 4.18 (26 ER in 56.0 IP, 9.0 K/9, 2.1 BB/9, 4.31 K/BB) despite maintaining 38 saves. In 2038, over 89.2 IP, he posted a 4.11 ERA. The piece noted Rosales' pitch mix (96 mph 4-seam, 94 mph 2-seam, 84 mph changeup at age 27, 95 overall) and isolated World Series Game 2 (2 ER allowed in relief of Travis Coles, alongside Clark Saryan's 2 ER) as a single appearance supporting observation rather than projection.
* **A's Off-Season Roster Moves:** 
  * *Mark Aitken Departure:* Veteran 1B Mark Aitken signed a 1-year, $21 million deal with the Colorado Rockies, leaving Oakland after a 2041 season of .283 AVG, 153 hits, 33 HRs, 87 RBIs, and 3.2 WAR ($21.4M salary in 2041). Aitken (93 overall, 3 Gold Gloves, 2037 Playoff MVP) leaves a void in leadership and community presence. In response, GM Mike Paulson shifted Ernesto Quintero across the diamond to 1B, opening third base to a spring training competition between Kevin Clark (.327 AVG in 55 ABs, 83 discipline, 87 clutch) and Giancarlo Hernandez (96 fielding rating).
  * *Vladimir Gonzalez Extension:* Right fielder Vladimir Gonzalez signed a 5-year, $119 million extension ($23.8 million AAV), avoiding arbitration. Coming off a career-best 4.7 WAR season (.306 AVG, 161 hits, 28 HRs, 76 RBIs, 17 SBs, only 60 K's in 527 ABs at age 27, 92 overall), Gonzalez absorbed the $21M+ freed up by Aitken's departure (Gonzalez earned $2.7M in 2041).
  * *Danny Horn MVP Season & Wiffle Ball:* Center fielder Danny Horn (30 years old, 99 overall, signed at $37.8M AAV through 2044) put up an unprecedented 13.3 WAR season (.338 AVG, 210 hits, 48 2B, 9 3B, 56 HR, 113 RBI, 152 runs, 80 SB with only 9 CS, 65 K's in 671 PA, .997 fielding % with 1 error in 1,279 innings). Horn won the MVP, Hank Aaron Award, batting title, home run title, Silver Slugger, and All-Star selection. Horn is also bringing his second annual wiffle ball tournament to Dimond this cycle (originally launched in West Oakland in C79 and hosted in Temescal in C80).
* **Oakland Oaks Basketball Coverage:**
  * *Home Win vs. Nuggets (137-113):* Played before a 94% home crowd. Austin Reaves scored 32 points in 22 minutes (8-14 FG, 6-10 3PT, 10-10 FT). T. Jerome logged a 14-point, 11-assist double-double off the bench in 25 minutes. Wendell Carter Jr. added 17 points (6-9 FG) and AJ Dybantsa chipped in 15 points.
  * *Comeback Road Win vs. Warriors (98-96):* Trailing 59-44 at halftime (down 15), the Oaks outscored Golden State 54-37 in the second half (30-19 3rd qtr, 24-18 4th qtr). Reaves led with 23 points, 6 rebounds, 5 assists, and 3 steals in 33 minutes. T. Jerome scored 17 points (7-13 FG, 0 TO), extending his double-figure scoring streak to five games (19, 28, 23, 14, 17 points).
  * *Head Coach Wilson Shepard:* The Oaks moved to 11-2 overall (3-1 Cup play). Under Shepard, the team holds a stellar 10-2-1 fourth-quarter record across 13 games.

### 2.3 Health & Data Science Beat Summary

  * *Yu Zen (Rockridge):* Admitted C105, discharged C109.
  * *Maurice Franklin (Rockridge):* Admitted C106, discharged C111.

ME Patel noted unprompted on the record that he is uncomfortable closing Yu Zen's file, stating his lacerations "don't sit right with a fall, and the camera was blind."

### 2.4 Transit & Infrastructure Beat Summary
* **12th Street Station Performance (Trevor Shimizu):** 12th Street Oakland City Center carried 14,175 riders in C117 (up 1,962 from C116), while on-time performance dropped from 81% to 77%. 
* **Debunking Crowding as Sole Cause:** BART Station Manager Eleanor Rivera and PR Contact Ximena Mehta attributed delays to "waves" of passengers expanding platform dwell times. Shimizu disproved this as the sole cause by comparing historical data: in C112, 12th Street carried nearly identical ridership (14,162) while maintaining an 89% on-time rate. Furthermore, 12th Street landed on the exact same 77% on-time metric across vastly different volume levels: C107 (8,191 riders), C114 (10,011 riders), and C117 (14,175 riders), proving a structural bottleneck exists independent of headcounts.
* **Systemwide Shifts & Traffic Corridor Gridlock:** Total transit ridership across 8 stations reached 71,090 (up from 68,505). Significant volume shifts included Rockridge (+1,388 to 5,945) and Fruitvale (-1,336 to 8,007, 85% on-time). Corridors experienced heavy gridlock spikes:
  * *I-880 North:* Index 100 (up 11; game day & Baylight construction).
  * *I-980:* Index 100 (up 14; game day & Baylight construction).
  * *International Blvd:* Index 84 (up 11; **no logged cause**).
  * *MacArthur Blvd:* Index 77 (up 7; **no logged cause**).

### 3.2 Detailed Analysis of FLAGGED Draft: Jordan Velez (Business Desk)

* **Draft Details:**
  * **Title:** *"Oakland Casino Town lost three jobs. Nobody will say who they were."*
  * **Author:** Jordan Velez (Economics & Labor)
  * **File Path:** `output/cron-compare/flagged/business_c117_business-desk_packet-v2_anthropic-claude-sonnet-5-5.md`

#### 3.2.1 Summary of Reported Content
Velez reported a net loss of 3 jobs at Oakland Casino Town in the Baylight District (headcount dropping from 97 to 94 employees, representing a -1.5% annual growth rate and a net loss of 5 jobs since C115 when headcount was 99). Velez calculated that based on the casino's $70,000 average salary, the 3 departures removed approximately $210,000 in annual payroll. 

The draft contrasted management's account from Casino Manager Philly Rodriguez (claiming "attrition" where two dealers moved to San Jose and one security guard returned to school) against bookmaker Manfred Owens' observations of stretched shifts, thinner weeknight floors, and "folks who got let go here." 

Velez noted single-job additions across other local sectors: DigitalOcean (+1 job to 801), Bayline 6 (+1 job to 143), Atlas Bay Architects (+1 job), and the Bay Tribune (+1 job). Velez also detailed wagering metrics ($246,000 house float; 17 undocked wagers; 14 sports wagers; net gain of $320 by Maria Keen on a $385 stake; loss of $428 by Jax Caldera). 

Throughout the piece, Velez heavily quoted Deputy Mayor for Economic Development Marcus Osei regarding municipal documentation and labor churn.

#### 3.2.2 Editorial Gate Finding
The automated editorial gate halted publication based on a **High-Severity** rule violation:
* **Check:** `invented-voice`
* **Target:** Marcus Osei (POP-00036)
* **Specific Issue:** `"Marcus Osei (POP-00036) cannot be quoted: INSTITUTIONAL"`

#### 3.2.3 Cross-Source Verification & Gate Verdict
A comprehensive cross-source audit confirms the following:
1. **Source Mapping of Valid Quotes:** The direct quotes attributed to Casino Manager Philly Rodriguez match his exact recorded transcript in the raw interview source file `West Oakland, in their own words`. Similarly, the statements attributed to floor bookmaker Manfred Owens match his exact transcript in `Jack London, in their own words`.
2. **Verification of Flagged Voice:** Deputy Mayor Marcus Osei (POP-00036) appears nowhere in any raw citizen interview transcript, interview log, or city state record for Cycle 117. The quotes attributed to Osei (such as claiming his office has *"no names, no roles, no severance record"*, describing the shift as *"churn, not growth"*, and stating *"one job added at DigitalOcean and three lost at the casino is not a wash"*) were completely fabricated within the draft.

* **Verdict:** The gate decision is **100% accurate and holds up completely against all source context**. The draft violated fundamental Tribune accuracy standards by synthesizing quotes for an institutional official who did not speak on the record. The hold on this draft remains absolute until it is fully rewritten and scrubbed of unverified voices.
