## UNDOCKED: Silk takes the top rung, but the week belongs to three different routes

Three pilots filled this week’s UNDOCKED card, and Ronald Silk leaves it with the clearest board position: rank #1, 38.15 achievement points, one cycle led, a current streak of one, and zero credits on the standings. Luis Taz and Lena Okafor each brought two episodes to the week’s picture. Their runs make for a lively supporting cast, but the supplied standings give no basis for placing either above or below the other.

Silk’s advantage is a lead, not a long reign: one cycle led and a streak of one. His Tuesday route crossed a long chain of stops, with one titanium ore and one iron ore mined; he finished even on credits. Friday brought another long route, again ending even. There’s no need to pretend that a route alone explains his standing. Silk’s own account offers a more personal lens: “the mindset and focus I use for complex wiring jobs seems to translate to navigating through the stars and managing resources during the show.” He is an Electrician, but that connection is his reflection, not proof of what moved the board.

Taz, a Janitor, supplied the week’s sharpest contrast in pace. On Sunday, his episode recorded twelve fights, two iron ore mined, and an even credit result. Wednesday’s report turned to mining: nine copper ore, two antimatter containment cells, and four iron ore, again with credits even. Taz has described the alertness of his day job as something he brings to flying: “stay alert, stay moving, and get the job done.” His week gives that line a vivid rhythm without making the job a scoreboard formula.

Okafor, a Daycare Director, showed the most visible change in credits across her two supplied episodes. Monday’s run recorded eleven iron ore and six copper ore mined, one fight, and an even result. Thursday added a contract, further mining and sales, one fight, and an 18-credit gain. That makes her Thursday the week’s one recorded positive credit finish; it does not, by itself, establish a standings climb.

The earlier approved-pack lookback available for this recap returned no prior episodes for Silk, Taz, or Okafor. That is a limit of the available record, not proof that none aired. So this week’s board reads as a snapshot: Silk on top, with the other two pilots’ runs giving the next edition plenty to compare. Will Ronald Silk continue to lead the UNDOCKED board—or will another week’s flying redraw the order?
