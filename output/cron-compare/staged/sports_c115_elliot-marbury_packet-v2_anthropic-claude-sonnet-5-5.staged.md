# Taveras's 54 Home Runs Sit Outside Seven Seasons of His Own Record

Eric Taveras, second baseman for the Athletics, hit 54 home runs in 555 at-bats in 2041. His dossier works that out to one home run every 10.3 at-bats. In the six earlier seasons on the same dossier, his best home-run rate was one every 16.3 at-bats, in 2038. This memo asks whether that jump is a new baseline or a single-season outlier. The record supports only a limited answer.

Before 2041, he hit between 19 and 31 home runs in each of six seasons. Five of those seasons had 555 or more at-bats, so the sample behind each rate is full-season size. The 2041 figure is 54. Slugging moved with it: .638 in 2041, against .459 in 2040 and a range of .374 to .495 across the first six seasons.

**The strikeout column**

In 2041 he struck out 81 times, one every 6.9 at-bats. His earlier rates ran from one every 4.1 at-bats (2036) to one every 4.9 (2038). The 2040 season was one every 4.4 and 2039 was one every 5.0. His 2040 total was 132 strikeouts and his 2036 total was 144. The 2041 strikeout rate is therefore also outside his own range, in the better direction.

Walks did not follow the strikeouts down. He drew 36 in 2041, against 48 in 2040 and 49 in 2039. His OBP of .339 is near the .337 of 2035, even though his average is .295 against .270 that year. In 2041 the gain shows up in extra-base power and contact, not in plate discipline as the walk column records it. He had 2 stolen bases, a career low on this dossier. His RBI total of 124 is the highest of the seven lines (the next is 92 in 2040).

**The claim**

Taveras's 2041 home-run rate (one every 10.3 at-bats) and strikeout rate (one every 6.9 at-bats) are both outside the range of his six prior seasons. The record cannot yet separate a lasting change from one season at the extreme end. His six earlier lines point toward the outlier reading. One 555-at-bat season is enough to establish an event, but it is not enough to establish an identity.

**What would kill it**

Take 2042 with at least 500 at-bats. If Taveras hits a home run no less often than once every 16.3 at-bats, the pace of his best pre-2041 season, and strikes out no more often than once every 4.9 at-bats, the outlier reading fails. In that case 2041 would be the start of a new range. If his home-run rate falls back past once every 20 at-bats, it was an outlier. If it lands between those two marks, the dossier still cannot decide, and the memo says so.
