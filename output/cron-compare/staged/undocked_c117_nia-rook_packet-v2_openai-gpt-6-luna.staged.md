# UNDOCKED: Daniela Kapoor makes the route the story

Daniela Kapoor’s Sunday-night flight put exploration in the foreground: a route across nine named systems, mining along the way, and a string of achievements whose points give the night its shape. The board’s top line, meanwhile, belongs to Marguerite Kimball-Ostrow, listed at #1 with 49.08 achievement points and a current streak of two cycles led. Two different kinds of momentum, sharing the same episode.

Kapoor, a teacher, went through Sol, Alpha Centauri, Tau Ceti, Electra, Mimosa, Ross 154, Arcturus, Timberline and Maplevale. The mining haul was 8 copper ore, 15 iron ore and 1 antimatter containment cell. That’s a lot of movement and material for a night whose score is especially legible in the achievements: Wanderer brought 8 points, Maiden Jump 5, Voyager I 3.2, The Truth Is Out There 2.5, Cartographer 2.4 and Chronicler 1.2. Voyager II added 0.8; Prospector I, Far Traveler and Jump Master each added a smaller share.

The temptation is to make the route itself the headline. But the points suggest a more particular pleasure: this flight kept finding new ways to count as discovery. Kapoor described it simply: “Sunday night was a great flight on UNDOCKED. I was happy with how it went, I mined some good resources and jumped around to different systems. I went through Sol, Alpha Centauri, and a few others, and managed to earn a bunch of achievements. It was a lot of fun.”

At a watch-room table, I can imagine the argument splitting over what made the night satisfying: the long string of destinations, or the feeling that each stop left a mark. The episode gives both sides something to point to. A viewer drawn to the route gets nine systems; a viewer drawn to the record gets achievements ranging from broad travel to the fine-grained additions of a hundredth of a point.

And then there’s the board. Kimball-Ostrow leads; Kapoor’s flight is its own story, not proof of a change in rank. The paper’s C116 coverage followed Kimball-Ostrow through a long route and described a night with no credit swing, while the current standings put her atop the achievement-points board. That earlier coverage offers a useful contrast in emphasis, not a verdict on this episode. UNDOCKED is liveliest when the chair’s appetite and the board’s answer can sit side by side. Sunday night, Kapoor flew outward; the top rung stayed with Kimball-Ostrow.
