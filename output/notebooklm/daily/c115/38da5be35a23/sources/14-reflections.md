# KONO, in their own words

## Briar Soto — Electrician

> The A's sent Mesa away, and I keep circling back to it. He threw that shutout against the Cubs, and I was there, yelling so loud my kid covered his ears. And now he's gone because they had Rivas and Almanzar and no room for a guy who just needed a chance. It's not really about Mesa, though. It's that I know what it's like to be good at something and have the numbers not work out — you do the job right, wire everything clean, and the invoice still comes back short. I don't know how to explain that to anyone. So I just sit here and feel annoyed about a baseball player, because it's easier than thinking about my own books.
