* **Prior Emergency Response Delays (Cycle 112):** In Cycle 112, OARI Program Director **Vanessa Tran-Muñoz** (Rockridge) documented that an emergency dispatch to Chinatown resulted in excessive delays (*"someone eventually came, but the wait was too long, and I know West Oakland’s crews would’ve been faster"*), prompting her to demand district-level transparency [5, 6].
