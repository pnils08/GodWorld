## 3. Gate Desk Decisions & Audit of Flagged Drafts

### 3.1 Summary of Gate Desk Activity

During Cycle 116, the editorial gate desk reviewed all incoming analytical drafts to enforce factual accuracy and compliance with canonical databases. Two drafts were flagged and held back from publication. 

Both held files were authored by sports reporter Elliot Marbury and focused on Athletics starting pitcher Henry Rivas. The sole cause for both flags was the insertion of non-canonical pitching statistics for veteran pitcher Benji Dillon within the draft text.

---

### 3.2 Evaluation of FLAGGED Draft 1

* **Headline:** "Rivas's 1.6 Walks per Nine Is a 136.1-Inning Reading, Not a New Level"
* **File Path:** `output/cron-compare/flagged/sports_c116_elliot-marbury_packet-v2_anthropic-claude-sonnet-5-5-1800.md`

#### What the Draft Reported
Marbury analyzed Henry Rivas’s 2041 command figures: 11-4 record, 2.64 ERA, 123 hits, 40 earned runs, 16 home runs, 24 walks, and 133 strikeouts (1.6 BB/9, 8.8 K/9, 5.54 K/BB) over 136.1 innings in 22 starts. The draft argued that Rivas’s low walk rate was a small-sample anomaly rather than a permanent career shift, comparing it against Rivas's full-season baselines from 2038 (183.1 IP, 39 BB, 1.9 BB/9), 2039 (187.2 IP, 38 BB, 1.8 BB/9), and 2040 (184.2 IP, 59 BB, 2.9 BB/9). 

To contextualize Rivas within the A's rotation, Marbury included a peer comparison section featuring Arturo Ramos (212.2 IP in 33 GS, 23-1 record, 2.41 ERA, 1.7 BB/9, 7.7 K/9, 4.44 K/BB, 41 BB, 182 SO) and Benji Dillon. In Dillon's entry, Marbury stated that Dillon threw 150.1 innings over 35 appearances and 28 starts, posting a 10-8 record, 4.43 ERA, 3.9 BB/9, 7.9 K/9, 2.03 K/BB, 65 walks, and 132 strikeouts.

#### What the Gate Found
The editorial gate flagged the draft with high severity, issuing the following finding:
> *Issue:* Contradicts canonical 2041 season pitching record in ground truth (Benji Dillon was 9-8 with a 4.61 ERA, 120 strikeouts, 56 walks in 140.2 innings).

#### Verification Against Sources
Cross-referencing the draft's assertions against canonical ground truth established in *The Bay Tribune Daily C116 — Cycle 116 opens — Sports* log confirms the following:
* **Canonical Ground Truth for Benji Dillon (2041):** 38 years old, 9-8 record, 4.61 ERA, 140.2 innings pitched, 120 strikeouts, 56 walks, 2.0 WAR.
* **Draft Citation for Benji Dillon (2041):** 10-8 record, 4.43 ERA, 150.1 innings pitched, 132 strikeouts, 65 walks.

**Conclusion:** The gate desk’s rejection is **100% verified and correct**. Marbury introduced completely inaccurate, non-canonical statistics for Benji Dillon. The draft was properly spiked.
