## Patricia Nwosu — Deputy Health Officer, Alameda County

And I keep looking at it, and I keep thinking—who actually gets those spots? I asked at a briefing once. Got a very smooth answer about equity metrics and outreach targets. But I've seen the data. I know which ZIP codes show up in the program rosters. And it's not the ones my kids' classmates live in. Does it matter? Yes. It matters that the flyer promises something the numbers don't back up.
