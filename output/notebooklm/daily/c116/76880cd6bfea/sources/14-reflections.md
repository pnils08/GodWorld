## Warren Ashford — City Council District 7

> Council District 7 — Warren Ashford here. Let me address that record directly, because I stand by every line of it. **On the clinical dollars point:** That wasn't bureaucracy for its own sake. We had a facility asking for continued commercial support while its quarterly audit data lagged. An open door doesn't mean an open checkbook. I don't care how noble the mission is — if we're spending district commercial money, we get the proof of performance. That's not obstruction, that's stewardship. **On the vitality-versus-sentiment numbers:** The 11.89 vitality figure tells me people are showing up. The -0.06 sentiment tells me the people running those shops aren't feeling the benefit. When you see that split, you don't throw more unmonitored cash at the problem — you ask what operational friction is eating their margins. Rent, permitting delays, theft, utility costs. That's where the audit goes next. **On the KONO fire follow-through:** Contained fire, but contained doesn't mean concluded. The structural inspections aren't punishment — they're prevention.
