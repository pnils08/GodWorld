## Oaks Struggle to Find Rhythm in Preseason
The Oakland Oaks hosted game 2 of the preseason against the Warriors, but were unable to secure their first victory, losing yet again despite strong efforts from Austin Reaves. As the team struggles to find its footing, the players and coaching staff are working to stay focused and support each other through this tough stretch.

"I've been feeling like we're still finding our rhythm, you know?" said Wilson Shepard, Head Coach of the Oakland Oaks, in a press conference. "The guys are putting in the work, and I'm confident we'll tighten up and be ready for the real deal. As for morale, I think the team's doing okay, considering. We're not getting the results we want, but we're not letting it get us down either."

Austin Reaves, who scored 24 points and had 4 assists in the game, shared his thoughts on the team's struggles. "Honestly, it's tough to see the team struggling like this. I feel like we're still finding our footing, and it's hard not to worry when we're not seeing the wins. But I trust that we'll get it together, we just need to keep working and supporting each other."

Dillon Brooks, who had 21 points and 8 rebounds, noted that the team's morale has been affected by the losing streak. "Honestly, I've noticed the team's morale has been a bit down lately, but I think we're all just trying to stay focused and work through this tough stretch. As someone who lives in Uptown, I've seen how much the community loves and supports The Oaks, and I'm confident we'll get back on track soon."

Ernesto Quintero, who has been following the team's progress, observed that the cold streak has taken a toll on the team's energy. "I've seen the team's morale take a hit with this cold streak, it's tough to keep the energy up when we're not winning. I feel like we're all trying to stay focused and support each other, but it's hard not to get discouraged. I'm trying to stay steady and keep my head in the game, but it's definitely affecting us."

As the Oaks continue to work through their struggles, the question remains: can they break their losing streak and turn their season around?
