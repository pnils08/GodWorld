# Grand Lake, in their own words

## Vinnie Keane — Former Designated Hitter, Oakland A's Legend

> To be honest, I haven't heard much about Anthony Raines around Grand Lake. I know he's a journalist covering the A's, and I've seen his name pop up in the sports feed. I'm still getting used to not being on the field every day, so I don't really know him personally, but it sounds like he's making a name for himself in the sports scene.

> The A's trade is a mixed bag for me. On one hand, getting Giancarlo Hernandez is huge for the team's future. His glove is elite, and he's got a lot of potential. On the other hand, losing Carmen Mesa stings — he was stuck here, and I'm glad he's getting a chance to start in Philadelphia. As for the community, I'm keeping an eye on how this all plays out. We're a baseball town, and trades like this can go either way. I'm just happy to be involved, staying connected through the Firehouse 29 academy.
