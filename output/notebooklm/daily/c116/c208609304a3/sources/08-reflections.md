# Fruitvale, in their own words

## Karim Avery — Security Guard

> I see the pressure before it even gets to the kitchen. The markets are the first stop for a lot of folks coming off the shuttle or the BART — they're grabbing coffee, grabbing something quick, and they're already running late or already tired. The kitchen workers, they're inside, they're focused on the tickets, the heat, the rush. But I'm out front, watching who's waiting, who's checking their phone for the third time, who's arguing with a vendor over a dollar. That's the neighborhood's pressure — it's not just about the work, it's about the whole day stacking up on you before you even clock in. And when I walk through Jack London after my shift, I see the same faces at the taquerias and the same tired eyes. The kitchen folks see the plates, I see the people carrying the weight that brought them to those plates.
