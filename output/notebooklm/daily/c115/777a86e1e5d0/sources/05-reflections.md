# Brooklyn, in their own words

## Noelia Bello — Pharmacy tech

> Honestly, underneath the even keel, there's a bit of a hum. Not worry, exactly, but a quiet alertness. I rearranged the front room the other day and I keep walking past it, looking at it like it might tell me something about the day ahead. It’s just me here, and I keep the routine steady—the route, the regulars, the same faces—and that’s fine. But sometimes I catch myself wondering if steady is the same as waiting for something I haven't named yet.
