# The Pastry Pulse of OakHouse  

At OakHouse in Rockridge, the pastry team doesn’t just bake—they sync. Mimmie Carlisle, Jesus Jorden, and Kalyani Alvarez move through the kitchen like a unit, their hands shaping dough, their rhythms steady even as the rest of the restaurant buzzes around them. This isn’t just a job for them; it’s a shared climb toward something larger.  

Mimmie Carlisle, OakHouse’s lead pastry chef, describes the dynamic as something electric yet grounded. *“Working with Jesus and Kalyani at OakHouse has been a real push for me—it’s like we’re all climbing toward something bigger, together. I’ve felt that drive in the kitchen, especially lately. It’s not just about the pastries; it’s about creating something that feels like ours.”*  

For Jesus Jorden, the rhythm of the kitchen is second nature. *“Working with Mimmie and Kalyani at OakHouse has been a steady rhythm—like the kind of teamwork that doesn’t need much talking. We just know what needs to be done, and it gets done. It’s not flashy, but it’s reliable, and that’s what matters in a kitchen.”*  

Kalyani Alvarez, the third corner of the team, brings her own flair to the mix. *“Working with Mimmie and Jesus at OakHouse has been a real pleasure. We each bring something unique to the table, and it’s been rewarding to see how our different styles complement each other. It’s not just about the pastries we create, but the camaraderie and shared passion that make the team special.”*  

Depak Ying, a Rockridge local, has noticed the team’s impact. *“I’ve heard people around Rockridge talking about how the pastry team at OakHouse—Mimmie, Jesus, and Kalyani—are really raising the bar with their desserts. It’s nice to see such talent in our neighborhood.”*  

The team’s collaboration is a microcosm of what makes kitchens like OakHouse’s thrive: a shared vision, a steady rhythm, and a willingness to push toward something greater. What does it take to sustain that kind of energy in a high-pressure kitchen?
