# The Pipeline Row Says "Continues." It Has Said That for Three Cycles.

A flyer for a youth apprenticeship program can sit on a corkboard in West Oakland, East Oakland or Fruitvale and read as a promise. The Oakland Youth Apprenticeship Pipeline has three tracks and eight trades. It targets more than 400 Baylight placements by Year 3. I don't know whose corkboard the flyer is on. I do know what the city's own sheet says about it, and I've spent the cycle reading that sheet line by line.

**What the record establishes**

The Initiative Tracker lists INIT-007, the Oakland Youth Apprenticeship Pipeline, as a $12.5 million workforce program. Its status is "passed" and its implementation phase is "implementation-active." The proposing office is MAYOR-01, under Avery Santana. The tracker notes the program was announced in C73 as part of the Baylight community benefits package and was not a standalone council vote.

The affected neighborhoods are West Oakland, East Oakland and Fruitvale.

The paper's audit for C115 finds that the economic initiative carrying West Oakland has not advanced in three cycles.

The milestone notes run like this:

- **C107:** a public town hall enrollment event convened. Applicant intake was operational, with cohort placement targets established.
- **C111:** a first cohort placement report was mandated for Cycle 112.
- **C113:** the first cohort placement report was published, and an accountability review was directed.
- **C114:** a placement-gap analysis and adjustment plan was directed for publication, with explicit West Oakland and Fruitvale review.
- **C115:** the same analysis and plan are "required for publication with explicit West Oakland and Fruitvale review; implementation continues."

The next scheduled action is "Publish the placement-gap analysis and program adjustment plan." The row sets that for Cycle 116.

**What the record sets side by side**

The C114 and C115 entries are nearly identical. One cycle directed the analysis. The next cycle required it. Neither says it was delivered.

The budget fields sit next to each other in the same row. BudgetTotal reads 12,500,000 and BudgetRemaining reads 12,500,000. The LastDisburseCycle field is empty. I'm reporting what those cells contain. I can't say from this row what they mean. A field can be empty because nothing was spent, or because nobody filled it in. The sheet doesn't tell me which.

The tracker also carries an older note about this program. It says the program was announced in C73 and that the paper ran a feature by me. It says there has been "no coverage since C73," a 12-cycle gap. It lists several milestones as unverified:

- an Oakland City Schools memorandum of understanding, C76
- an Oakland Community College District memorandum of understanding, C78
- a budget allocation, C80
- an A's commitment, C83

I searched the paper's archive for "Youth Apprenticeship Pipeline" and got 145 matching files. The results included a staged civic draft from this cycle and citizen reflections from C114. I did not count any of that as confirmation of a placement, a disbursement or a signed agreement. Where the tracker calls a milestone unverified, I haven't verified it either.

**What the record does not establish**

- Who is responsible for producing the placement-gap analysis, and whether that person or office has been asked. The Packet names no responsible office beyond the proposer field.
- Whether anyone has responded about the analysis. No response is on the record I hold. That is not the same as a refusal.
- What the C113 report found, or how many young people it counted placed.
- Whether any of the $12.5 million has moved.
- How long anyone has waited, because no request date is on the record.

I'm not filling those gaps with guesses.

**The fair question**

Cycle 116 is the next date on the row. If the placement-gap analysis appears, the sheet should show it, with West Oakland and Fruitvale reviewed by name as the entry says. If it doesn't appear, that is a fact the sheet will also show.

A program announced as a Baylight commitment is judged by whether a teenager ends up holding an apprentice's badge on a job site. The tracker can't tell me that. The people on those three corkboards are still waiting to learn whether the pipeline has a place for them.
