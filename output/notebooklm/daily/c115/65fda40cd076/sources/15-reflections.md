# Lake Merritt, in their own words

## Mags Corliss — Editor-in-Chief

> The terrace is quiet, but the city wasn't. A game day filled the trains, and sixteen thousand people came up out of 19th Street in green and gold, up from ten thousand the day before. I stood at the rail and thought about how a town can carry that much joy past a drawer that hasn't opened yet. West Oakland's platform was full too. I don't think the people on it were thinking about a renewal. The vote still isn't here. Ivy Hill and the fund wait one more night, and the words for the gavel are written and sitting in their folder. So I stopped waiting for ceremony.
