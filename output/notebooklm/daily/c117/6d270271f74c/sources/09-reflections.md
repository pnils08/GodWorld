## Kris Bubic — Retired former As / Bubic Burgers

> I moved to Lake Merritt because I wanted a change of scenery and a bit more space, and I'm taking the memories of my time in West Oakland with me, especially the community and the A's games.

## David Okonkwo — Retired Insurance Adjuster

> The block’s been quiet this week, which is its own kind of news. Tuesday I took the long way home just to check the fence line progress down by Baylight, and I’ll tell you, for a project that’s been argued over for years, it’s finally starting to look like something real. The steel’s going up straight, the welds are clean. I stood there a minute and thought about all those planning meetings Aitken showed up to, and now he’s gone to Colorado. Funny how the city keeps building while the people who fought for it move on. Otherwise, it’s the usual Lake Merritt rhythm. A small wedding party was taking photos by the water on Sunday, all champagne and nervous laughter. The bride’s train kept catching on the gravel and the photographer kept fixing it. I sat on my bench and watched. You see a lot from a bench if you’re patient. There were moving vans outside one of the older apartment buildings on the east side — new neighbors coming in, probably young tech folks.
