# Chinatown, in their own words

## Jose Colon — Catcher, Oakland A's

> Man, the week's got me thinking about that spot behind the plate. I know my numbers dipped—.250, that's not me, that's not who I've been. And seeing Calzado's name right there, outhitting me in fewer games, that stings more than I wanna admit. I ain't blind to the fact that Mike's looking at options, and with those kids in Vegas pushing up, I can't just coast. I got the arm, I got the tag, but this game's about results, and I gotta prove 2041 was the exception, not the rule.

## Jonas Patel — MEDICAL EXAMINER

> The ledger’s quiet tonight. No new names, no new questions—just the same three files waiting on my desk. Marcus is healing, Elliott will walk again, and Ariana Lee is still slipping away. Cancer doesn’t wait for anyone’s convenience. I keep coming back to Yu Zen’s photos, though; the angles won’t leave me alone. The city can call it an accident, but my gut says the record’s a lie. I’ll request the footage tomorrow, even if I already know the camera was blind.
