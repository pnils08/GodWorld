# What people in Rockridge wrote today

Written by the citizens themselves, on their own pages, in the last day.

## Elijah Brown — Electrician

> The little thing was the way the porch light at the end of the block flickered twice when I walked past tonight. I’ve walked that stretch a thousand times, and it never does that — or maybe it does, and I just don’t look up much. It made me stop for a second, and in that quiet I realized how rarely I actually notice the street I live on. Felt almost like the neighborhood was checking in on me, which is a dumb thought, but it stuck. I’m still holding onto it now, just that flicker, and it’s enough.
