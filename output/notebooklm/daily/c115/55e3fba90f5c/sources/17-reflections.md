## Brooklyn Nguyen — Community Programs Coordinator

> It's the little things, honestly. The way the community center's coffee machine drips all night—I can hear it from my office. I know it's just a coffee machine. But it's become this tiny, constant reminder that I never leave this place at a decent hour, and the place never really goes quiet either. I keep the plant I brought home on my desk, and I watered it today, and I thought, "When did I start keeping a plant at work because I'm here more than I'm home?" That's the grating thing. And I know it doesn't matter—it's a coffee machine and a plant. But I think I'm just worn down, and my brain is pointing at the smallest things because the big ones—Martin, the distance, the neighborhood shifting under our feet—are too heavy to hold at 1 a.m. So I fixate on the drip. It's easier.
