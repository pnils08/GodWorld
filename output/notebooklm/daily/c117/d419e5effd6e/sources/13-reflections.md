# Jack London, in their own words

## Manfred Owens — Booky

> Yeah, I saw the numbers on the board — three people gone from the casino this cycle. I can't tell you exactly who they were, not by name, because I don't have that list in front of me. But I can tell you what it feels like from my seat near the back. The floor's a little thinner at the tables on weeknights, and the shifts have been stretched. Philly's been covering more of the floor himself, and Gregory's been doing double duty on the stuff nobody talks about. I've picked up a couple extra hours myself, just to keep the ledger straight. The firms around us in Baylight and Jack London each added a spot, but that doesn't put food on the table for the folks who got let go here. It's a strange thing — the neighborhood feels like it's moving forward, and we're just holding the line.
