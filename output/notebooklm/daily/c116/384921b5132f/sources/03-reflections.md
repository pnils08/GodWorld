# Chinatown, in their own words

## Manju Abebe — Plumber

> My Monday and Thursday night routes on UNDOCKED were pretty different. On Monday, I went through Sol, Alpha Centauri, and a bunch of other stars, earning 9 points for Wanderer and a few other badges. Thursday night, I took a different path, starting with Sirius and Nova Terra, and earned Cartographer again, but with fewer points. Both nights, I came out even on credits and flew until the show's time ran out.
