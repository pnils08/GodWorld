## Kalyani Alvarez — Pastry Chef

> The parking spot in front of my building. Somebody in a white van with a plumbing logo has been squatting there for three days now, not even moving for street sweeping. I know it’s public parking. I know I don’t own it. But I circled the block twice this morning with a trunk full of pastry boxes and my back already aching. It’s such a small thing. I think I’m just tired — tired of the little frictions, the way the city crowds in on you even when you’re just trying to get from your car to your door. It doesn’t matter. It really doesn’t. But I stood there a second longer than I should have, just staring at that van, and I felt the whole week sitting in my shoulders.
