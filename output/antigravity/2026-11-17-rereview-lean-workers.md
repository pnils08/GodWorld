### B. Edge-Case Findings in `readRecord` and `capLines`

- **Finding 2.1 ([workerWake.js:47](file:///root/GodWorld/scripts/workerWake.js#L47), [:674-678](file:///root/GodWorld/scripts/workerWake.js#L674-L678), [:685-687](file:///root/GodWorld/scripts/workerWake.js#L685-L687), [:691](file:///root/GodWorld/scripts/workerWake.js#L691)) — A table cell holding a newline (`\n`) is split by `text.split('\n')` in `readRecord`:**
  `table(rows, cols)` ([workerWake.js:47](file:///root/GodWorld/scripts/workerWake.js#L47)) joins cells with `String(r[c] == null ? '' : r[c])` without replacing `\n`. When `readRecord` splits `text` on `\n` ([workerWake.js:674](file:///root/GodWorld/scripts/workerWake.js#L674)) and classifies lines by `const isRow = (l) => l.includes(' | ');` ([:675](file:///root/GodWorld/scripts/workerWake.js#L675)):
  1. **Newline in the last column of a row** (e.g. `Summary`, `Event`, `Detail`, `Note`, `Factors`): the continuation line after `\n` has no `' | '`, so `isRow(l)` is `false`. `readRecord` puts the continuation line into `notes` ([:677](file:///root/GodWorld/scripts/workerWake.js#L677)), detaching it from its row and printing it at the very end of the table (`tail` at [:691, 696](file:///root/GodWorld/scripts/workerWake.js#L691)) — or dropping it completely if `rows.length === 0` ([:690](file:///root/GodWorld/scripts/workerWake.js#L690)).
  2. **Newline in any non-final column of a row:** the second half of the row still contains `' | '` (`isRow(l) === true`), so `readRecord` treats one row as two separate rows (inflating `total`). On a `byCycle` table, the second fragment's first cell is not a Cycle number (`cyc(r)` is `NaN`), so `ranged` queries (`isFinite(c)` at [:685](file:///root/GodWorld/scripts/workerWake.js#L685)) drop the second half of the row, and un-ranged `byCycle` queries (`(cyc(b[0]) || 0)` at [:687](file:///root/GodWorld/scripts/workerWake.js#L687)) sort the second half of the row to Cycle `0` at the bottom of the table.
- **Finding 2.2 ([workerWake.js:676](file:///root/GodWorld/scripts/workerWake.js#L676)) — Non-table records (`!isRow(lines[0])`, including `Your initiative on the city's tracker (...)`) ignore `match`, `from_cycle`, and `to_cycle`:**
  When `!isRow(lines[0])` is true (all 5 `k.initiative(id)` records and any record whose text is `'none'`), line 676 returns `capLines(title + ':\n' + text, RECORD_CAP)` immediately before checking `input.match`, `input.from_cycle`, or `input.to_cycle`. Calling `record_read({ record: "Your initiative on the city's tracker (INIT-001)", match: "Milestone" })` returns the entire unfiltered 59-line block instead of lines matching `"Milestone"`.
- **Finding 2.3 ([workerWake.js:675-681](file:///root/GodWorld/scripts/workerWake.js#L675-L681)) — Any non-table record whose first line contains `' | '` is misclassified as a table:**
  Because `readRecord` detects tables solely via `lines[0].includes(' | ')` ([:675-676](file:///root/GodWorld/scripts/workerWake.js#L675-L676)), a non-table record whose first line happens to contain `' | '` (and even more so if its first token before `' | '` is `Cycle` / `cycle`, e.g., `cycle: 116 | ...`) has `lines[0]` treated as `head`, any subsequent lines without `' | '` swept into `notes`, and `rows` left empty (`total = 0`), returning `<title>:\nno row (0 rows on the record)` and discarding the text in `notes`.
