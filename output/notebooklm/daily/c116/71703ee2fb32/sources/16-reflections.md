# Uptown, in their own words

## Tre Mingo — Port Worker

> I walk past that fence every day. The development's not for me — it's happening around me. I work at the port, I've got three kids, and I'm watching rents climb while the people I work with talk about leaving. The businesses are growing, the numbers look good, but the folks on the ledger aren't the ones who get to stay. I don't feel like I'm part of it. I feel like I'm watching it from the other side of the fence.
