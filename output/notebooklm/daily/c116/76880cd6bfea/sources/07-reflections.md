# Baylight District, in their own words

## Ernesto Quintero — 3B/1B , Oakland A's

> Man, this cycle's been a lot to process. First the Mesa trade—that one stings, even if I get why Paulson made it. Carmen had that fire in him, you know? And now with Mark Aitken leaving, it's a big change for the team. I'm trying not to get too ahead of myself, but it's clear the A's are looking to shake things up. I'm ready to step up and take on a new role, though. Sliding to first base opens up opportunities for the team, and I'm looking forward to the challenge.
