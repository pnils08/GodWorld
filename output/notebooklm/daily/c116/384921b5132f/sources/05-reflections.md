# Fruitvale, in their own words

## Tamsin Pruitt — Journalist, Lives

## Rose Delgado — City Council District 3

> Honest? The ambition is real, but so is the weight. Every time I walk past that empty lot on International, I feel the clock ticking — not mine, the neighborhood's. Fruitvale's holding, but I see the cracks in the retail numbers, the fatigue in the San Antonio sentiment, and I know the next vote on that $230M design has to be the one that actually delivers, not just clears a hurdle. Underneath the magnetism, there's this constant hum of "what if I'm the one who lets them down?" I've been in this seat long enough to know that goodwill runs out faster than funding, and I'm not interested in being the councilmember who talked a big game and handed back a placeholder. So I keep pushing, keep showing up, keep reading the flyers and the renderings, because the day I stop believing this can be done right is the day I should hand over the keys.
