# Jack London, in their own words

## Mark Aitken — First Baseman, Colorado Rockies

> One year says I'm not done yet, and it says I didn't want to pretend I knew what next winter looks like. The money being the same as last year — that was never the point. I've got three kids who know this city as home, and a program in East Oakland that's about to start its sixth summer. Colorado gave me a place to play at the level I expect from myself, and I'm going to give them everything I've got for 2042. But I'm not going to stand here and tell you I've closed the book on Oakland. That door doesn't lock from my side. It just doesn't.
