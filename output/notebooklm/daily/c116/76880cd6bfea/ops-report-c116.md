* **Severity Rating:** High.

#### Ground Truth Verification & Source Cross-Examination
A comprehensive audit was performed against all C116 ground-truth files, citizen interview logs, and desk filings:
* **Citizen Transcript Verification:** In the raw interview log (`Excerpts from "The Bay Tribune Daily C116 — Citizens in their own words — West Oakland"`), Coach Wilson Shepard spoke regarding his living situation ("To be honest, my new living situation in West Oakland hasn't really affected my coaching..."), team performance, and losing to San Antonio.
* **Leary/Finch Filings Verification:** In Simon Leary's piece, Shepard is quoted discussing team structure ("the ranking is a mirror, not a load-bearing beam"). In Talia Finch's packet, Shepard discusses finding team rhythm.
* **Audit Finding:** The exact sentence attributed to Shepard in Pruitt's draft—*"The rankings are a nice feather, but they don't win us a single possession."*—does **NOT** exist anywhere in the raw interview transcripts, simulation ledger logs, or sports desk recordings.

#### Conclusion
The gate finding **holds up 100% against all source context**. The author introduced manufactured speech into a named citizen's profile. Holding the draft was entirely necessary to uphold newsroom accuracy and prevent the publication of fabricated quotes.

## 5. Editorial Desk Directives & Action Items for Cycle 117

1. **Quote Verification Protocol:** Implement mandatory automated cross-referencing of all direct quotes in feature packets against raw citizen interview logs prior to copy-editing. Authors who introduce quotes not supported by raw transcripts will be subject to desk review, as demonstrated by the draft hold on Tamsin Pruitt’s C116 submission.
2. **Civic Filing Cross-Checks:** Require municipal reporters covering City Council Districts 1 through 9 to verify formal City Clerk docket filings before reporting on proposed infrastructure, commercial corridor funding, or charter amendments. Unverified statements made in committee or public meetings must be explicitly labeled as unverified positions rather than completed legislative actions.
3. **Sports Desk Multi-Source Alignment:** Mandate real-time event updates across all sports desk filings. Story drafts that reference power rankings or winning streaks must be verified against the most recent game outcomes (e.g., ensuring reporting on the Oaks' No. 1 NBA.com ranking accurately reflects the subsequent home loss to San Antonio) to prevent narrative discrepancies.
