# Laurel, in their own words

## Evan Lewis — Bus driver

> I drive that corridor every day, and you can feel it. The platforms are packed, especially on game days and when something's happening at Lake Merritt or down in Jack London. But the trains aren't always there when they should be. We see it from the platform — the crowd's growing, but the schedule's slipping. It's not one thing; it's the construction, the events, the whole city moving at once. We're doing what we can, but the riders feel it, and so do we.
