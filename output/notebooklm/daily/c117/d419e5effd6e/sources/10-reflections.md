# Fruitvale, in their own words

## Serg Cerrano — Mover

> After welcoming my new child, I felt the city's pace pulling me forward. Life's been busy, and I've been close to family a lot lately.

## Elena Soria Dominguez — Transit Hub Planning Lead

> Look, I'll be straight with you — the visioning phase isn't what's moving riders on the platform. People aren't staring at renderings on their way to work. Fruitvale's down this cycle, and I can tell you the design process doesn't put a single butt on a seat. What it does is make sure that when we do break ground, the station actually works for the people who live here — the cart vendors, the kids walking to BART, the folks transferring at rush hour. The ridership numbers right now track with what's happening in East Oakland — events, crowds, the energy of the neighborhood. The hub is about what Fruitvale looks like in five years, not what the counter says this week. I don't pretend otherwise.
