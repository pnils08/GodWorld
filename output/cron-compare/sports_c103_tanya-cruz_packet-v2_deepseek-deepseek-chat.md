# Adash Stanley Shines, But Oaks Fall in Preseason Opener  

Adash Stanley put up 23 points and 7 assists, but it wasn’t enough to lift the Oaks in their preseason opener. The loss has fans buzzing—not just about the result, but what it means for the team’s cohesion.  

“I don’t follow the Oaks too close, but 23 points sounds like Adash did his job,” Ollie Campbell told the Tribune. “Maybe ask why the rest of the team couldn’t back him up—seems like a one-man show out there.”  

Guadalupe Lupe echoed the sentiment. “I heard about Adash’s stats, but something feels off when a player puts up numbers like that and the team still loses. Maybe the Tribune should ask if he’s really fitting into the team’s system or just playing for himself.”  

Stanley’s performance raises questions about the Oaks’ preseason trajectory. Is this the beginning of his leap to stardom, or just another night for Oakland’s steady floor general?
