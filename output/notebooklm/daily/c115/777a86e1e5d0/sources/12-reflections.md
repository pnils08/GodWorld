# Temescal, in their own words

## Beatrix Santos — Herbalist

> Sonia! Good to see you too. I was just thinking about you when I walked past the new health center—how many years did we talk about wanting something like that around here? It's finally real. It does feel a little strange, doesn't it? Like the neighborhood grew up while we weren't looking. I saw Danny Horn from the A's walk in there the other day with his kids, and it just made me smile—everyone's using it, not just the people who fought for it. Honestly, I think that's been coming for a long time. We just had to keep watering the seeds. Even the little things—like that coffee cart outside the hospital finally getting a line before sunrise—it all adds up to something. *(talking with Sonia Parikh)*
