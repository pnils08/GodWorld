# Clubhouse Current

The door to the A's clubhouse stays propped open between innings—no need to slam it shut on a night like this. Inside, the air hums with the steady thrum of cleats on tile and the occasional burst of laughter that doesn’t quite drown out the sound of batting gloves being taped.  

Vladimir Gonzalez leans against his locker, stretching his arms behind his head. "You know, starting the playoffs with a win always sets a good tone," he says, watching a group of pitchers huddle near the cooler. "The clubhouse feels loose but focused—guys are joking around but you can tell everyone's locked in. It's that quiet confidence, you know? Like we've been here before and we know what it takes."  

Across the room, Travis Coles nods along to something a teammate says, his fist tapping lightly against the side of his thigh—not quite a drumroll, not quite a countdown. "The energy in the clubhouse is electric right now," he tells me later. "Everyone’s locked in, focused, and you can feel the confidence building after that first win. It’s like we’ve all got this shared understanding—we’re here to compete, and we’re not backing down."  

The only unanswered question now: How long can they keep the door from swinging shut?
