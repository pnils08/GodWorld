# codex review — engine.214 Mood Per Hood plan

**Target:** `docs/plans/2026-10-10-engine-214-mood-per-hood.md`, D1–D12, Tasks, Acceptance and Needs proof; parent §engine.214. Seven hunts in the requested order.

**Result:** HOLD

The cluster removal and equal-hood rule are supportable. The plan still needs decisions on duplicate initiative sentiment, seed gates, effect ordering and the cut-over estimate. These are build-contract gaps, not reasons to retain clusters.

Evidence: checkout `05b2578dcfe4ea74daec165a09ad72dc2e860b66`; the dynamics source has no diff from the plan's `d794209b` reference. Read-only source inspection, local JSON arithmetic and isolated in-memory helper probes only. `node scripts/sentimentRestingLevel.test.js`: **31 passed, 0 failed** on the existing implementation. No bench, deployment or live-Sheet validation was performed. The adjacency snapshot used below is **C107**, paired with C110 carried sentiments; it is not a C110 graph readback.

## Findings

1. **Hunt 1 — no missed production cluster-output reader found; the absolute “no reader outside” wording excludes real test readers. — INFO.** Searches across current engine, utilities, lib and scripts found no production consumer of `getClusterDynamics_`, `clusterDynamics`, `clusterDefinitions`, `previousClusterDynamics`, `storySeedSignals.byCluster` or `byDomainCluster` outside the producer. The three production `clusterDefinitions` mentions are comments (`phase03-population/updateCrimeMetrics.js:13`, `:1212`; `phase07-evening-media/applyStorySeeds.js:104`). However, `scripts/hoodEconomyRelative.test.js:85` reads `S.clusterDynamics` and dereferences its waterfront economy at `:105`; `scripts/hoodBlindTables.test.js:250` reads `clusterDefinitions` and `:271` reads `clusterDynamics`. Thus D11's claim (`docs/plans/2026-10-10-engine-214-mood-per-hood.md:48`) is sound for production callers, not for all external readers. Hunt 7 below identifies the incomplete test migration. No production-reader expansion is required by this search.

2. **Hunt 2 — D7 + D10 count initiative sentiment through both the hood mean and the existing city boost. — HIGH.** The same initiative produces local sentiment at `phase02-world-state/applyInitiativeImplementationEffects.js:704` and a half-strength city contribution at `:713`. Today the local bus lands after hood momentum (`phase02-world-state/applyCityDynamics.js:1493`), while the city aggregates separate cluster objects (`:1639`, `:1654`) and then adds `initiativeImplementationEffects.sentimentBoost` (`:1812`). D7 retains the local fold and D10/Task 8 retain the city boost after averaging the resulting hoods (`docs/plans/2026-10-10-engine-214-mood-per-hood.md:40`, `:46`, `:63`). That adds a second city path for the same cause.

   Isolated arithmetic, with unchanged prior carriers, no clamp and ordinary city momentum 0.50: one initiative contributing +0.06 to each of k target hoods already contributes +0.03 citywide. The proposed mean adds another `0.50 × k × 0.06 / 22`; k=22 yields +0.06 total instead of +0.03. The new aggregate contribution also enters `preBoostCity` (`applyCityDynamics.js:1695`) and its next-Cycle carrier (`:1890`), unlike the deliberately excluded direct boost. **Fix:** specify which city path owns this contribution, and how its carrier behaves; test a targeted initiative and its removal over successive Cycles. Demographic/economy micro-block removal is explicit in D7, and D4 explicitly elects to retain forecast plus realised fog; neither is an additional finding here.

3. **Hunt 2 — P2 misses the zero-median failure and a sentiment-moving absolute domain gate. — MEDIUM.** `seedClusterMedian_` includes zero totals (`phase02-world-state/applyCityDynamics.js:959`); `seedDomainMedian_` includes absent domains as zero (`:968`). When the median is zero, the total, COMMUNITY and BUSINESS ratios become **1**, regardless of the active hood's weight (`:988`, `:1018`, `:1022`). Rekeying these to all 22 hoods makes sparse domains especially vulnerable. An in-memory probe of these actual helpers, using clearly synthetic keys, gave one entry weight 20 in total/COMMUNITY/BUSINESS and 21 zero entries: median 0, sentiment 0, every multiplier 1. Renaming the keys cannot cure that behavior. Conversely, calculating medians only over active entries changes the comparison population; that choice also needs a rule.

   P2 currently checks only CULTURE/NIGHTLIFE ≥6/≥3 and proposes a hood-median fallback (`docs/plans/2026-10-10-engine-214-mood-per-hood.md:101`). It omits CIVIC+SAFETY ≥6/≥3, which subtracts sentiment (`applyCityDynamics.js:1011`, `:1029`). D7 also leaves the fate of the existing local ≥6/≥3 seed lift (`:1415`) unspecified when the former cluster total lift becomes another hood-total lift (`:977`). **Fix:** cover total, COMMUNITY, BUSINESS and CIVIC+SAFETY as well as the two activity domains; define empty/sparse-domain behavior and explicitly retain or remove the second total-weight ladder. A no-seed or no-domain bench alone cannot establish equivalence.

4. **Hunt 2 — P1's “loses nothing” premise is false in source; branch counts cannot prove it. — MEDIUM.** The retiring crime ripple changes tourism at ≥2, and public spaces at ≥3 (`phase02-world-state/applyCityDynamics.js:1051`, `:1056`). The retained hood block only changes nightlife and sentiment (`:1451`). For an isolated three-spike input in one hood, the old ripple contributes tourism ×0.88 and public spaces ×0.88; the retained block contributes neither, even though the hood still registers a crime-branch hit. The removed function also emits the crime attribution receipt at `:1074`; the retained block does not.

   **Fix:** replace the branch-count-only proof in `docs/plans/2026-10-10-engine-214-mood-per-hood.md:100` with per-metric and receipt comparisons for 1/2/3 spikes, including spikes split among neighboring hoods. Explicitly choose whether those lost effects/receipts are retired or receive a per-hood home. This does not require keeping the cluster spillover. C110's carried `crimeSpikes` is empty (`output/carry_forward_c110.json:15`), so that snapshot cannot settle P1.

5. **Hunt 3 — the existing pass reads live Sentiment as a first-carry fallback; D1 needs that precise exception. — LOW.** `phase02-world-state/applyCityDynamics.js:1477` reads `S.neighborhoodState[nhood].sentiment` when no carried hood object exists, and `:1483` blends it at 0.30. The loader reads the live `Sentiment` header at `phase02-world-state/loadNeighborhoodState.js:72` and populates that field at `:121`. This is column I of the writer-owned A–O block (`phase08-v3-chicago/v3NeighborhoodWriter.js:28`, `:49`). No other direct A–O read was found in the dynamics source. D1's narrow “never as a base” rule can coexist with this fallback; its broader explanation that reading these columns as input is forbidden cannot, if D7's momentum is unchanged (`docs/plans/2026-10-10-engine-214-mood-per-hood.md:28`, `:40`). **Fix:** explicitly preserve or retire this bootstrap exception and test missing carry separately from normal carry. Do not accidentally substitute NightlifeProfile/RetailVitality for authored base multipliers.

6. **Hunt 4 — equal-22 is defensible, but the −0.013 cut-over promise does not follow from the cited data or current aggregation stages. — HIGH.** Parsing `PREV_CYCLE_STATE_JSON.json.neighborhoodDynamics` in `output/carry_forward_c110.json:15` gives the exact mean **0.3689426459272726**. Those values are already hood-momentum/fold outputs. The old raw city averages cluster values after capacity friction (`phase02-world-state/applyCityDynamics.js:1606`, `:1639`); it never averages these final hood values. The snapshot contains no corresponding raw cluster objects. Weighting the C110 means of the original anchor groups gives **0.39143671053**; weighting means of all adopted members using the available C107 graph gives **0.37969875090**. Neither reconstructs the old raw city or establishes the quoted 0.382.

   There is also a changed temporal response: D7 retains hood momentum 0.30 and D10 retains city momentum 0.50. Away from clamps, a uniform new sentiment input u therefore reaches the new city immediately as `0.70 × 0.50 × u = 0.35u`; the old city receives `0.50u` directly from its cluster calculation (`applyCityDynamics.js:150`, `:160`, `:1483`, `:1684`). Capacity now reaches the hood values, too, whereas the old hood copies precede the cluster-only friction. Changed character, crime, seed and calendar rules introduce further differences. Even an isolated −0.013 raw-aggregate change becomes −0.0065 after ordinary city momentum, before rounding.

   **Fix:** keep equal-hood weighting if desired, but define which processing stage feeds it, acknowledge the extra hood smoothing, reconcile finding 2, and derive the full first-Cycle and subsequent-Cycle step from paired runs. Separate a raw-mean diagnostic from the final `CitySentiment` acceptance band. D10's “about that much and no more” and Acceptance 5 (`docs/plans/2026-10-10-engine-214-mood-per-hood.md:46`, `:76`) currently promise a bound without the necessary evidence. The C110 values do not establish that population or sample weighting would be a better rule.

7. **Hunt 5 — 0.12 passes the available snapshot calculation; the actionable gap is bleed placement relative to the political fold. — MEDIUM.** C110 sentiments range from Dimond **0.30174230** to West Oakland **0.53173125**, spread **0.22998895** (`output/carry_forward_c110.json:15`). I loaded the C107 map snapshot (`output/engine-sheet/2026-09-19-neighborhood-map-live-pre-recalibration.json:1`) through the actual canon loader, including its symmetric adjacency construction (`phase01-config/canonNeighborhoodLoader.js:155`), then applied one simultaneous `s + 0.12 × (neighborMean − s)` pass. Result: minimum **0.30714975**, maximum **0.51691435**, spread **0.20976460**; mean **0.36907627**. This exceeds Acceptance 4's 0.18 floor. It is a conditional snapshot calculation, not the rebuilt engine's C112 proof.

   Node count alone does not determine smoothing: the neighbor average is normalized. For one pass, a graph-independent lower bound is `(1 − 2×0.12) × range`, here **0.17479161**, so neither automatic failure nor a guaranteed 0.18 floor follows merely from 22 nodes. D8's “22 nodes smooth harder than 5” should be replaced with the measured graph/input result (`docs/plans/2026-10-10-engine-214-mood-per-hood.md:42`).

   The missing ordering decision matters more: the current code explicitly protects initiative deltas by placing their fold **after bleed** (`applyCityDynamics.js:1300`). D8 says post-momentum/pre-clamp, while Task 5 lists momentum → fold → commute → clamp without locating bleed (`plan:60`). Appending the bleed pass after that loop would reduce a lone target's fresh delta d to 0.88d and export part of it to neighbors. **Fix:** specify a simultaneous all-hood bleed stage before the initiative/approval fold, or explicitly authorize changed propagation. Also locate capacity before the final clamp/aggregate; Tasks 5–7 presently leave its position implicit.

8. **Hunts 4–5 — Acceptance 2's zero-spread C110 baseline is factually wrong. — LOW.** `docs/plans/2026-10-10-engine-214-mood-per-hood.md:73` says nightlife and tourism spread is 0.00 in all five former clusters. From `output/carry_forward_c110.json:15`, Downtown nightlife is **1.87611420** versus Uptown/KONO/Chinatown **1.82044928**, already a **0.05566492** spread. West Oakland versus Jack London nightlife differs by **0.01559208** even before adopted members are included. Downtown/Chinatown tourism differs by **0.00711711**; Rockridge/Temescal by **0.00618477**. These are not all zero even at two decimals. **Fix:** retain the independent authored-character test, but correct the measured baseline and compare each metric against its actual before value. The >0.05 nightlife target is already met in Downtown, so that threshold alone cannot demonstrate the cut's effect.

9. **Hunt 6 — no new hood-name table is specified, but several old named branches have no replacement rule. — MEDIUM.** D2's EmployerCharacter table is openly declared, and D4/D5 key their stated selectors by sheet vocabulary. The plan's named hoods are examples, not an additional proposed membership table. However, “fold `applyLocalPlaceBias_` into the table” (`docs/plans/2026-10-10-engine-214-mood-per-hood.md:30`) cannot absorb its conditional wind ≥25, rain ≥0.4 and Winter effects into static coefficients (`phase02-world-state/applyCityDynamics.js:1110`, `:1113`, `:1116`). Task 5 retains lag drags without replacing waterfront tourism +0.04 and downtown nightlife +0.03 (`:1224`). D5 replaces the three seed predicates, but leaves the explicit EAST_OAKLAND Creation Day +0.08 and DOWNTOWN_CORE major-holiday +0.22 branches (`:844`, `:849`), and the downtown default used to weight citywide/unassigned seeds (`:931`, `:939`), without a stated non-cluster rule.

   **Fix:** list a sheet-label/scene/zone selector or an intentional deletion for each residual branch; define citywide seed weighting separately. Otherwise the builder must either recreate hidden membership assumptions or silently remove behavior. Include `neighborhoodToCluster_`, `byCluster` and `seedClusterMedian_` in the retirement/rekey inventory (`applyCityDynamics.js:880`, `:895`, `:959`), not just `byDomainCluster`.

10. **Hunt 7 — D11 misses one harness and understates two named test migrations. — MEDIUM.** `scripts/sentimentRestingLevel.test.js` is absent from D11 (`docs/plans/2026-10-10-engine-214-mood-per-hood.md:48`). It extracts `seedClusterMedian_` (`scripts/sentimentRestingLevel.test.js:36`), creates `CLUSTERS`, a neighborhood-to-cluster helper and `hoodClusters` (`:42`), and supplies `byCluster`/`byDomainCluster` (`:85`). Its sports fixture still uses a cluster argument (`:163`). These must migrate while preserving its resting-level and no-boost-echo assertions; leaving it unchanged violates Acceptance 6 and can fail helper extraction after retirement.

    The named `hoodEconomyRelative.test.js` needs more than removing config seeds: it dereferences `S.clusterDynamics` at `:105` and `:109`. Removing only engine94SheetContract's B2 test block also leaves top-level exports of retired symbols at `scripts/engine94SheetContract.test.js:18`. The other seed consumers are correctly identified: `scripts/hoodBlindTables.test.js:43`, `scripts/hoodEconomyRelative.test.js:58`, `scripts/activityObservationsCarry.test.js:40`; the live seeder and caller are `phase01-config/engine94SheetContract.js:312` and `phase01-config/godWorldEngine2.js:507`. **Fix:** enumerate these assertion/export changes and the missed resting-level harness in Task 9, then run those suites alongside the new per-hood suite. Existing 31/31 resting-level results establish a baseline, not acceptance of the proposed cut.
