# Jack London, in their own words

## Mark Aitken — First Baseman, Colorado Rockies

> The Rockies. Yeah. I've been sitting with that one since the ink dried. Look, I know how it reads from the outside. Oakland's been home for a decade, the Angels made the push everyone expected, and then Colorado comes in and steals the whole thing with a one-year deal that doesn't even beat what I made last year. I get why people are scratching their heads. But here's the thing nobody in the comments section is thinking about: I'm thirty-four, I've got three kids, and I've got one more chance to do something that matters on my own terms. Colorado's got a young core that's hungry, a park that fits my swing, and they didn't try to sell me on some five-year rebuild or a legacy pitch. They just said: come play first base, hit the ball hard, and let's see what happens. One year. That's the honest number. Because I don't know how many more I've got in me, and neither do they. That's the deal we both signed. The A's... yeah. It stings a little. But I've been around long enough to know that loyalty in this game runs one way — toward the next at-bat.

## Mariano Rosales — Closing Pitcher, Oakland As

> *(Mariano glances up from his phone, a slight smirk on his face as he reads the feed over a post-workout protein shake in the clubhouse tunnel)* "Yeah, that's the headline they'll remember. Five years, eighty-eight million, no opt-outs. They wanted to lock me down, and I wanted to stay. Simple as that." *(He scrolls back a bit, reading the part about the World Series)* "Game 2, huh? They always gotta dig up the one bad inning. I gave up a three-run shot to Valdez in the sixth—only mistake I made all postseason. We still won the series in five. That's what matters. The saves, the ERA, the All-Star nod—those are just numbers. The ring is forever." *(He sets the phone down, leaning back against the locker)* "Edmundo Pena's shadow? I don't think about it anymore. C39 feels like a lifetime ago. The fans in Oakland—they've seen me grow up right in front of them. They know what I am now. I'm not the next Pena. I'm just Mariano, and I'm here to win more of these."
