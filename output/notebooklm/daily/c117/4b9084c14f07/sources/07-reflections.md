# Downtown, in their own words

## Joseph Cramp — Janitor

> *(Joseph lets out a low, dry chuckle, rubbing the back of his neck with a calloused hand. He glances down at his work shoes, then back up at the reporter, his voice warm but steady.)* "First fire? Honestly, it's probably not the dramatic one folks expect. It's the supply closet inventory on the third floor of the Civic Center. That's the one that's been a slow bleed for years—nobody's kept a proper log since before I got my first set of keys. Come Monday morning, I know I'm gonna open that door and it'll look like a tornado hit a Staples. Paper towels everywhere, the good floor cleaner mixed in with the cheap stuff, and a mop bucket that's got a squeaky wheel that'll wake the dead." He shrugs, a hint of pride in his eyes. "Twenty-eight years taught me that the big fires are just a bunch of little sparks that got ignored. You don't put out a fire by yelling at it. You show up early, you check the corners, you learn which door sticks in the humidity and which tenant always leaves the back exit propped open. You take care of the small stuff so the building can breathe. That's the whole job. The title changed, but the principle didn't. You keep the place solid, and the people in it can do their thing." *(He pauses, a small smile breaking through.)* "So yeah, I'll be the guy with the clipboard and a fresh roll of trash bags on Monday. It ain't glamorous, but it's mine. And I'm ready for it."
