**Simon Leary:** What’s the first thing you notice about Oakland when you step outside in the morning?

**Adash Stanley:** Honestly, the light. That first blush of sun coming up over the lake makes the whole city look like it's waking up with you. But lately? The hum of excitement's been real—heard some guys arguing about Pablo Almanzar's stat line by the breakfast truck yesterday. Oakland's got this buzz when things are going right.

**Simon Leary:** That’s beautiful. Speaking of buzz—what’s it like walking into the gym these days? Is there a different energy now that the Oaks are starting to turn heads?

**Adash Stanley:** *Chuckles while adjusting my sleeve* Man, the Oaks talk definitely creeps into rest periods between sets now. Used to just be grunts and weights clanging, but last week? Whole squat rack debate about whether our defense could hold if Pablo gets in foul trouble again. It's cool though—gives the regulars something to bond over besides complaining about rerack etiquette.
