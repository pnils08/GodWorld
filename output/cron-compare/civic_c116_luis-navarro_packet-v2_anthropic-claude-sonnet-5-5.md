# Four Cycles, One Stage: The Apprenticeship Pipeline's Gap Analysis Is Due Next Cycle

Fruitvale, West Oakland and East Oakland are the three neighborhoods the tracker lists for the Oakland Youth Apprenticeship Pipeline. The row for that program is where I started this cycle. It is also where the program has been stuck.

Here is what we know.

The tracker's C116 entry records the Pipeline as stalled. It is waiting on the publication of a placement-gap analysis and program adjustment plan, with explicit West Oakland and Fruitvale review. The row's history runs in order:

- C107: a public town hall enrollment event, with applicant intake operational.
- C111: the first cohort placement report mandated for Cycle 112.
- C113: the first cohort placement report published, with an accountability review directed.
- C114 and C115: the gap analysis directed, then required again.
- C116: stalled.

The row's next scheduled action is "Publish placement-gap analysis and program adjustment plan," set for Cycle 117. The economic initiative carrying West Oakland has not advanced in four cycles.

Councilmember Rose Delgado, who represents Fruitvale on the D3 seat, has put that span into her office's record this cycle. "A seat in the classroom is not a paycheck," she said. "Four cycles in one stage — publish the gap analysis and show Fruitvale's young people in the roles."

Her sentence names the distinction the paper has been circling since C101. That cycle's count was 86 applied and 31 placed. Applying is not placement, and a classroom seat is not a role on a site.

Here is what I checked and found.

The tracker row lists the budget as $12.5M. It lists the total at 12,500,000 and the remaining balance at 12,500,000. The row's last-disbursement field is blank. I can't tell you from the row what that pairing means. Money may be committed but unspent, or the fields may not have been updated. The row doesn't say, and I'm not going to guess.

I also found a mismatch in the record. The row's note says the program was "announced C73 as part of Baylight CBA package. Not a standalone council vote." The paper's C98 coverage by Carmen Delaine carried the headline "Apprenticeship Pipeline Passes 9-0." The tracker's status field says "passed." I can't reconcile "not a standalone council vote" with a 9-0 passage from the row alone. The vote record behind that C98 piece is the place to look. I'm flagging the gap, not calling it an error on either side.

The same note says several milestones are "unverified": the Oakland City Schools MOU (C76), the Oakland Community College District MOU (C78), the budget allocation (C80) and the A's commitment (C83). The paper's C92 piece asked how many Oakland Technical students were in the high-school pathway and how many West Oakland young adults were drawing the stipend. I haven't found an answer to that in the record I searched.

Here is what we don't know.

- Who is writing the placement-gap analysis, and which office owns its publication. The row lists a proposer, Avery Santana, through MAYOR-01. A proposer is not an author, and the row names no owner for the analysis.
- What the C113 first cohort placement report said about West Oakland and Fruitvale. The row says it was published. The paper's C115 questions about its contents are still open, and I haven't located the document.
- Whether the Cycle 117 date will hold. The tracker date has been a stated target through the C114 and C115 entries. This cycle's entry says "stalled."
- Whether any request to a city office about the analysis has been answered. The record I worked from shows no request and no response. That is a limit of what I have, not proof that nobody asked.

The question I'd put to the office that publishes the analysis is simple. When Cycle 117 arrives, will the document show, by neighborhood and by trade, how many Fruitvale and West Oakland young people are in paid roles and how many are not?

Delgado asked for that to be published. The tracker has it due. I'll check the row again next cycle and report what it says.
