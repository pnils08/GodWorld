# Fruitvale's Transit Hub Is "Complete" on Paper. Its Schematics Are Still Owed.

*Cycle 117 · Civic desk · Luis Navarro*

The Fruitvale Transit Hub Phase II tracker entry reads COMPLETED. The Oakland Alternative Response Initiative's Fruitvale briefing is marked as directed and held. Both entries make it look like Fruitvale is further along than the record shows. I went through each one against the city's own sheets and the offices that answer for them.

**Here's what we know.**

**The transit hub.** The tracker line for Fruitvale Transit Hub Phase II — Visioning reads "status passed, phase design-phase since Cycle 108; voted Cycle 86, COMPLETED" (Initiative_Tracker, INIT-003). The council did vote at C86. The line does not mean the work is done. The same entry carries the next action: "Deliver preliminary schematics and publish the documented Fruitvale public comment-and-response record, due Cycle 118." The C117 milestone says further design advancement "is held," and that neither the schematics nor the comment-and-response record "are evidenced in the packet" (same sheet, INIT-003).

Elena Soria Dominguez, the Transit Hub Planning Lead, put it this way in an approved statement:

> "The tracker says 'complete' because the council done its part at C86 — it closes the visioning and the authorization history, nothing more. The schematics are not delivered yet, and the comment-and-response record is not published yet. I won't pretend otherwise."

When I asked her directly, she confirmed the schematics have not been delivered in a form she can put on the record. She said the council's directive since C111 has been preliminary schematics plus a documented Fruitvale comment-and-response record, and that design has waited on that record since C114–C115. She declined to give a delivery date beyond the Cycle 118 target on the sheet.

The public part of the process is a numbered comment log kept at the Chavez branch. Each comment is to be logged in the language it came in, with a written answer and a date before the drawings come down. Dominguez said the log is open to any resident to inspect. I asked how many entries have signed answers. She would not give a count, saying she did not have a verified figure in front of her and would not invent one. I'm treating that as an open item. I have not seen the log, and I have not counted it.

**The OARI briefing.** The tracker entry for the Oakland Alternative Response Initiative says the D3 Fruitvale briefing is "directed," and that the next action is to "execute and publish" it by Cycle 118 (Initiative_Tracker, INIT-002). The Mayor's office, Council District 3 and District 1 all put the same condition on the record: expansion stays held until the Fruitvale record is published, and the D5 audit and Baylight feasibility review have written next dates.

I asked Vanessa Tran-Muñoz, the OARI Program Director, whether the briefing has happened. Her answer, in paraphrase because she is not cleared for direct quotation in this piece, was no. Her office has no date, venue, notice date or participation arrangement to report. She also said that the van-event sheet has no Fruitvale row at all across its nineteen entries. Fruitvale's own OARI activity is thin in the record, and she said that is part of why the briefing is still owed. On the D5 audit and Baylight feasibility review, she said the next-date direction was filed at C117 and that she would have concrete calendar dates for them when the work is filed at C118. She also acknowledged a gap in the Clerk's index that her office still has to close.

**Here's what we don't know.**

- Whether the Chavez branch comment log has signed answers for every entry, or how many are outstanding.
- Whether the D5 and Baylight next dates appear in the Clerk's index. Tran-Muñoz said that check is still open on her side.
- Whether the Cycle 118 deadlines for the schematics and the OARI briefing will be met. Neither office has committed to a date beyond the sheet.

**Why it matters.** The "complete" label on INIT-003 does real work. A reader scanning the tracker would reasonably conclude the Fruitvale hub's visioning is finished and that design is moving forward. The sheet itself says design is held. The Mayor's C117 position says the same. Dominguez's own words about the log are the clearest account: her kids and mine walk past those drawings on the way to BART.

On the OARI side, the pattern is the same. A directed briefing is not a held briefing, and neither is a published one. Fruitvale has had decades of hearings. It is now getting a tracker that reads more finished than the work.

**What I'm doing next.** I'm filing for the Chavez branch comment log and the Clerk's index for the D5 audit and Baylight feasibility review. I'll count the signed answers myself rather than rely on a number from anyone. Cycle 118 is the due date for all three items. I'll be checking the sheet on the first day of it.

Here's what we know. The schematics aren't out. The comment record isn't published. The OARI briefing hasn't been executed. The tracker says otherwise, and the tracker is wrong on the question that matters.
