### **1. Housing Stabilization & Public Accounting (West Oakland & Citywide)**

#### **Prior Events, Promises & Public Record**
* **Legislative Origin & Vote:** Proposed by **Mayor Avery Santana**, the **West Oakland Stabilization Fund** was signed into law in **Cycle 78** on a **6–3 City Council vote** (recorded votes: **Ramon Vega** yes, **Leonard Tran** no, **Elliott Crane** yes remotely) with an initial **\$28 million budget** `[1, 2]`.
* **Disbursement History & Ledger Milestones:**
  * In **Cycle 110**, Fund Director **Marcus Webb** reported Month-Nine audit data to the Finance Committee: **79 households stabilized in nine months** at a **23-day average processing window** (\$400,000 approved for Month-Nine) `[1]`. By the end of Cycle 109, the ledger recorded **\$81,188 disbursed in direct household grants**, leaving **\$23 million remaining** `[1]`.
  * In **Cycle 111 (Y3C7)**, reporter **Carmen Delaine** logged the Month-Ten disbursement approval of another **\$400,000**, advancing the count to **91 households stabilized** (holding the 23-day processing standard) with **\$22.6 million remaining** `[2]`.
* **Institutional Promises & Positions:**
  * **Mayor Avery Santana:** Announced Month-Ten approval, stating: *"relief without bureaucracy keeps neighbors in their neighborhoods"* and *"the families who qualified for this money six months ago shouldn't wait another day"* `[1, 2]`.
  * **Deputy Mayor Brenda Okoro:** Stated: *"Stabilization is a race against displacement — month-ten disbursement keeps us ahead"* `[1, 2]`.
  * **District 1 Councilmember Denise Carter:** Framed the fund around commercial/residential movement: *"When storefronts go quiet, families go next—so we're taking the Stabilization Fund to where the need is moving"* `[1, 2]`.

#### **Direct Published Fact vs. Analytical Inference**
* **Direct Published Fact:** The fund moved \$400,000 in Month Ten to reach 91 stabilized households with \$22.6M remaining `[2]`. City treasury closed at \$110.3M after taking in \$5.1M `[2]`. Neighboring initiatives sit pending: the **West Oakland Tenant Defense Fund** (\$4.2M budget, vote scheduled Cycle 112) and **Commercial Stabilization for Grand Lake** (\$2.5M budget, legislation filed) `[2]`.
* **Analytical Inference & Unresolved Storylines for Cycle 112:**
  1. **The Accounting Reconciliation:** Editor **Mags Corliss** and Carmen Delaine flag an unexplained arithmetic discrepancy between the \$28M initial envelope, \$81,188 in early direct grants, and \$22.6M remaining `[1, 2]`.
  2. **Case-Level Transparency:** The 23-day processing average hides individual case outliers, and official records do not map specific blocks or tenant names receiving funds `[2]`.
  3. **Upcoming Actions:** The **Month-Eleven disbursement review in Cycle 112** and the **Cycle 112 Council vote on the Tenant Defense Fund** will determine whether public funds keep pace with displacement pressure `[2]`.
