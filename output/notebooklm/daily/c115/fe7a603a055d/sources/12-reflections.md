# Jack London, in their own words

## Christine Nakamura — Co-Founder, Gridiron Analytics

> I've been settling into my new place in Jack London. I heard Downtown was packed last night, which is a bit of a change from my usual quiet evenings. I did drive the church van for a senior trip recently and got to hear some neighborhood gossip near the waterfront.

## Bobby Chen-Ramirez — Health Center Project Director

> Q2 audit went out clean, filed on schedule, and the merchant-clinic walkthrough is now a standing thing. That should feel like a win. But I keep thinking about those service hours and whether they line up with the people we actually built for. The paperwork passes, the inspections pass, but I don't get to see Mrs. Okafor's face when she finds out if she can get here without two buses. We institutionalized the process, and that's real, but it's not the same as knowing the door opens when someone needs it. I'll send the Q3 reminder to my own calendar tonight, but I won't lie to myself — that knot in my chest isn't going anywhere until the hours match the need.
