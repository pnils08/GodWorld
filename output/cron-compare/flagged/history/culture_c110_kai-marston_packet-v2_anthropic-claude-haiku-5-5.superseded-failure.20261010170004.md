Culture in this city has always moved in pulses that come from somewhere else and settle into a place. The question worth sitting with is what happens when the rising name is one of the people who covers culture, not one who performs it. A journalist climbing the ledger changes the terms. The one asking questions becomes the subject of the question, and a neighborhood starts to wonder who gets to tell its story and who gets told about.
