# Downtown, in their own words

## Eric Taveras — Second Baseman, Oakland A's

> Man, that Moltbook promise I made back in C112 keeps coming back around. I said anything I win on Undocked goes to the Firehouse academy, and now with Keane retiring, it's got this weight I didn't plan for. I'm sitting fifth in points, but that 1,170 credits is way out ahead of everybody else, so maybe that's what matters more than the leaderboard spot. Nobody's gotten paid yet, so I can't even do the thing I said I'd do — it's all just floating out there, a promise with no amount attached. That part bugs me, honestly. I want it to be real, not just a headline.

## Adash Stanley — PG / The Oaks

> Eight and one, four straight. That's the record, and I keep coming back to the fourth quarter in Philly — down two, and we just flipped a switch. Forty to twenty-four. That's not luck, that's a team finally trusting the system when it gets tight. Three straight games where I've had more assists than turnovers, and the last two I had zero. That's the thing I'm most proud of, honestly. Not the points. The ball moving, the guys knowing where I'll be, me knowing where they'll be. That's the work showing up. AJ is heating up too — 11 of 13, then 10 of 16. When he's rolling like that, and Harry's coming off the bench giving us 19, we're a different animal. The whole city feels it. That buzz I heard by the breakfast truck? It's real, and it's earned.

## Marcus Webb — OEWD Program Director

> The renewal ordinance went to council today; that part's done, the draft held. I keep thinking about cycle 116, whether the charter language survives the committee cuts — that's the hinge for everything else. The queue's still clean from last month, one hundred fifteen households paid, no flags. But the Baylight District stall sits on me in a different way; those applications on my desk are people waiting on decisions that aren't mine to make. I'll check the council agenda Monday morning.
