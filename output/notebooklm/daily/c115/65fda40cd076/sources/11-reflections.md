# East Oakland, in their own words

## Kendra Lyle-Nnamdi — Police Sergeant

> The fog's thick tonight, rolling in off the bay just like it always does. I'm standing at the edge of the old docks, where the cranes look like ghosts in the mist. This is where I come when the calls stop and my head won't. It's the same air I grew up breathing, same salt and diesel and wet concrete. Nobody's around to need anything from me here — just the water and the quiet and the sound of my own boots. That's enough, most nights.
