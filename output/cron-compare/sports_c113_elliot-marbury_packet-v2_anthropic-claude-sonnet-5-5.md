# Gonzalez's 2039 Season: What 506 At-Bats Show About His Power and Contact

Vladimir Gonzalez's 2039 line for the Athletics is 127 games, 506 at-bats, 137 hits, 14 home runs and 78 strikeouts. His career line works that out to one home run every 36.1 at-bats and one strikeout every 6.5 at-bats. That is the only one of his three dossier seasons large enough to carry a rate.

## The claim

**The claim:** Gonzalez's 2039 rates, one home run per 36.1 at-bats and one strikeout per 6.5, are the best available estimate of his power and contact profile. The other two seasons are too thin to move that estimate.

Both short seasons rest on two home runs. A single home run more or less would move either rate sharply. The 2038 figure of 60.0 and the 2037 figure of 30.5 sit on opposite sides of the 2039 rate, and neither sample is large enough to say which way the true rate leans.

2038 deserves a second look because its AVG and SLG are the weakest of the three, at .200 and .317. His OBP that year was .322, close to the .335 he posted over a full 2039. That gap between a low average and a steady on-base figure comes from the 20 walks in 120 at-bats. It is the one 2038 cell that holds up against the full season. His 2038 strikeout rate, one every 4.8 at-bats, is also the most frequent of the three. That is the number most worth watching, because it is the one most likely to show a real contact problem if it persists. On 120 at-bats it is not yet a finding.

## The peer band

Frank Reyna, Athletics, also a right fielder, has a 2041 dossier line of 46 games, 164 at-bats, 52 hits and 14 home runs. He has 18 strikeouts, a .317 average and a .683 slugging mark. His rates are one home run every 11.7 at-bats and one strikeout every 9.1.

Reyna hit the same 14 home runs as Gonzalez did in 2039, in 164 at-bats rather than 506. His strikeout rate is also better. His 2041 season is a thin sample and his dossier holds one season. It shows what a short run can produce. It does not show what a right fielder in this lineup normally is.

His 6 walks against Gonzalez's 49 in 2039 put their OBP figures close together, .339 against .335. The difference between them is mostly power, not on-base skill. Gonzalez's 2039 slugging of .427 is well below Reyna's .683. A 2041 season is also later than anything on Gonzalez's dossier, so the two lines are not from the same year and are not merged here.

## What would disprove it

The 2039 rates are the baseline. A full 2040 season that keeps Gonzalez at or beyond one home run per 50 at-bats and no more often than one strikeout per 5 would leave the claim standing. A full-year rate that slides toward the 2038 mark of one home run per 60.0 at-bats, or a strikeout rate at or below the 2038 mark of one per 4.8 at-bats, would show 2039 was the outlier. Either result, on a sample of several hundred at-bats, would count against the claim.
