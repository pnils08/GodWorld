That is the human reading of a technical condition: fewer riders do not create an empty station, but they alter the work around the trains. That split is the important infrastructure fact. The system did not simply become less used everywhere. The next question is operational rather than dramatic. If Lake Merritt’s lower ridership continues, what will distinguish a temporary thinning from a change in how the station is being used? The current numbers show the condition. They do not yet show its duration, its cause or what transit workers will be asked to absorb next.
