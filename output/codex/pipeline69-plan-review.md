### (c) Citizen names, metrics, and place scope

1. **Confirmed citizen names already survive the seed route.** C107 seed `output/beats/Story_Seed_Deck.jsonl:229` says `Rico Valez spotted at Canopy Market`, but Rico is absent from that row's `Citizens`. Row `:230` similarly names GameGirl Gia without listing her in `Citizens`. Both pass the current pure translator, and both appear in the existing texture (`output/neighborhood_texture_c107.md:31`, `:75`). Their ledger identities are verified at `output/simulation_ledger_snapshot.jsonl:470` and `:895`. Adding a name filter only to new SEEN lines leaves these NOTED leaks intact (`scripts/buildNeighborhoodTexture.js:186`).
2. **Confirmed metric reaches the model input.** `Gridiron Analytics added 2 employees` survives `seedSourceLines` unchanged (`output/beats/Story_Seed_Deck.jsonl:225`; translator filter `scripts/buildNeighborhoodTexture.js:133-136`). That filter catches signed numbers, decimals, and selected system terms, but not an unsigned integer count. Output `metricWarnings` also misses this count and is warning-only (`:446-455`, `:557-558`). This review demonstrates source admission, not that the final C107 texture printed the count.
3. **Sanitization has bypasses and incomplete coverage.** Relocation and initiative-phase branches push then continue before the shared name/metric loop (`scripts/buildNeighborhoodTexture.js:117-126`, `:131-136`). EVENT descriptions, VENUE names, and CITY EVENT text enter the same bundle without that loop (`:195-208`). A common final source boundary must enforce the task's citizen-name/metric rule; copying just the seed replacement logic into LifeHistory handling is insufficient. Current prompt rules forbid real-world people but permit names in sources; the output sweep checks faith/sports real-name lists rather than the Simulation_Ledger roster (`:239`, `:416-432`).
4. **A citizen's Neighborhood is not necessarily the event's location.** A C107 citizen line says they avoided Jack London after hearing about its crowds (`output/simulation_ledger_snapshot.jsonl:6`). A PrevEvening line grouped under the resident's hood must not turn those crowds into an event in that hood. The existing prompt's source-grounding rule does not resolve that distinction by itself (`scripts/buildNeighborhoodTexture.js:236`; plan routing at `docs/plans/2026-09-13-run-cycle-packages-the-world.md:68-70`). The whitelist also admits private reactions such as “stayed cautious” (`output/simulation_ledger_snapshot.jsonl:14`); tag admission is not a complete place-shaped-text classifier.
