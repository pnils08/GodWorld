## West Oakland Stabilization Fund Enters Month Nine of Disbursement  

The West Oakland Stabilization Fund, a $28 million initiative approved by City Council in Cycle 78, has now disbursed $4.6 million across nine months of active implementation. Designed as an economic stabilization measure for long-tenure West Oakland residents and businesses, the Fund has issued 49 grants to date, with the latest tranche of $400,000 released in Cycle 109.  

Council voted 6-3 to approve the initiative in Cycle 78, with District 4 Councilmember Ramon Vega and District 2 Councilmember Leonard Tran casting opposing votes. Mayor Avery Santana signed the bill into law, leveraging Deputy Mayor Okoro’s authority to oversee disbursements.  

**Implementation Status:**  
- Budget: $28 million (Passed Cycle 78)  
- Disbursed to Date: $4.6 million (~16% of total)  
- Remaining Funds: $23.4 million  
- Next Scheduled Action: Month-nine disbursement review in Cycle 110  

Chartered as a targeted grant program, the Fund prioritizes households and small businesses most vulnerable to displacement amidst rapid development in West Oakland. Early disbursements focused on stabilizing rental payments and small business overhead, though specific metrics on grant outcomes remain unpublished.  

**Questions Forward:**  
1. With $23.4 million remaining, how will disbursement priorities evolve?  
2. Will the Fund expand to other neighborhoods, or remain focused on West Oakland?  

The Stabilization Fund represents one of Mayor Santana’s signature economic initiatives, now nearing its first full cycle of implementation. As disbursements continue, its impact on long-term stability in West Oakland will come into sharper focus.
