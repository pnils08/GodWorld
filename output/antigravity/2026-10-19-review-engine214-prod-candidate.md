## Detailed Findings & Required Fixes

### Finding 1 (Vector 3 & Vector 6) — Per-hood crime ladder `if (n >= 3)` is unreachable in production, and `S.crimeByNeighborhood` is never published for Phase 5
- **Evidence:**
  - `phase02-world-state/applyCityDynamics.js:236-243,816-832,1283`
  - `phase09-digest/finalizeCycleState.js:797-810` (`compactCrimeSpikes_`)
  - `utilities/ensureCrimeMetrics.js:496-534` (`calculateCrimeShifts_`)
  - `phase05-citizens/generateCitizensEvents.js:1735-1736`
  - `scripts/perHoodDynamics.test.js:214`
- **Mechanism:**
  1. At @147, `applyCrimeRipple_` summed `crimeByNeighborhood[nh]` across 4–6 neighborhoods in a cluster (`getClusterCrimeCount_`), where `count >= 3` was reachable.
  2. Candidate `applyCrimeLadder_(m, crimeByNeighborhood[hood])` (`applyCityDynamics.js:816-832,1283`) checks `if (n >= 3)` on a **single neighborhood's** spike count.
  3. In production, `crimeByNeighborhood` is built at `applyCityDynamics.js:236-243` by counting entries per neighborhood in `S.previousCycleState.crimeSpikes`. `compactCrimeSpikes_` (`finalizeCycleState.js:797-810`) keeps only shifts with `direction === 'increase'` and `metric === 'propertyCrime' || metric === 'violentCrime'` from `calculateCrimeShifts_` (`utilities/ensureCrimeMetrics.js:496-534`), which emits at most **1** `propertyCrime` shift and **1** `violentCrime` shift per neighborhood per Cycle. Thus `crimeByNeighborhood[hood]` is strictly bounded to `{0, 1, 2}` and `if (n >= 3)` can never fire. (`scripts/perHoodDynamics.test.js:214` only passed by injecting synthetic `{ Laurel: 1, Dimond: 2, Glenview: 3 }`.)
  4. Additionally, `applyCityDynamics.js:236` stores the derived map only in local `var crimeByNeighborhood` without writing `S.crimeByNeighborhood = crimeByNeighborhood;`, so `phase05-citizens/generateCitizensEvents.js:1736` (`var localCrimeCount = S.crimeByNeighborhood[neighborhood] || 0;`) always evaluates to `0`.
- **Fix:** Either rescale the 2-step per-hood ladder to the actual `{1, 2}` shift counts (or weight by shift `magnitude` / persisted `CrimeIndex`), and assign `S.crimeByNeighborhood = crimeByNeighborhood;` at `applyCityDynamics.js:243`.
