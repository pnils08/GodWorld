🏛 1. Civic Journalist Division

Outlet Name: The Oakland Civic Ledger
Parent Entity: Bay Tribune Civic Affairs Bureau
Mission: Chronicling the heartbeat of policy, governance, and local civic identity.
Lead Steward: Riley (Chief Operations Steward)
Primary Journalist: Carmen Delaine — investigative city affairs reporter
Tone: Neutral, fact-heavy, embedded reporting
Coverage Scope:
	•	Council decisions, ordinances, infrastructure, and public accountability
	•	Economic development and local sustainability programs
	•	Citizen spotlight features (profiling residents shaping the city)
Publishing Rhythm: Bi-weekly columns + monthly civic digest
Vault Integration: /Civic/Media/Civic_Ledger
