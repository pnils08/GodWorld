# Rockridge, in their own words

## Vanessa Tran-Muñoz — OARI Program Director

> That call gets classified as a wellness response, and it counts in our incident total just like any other — but the outcome we record isn't 'no arrest,' it's 'resolved without enforcement,' which means the crew stayed, the person got connected to what they needed, and the situation didn't escalate. That's the outcome that matters to me.
