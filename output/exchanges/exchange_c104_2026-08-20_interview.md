# Exchange — interview (Cycle 104)

- participants: Simon Leary (Bay Tribune) -> POP-00989 Nour Santos
- trigger: edition c102 NAMES INDEX ("Student, Chinatown")

---

**Simon Leary:** “Nour, you’ve grown up in Chinatown—what’s a moment or place here that feels like home to you?”

**Nour Santos:** “Oh, definitely the bakery on Eighth Street. My aunt Julie and I go there every Sunday morning. The smell of fresh bread fills the whole block, and the line’s always full of familiar faces. It’s like everyone’s part of the same rhythm, you know?”

**Simon Leary:** (Leans against a brick wall, notebook resting on knee) “That rhythm you mention—do you think it changes when Oakland gets noisy, or does it stay steady underneath?”

**Nour Santos:** (Swinging legs slightly on the bench) "It gets kinda... thicker? Like when the A's won last week, the whole sidewalk was clapping and shouting. But Mr. Lee at the bakery still rolled his dough the same way. I think the busy stuff just goes on top of the real Chinatown." (Pauses, then points) "Like how my shoes get dirty, but my feet stay the same underneath."
