# KONO, in their own words

## Jasmine Torres — Founder, Tenth Street Digital

> KONO's got a bit of a mixed bag going on right now. On one hand, life on the block seems pretty normal despite the lingering smell of smoke from the recent blaze. We've even got a newcomer from Jack London settling in, which is always nice to see. On the other hand, I've noticed that the neighborhood's vibe feels a bit off, and I'm not sure if it's just me being a fan of the A's and still feeling a little down about Mark Aitken leaving. I mean, he was a big part of Oakland, not just the team. As for the city's support, I'm not really sure what to say. I moved here from Chinatown a little while back because KONO felt like a better fit for me and my wife, but it's clear there's some tension around housing and retail vitality. I hope the city can figure out a way to balance things out.

## Dmitri Soto — Container Yard Supervisor

> To be honest, I haven't really seen the housing pressure affecting local workers around here. KONO's been bustling, retail's busy, and we're getting new households moving in. I've made the move here myself recently, from Jack London, and it's been a positive change so far.

## Michael Corliss — Photographer

> I've seen KONO's energy firsthand - we're always out and about, whether it's a game or a night out at Midnight Norae Karaoke. But with the housing pressure, it's like there's this undercurrent that's hard to ignore. I've painted over graffiti with neighbors I barely knew, and that's felt like a small win, but it's also made me wonder what's being lost as the neighborhood changes.

## Celeste Moon — singer

> To be honest, I've noticed that around here, there's a lot of community spirit, people looking out for each other. I helped a neighbor with the West Oakland Stabilization Fund portal recently because it was really confusing for them. We also have a seniors lunch program flyer on our community board. It's nice to see that kind of activity. At the same time, housing feels like a big issue. I've seen newcomers moving in, like the person from Jack London who just settled in down the street. But there's also the lingering smell of smoke from that recent blaze, which is a harsh reminder of some of the challenges we're facing.

## Amal Nair — Physical Therapist

> The window paid — the week changed color. I won on the UNDOCKED book, and it was a nice surprise. To be honest, it put a little extra spring in my step heading into the A's game.
