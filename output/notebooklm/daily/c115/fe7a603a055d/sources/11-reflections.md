# Fruitvale, in their own words

## Ramon Solano — Senior Pastor / Faith Leader

> As the Senior Pastor of St. Esperanza Parish, I've seen firsthand how our church supports the Latino community in Fruitvale. We serve as a community center and a place of worship, with a focus on family. Our congregation is about 800 strong, and we've been a part of this neighborhood for 119 years. We provide various services and activities that cater to the needs of our community, and I'm proud to be a part of it.

## Lucia Polito — Aura Wellness Practitioner

> I've founded Postpartum Oakland Network, a program that supports new mothers in our community. While I don't have a direct connection to St. Esperanza Parish, I've seen the impact that faith organizations can have on people's lives. In Fruitvale, we've had some beautiful collaborations between different community groups, and I think it's possible that St. Esperanza Parish could be a valuable partner for us. I'd love to explore that further.

## Elena Soria Dominguez — Transit Hub Planning Lead

> The schematics are done, and they're good. But they're just paper until the community review is documented and the public record is closed. Four cycles of visioning, and I'm still one comment period away from a real vote. Doña Esperanza asked me last week if her cart's spot is in the final drawings. I told her yes — because it is, and I meant it. But I've been staring at the same closed milestone all day, and I keep thinking about how "closed" doesn't mean "decided."

## Rose Delgado — City Council District - D3

> I keep coming back to that dip in retail vitality — 6.43 to 5.22 is not a wobble, that's a block losing its grip. And the sentiment swing in San Antonio, down to -0.16 while the whole city sits near zero, tells me people aren't confused, they're just tired of being sold the same plan. Fruitvale's holding, but it's holding by a thread, and I can feel that thread in every storefront I walk past. I filed the numbers because I need them on paper, proof that this isn't a mood swing — it's a pattern I can point to when I ask for investment. The quiet tonight is fine; it gives me room to figure out who else sees the same thing.
