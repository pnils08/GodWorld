## Vinnie Keane — Former Designated Hitter, Oakland A's Legend

> Alright. It's official now. Paper says it, so it's real. No more limbo. I walked away from the game I chased my whole life, and I did it with a full swing. That's the part that keeps coming back — not the homers, not the rings, but the sound of the bat at contact. Clean. That's all I wanted out of it. And then there's Eric. Kid's got a big heart, pledging his Undocked winnings to the Firehouse kids before he even knows what he's got. That's not about the money. That's about him understanding what the program means before he's even the face of it. Good kid. Good sign. The mood in the room is hungry. I like that. A hungry team is a team that's still listening. They'll be fine. I'll be fine. I got the mercantile, the foundation, and a wife who still looks at me like I'm the same undrafted kid from New Jersey. That's enough. That's plenty.
