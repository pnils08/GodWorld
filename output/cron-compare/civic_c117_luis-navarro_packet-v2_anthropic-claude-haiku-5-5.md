# Fruitvale Transit Hub: The Tribune Said Construction Had Started. The Record Says It Has Not.

By Luis Navarro, Managing Editor, Investigations

Here's what we know. Here's what we don't know.

**What we printed.** In Cycle 97 the Tribune ran "Ground Breaks in Fruitvale: The Transit Hub Enters Construction." The piece described a crane offloading to a cleared lot, survey stakes in the ground, and a site trailer where the east pedestrian plaza would stand. It quoted Elena Soria Dominguez, the Transit Hub Planning Lead, walking the perimeter before a ceremony. Our Cycle 101 piece, "Fruitvale's Bargain Comes Due," had already reported that the project was still in construction-planning with no phase advance in four cycles. The two pieces disagree, and the record now sides with the earlier one.

**What the record shows.** The initiative tracker lists Fruitvale Transit Hub Phase II as INIT-003. Its Visioning phase passed and was marked COMPLETED at Cycle 86. Its current phase is design-phase, not construction. The tracker has no construction milestone. Its C117 entry says that further design advancement is held, and that preliminary schematics and a documented Fruitvale public comment-and-response record "are not evidenced in the packet."

I asked Soria Dominguez directly. Her answer, in full on the record: "Construction has not begun. There is no construction on the Fruitvale Transit Hub Phase II, and the tracker does not say there is." She said INIT-003 shows no payments this cycle, and that her own work entries on the initiative (Cycles 113, 114 and 115) are planning and review work. She also said the tracker's C86 "complete" entry closes only the visioning and council-authorization history. It does not close the design work.

She was blunt about the print. "The Tribune's print saying otherwise is wrong. I'd ask for a correction." She is right to ask. We are not going to argue with the tracker because we once wrote a headline we liked.

**What the council and the mayor have said.** Council District 3 Rose Delgado, whose district includes the site, voted to hold further design advancement at the Cycle 86 authorization. She required the contracted design consultant to deliver preliminary schematics and hold a documented Fruitvale community review. Delgado told us about the review schedule and the scope of the hold:

> "The Fruitvale community review is set for the first Thursday of next month at the Fruitvale Senior Center, 6 p.m., with the design team in the room — and yes, the held design covers the site work, because we're not letting construction outrun the community's say."

Mayor Avery Santana's Cycle 117 position matches that hold. He directed that design advancement wait until schematics and a documented comment-and-response record are "delivered and published." Those words describe a project that is still on paper.

**What we don't know.** The packet does not show whether the design consultant has produced preliminary schematics. It does not show whether the Fruitvale review has been held or when the first Thursday falls. We have not seen the comment log. Soria Dominguez said the answer desk and numbered comment log are staffed at the Chavez branch, with every comment logged in the language it was given, but we have not seen those logs. We also have not verified the Cycle 87 report of a 7-1 council vote. The tracker records the C86 authorization and a "projected 6-3" count, and I have not reconciled the two.

**The ridership context.** Soria Dominguez gave us this cycle's numbers. Fruitvale station is at 8,007 riders, down from 9,343. On-time running is 0.85, against 0.83 before. She tied the wider pattern to event and game-day crowding. These are her figures, given on the record. I have not checked them against the transit metrics sheet.

**What happens next.** The tracker's next action, due Cycle 118, is to deliver the preliminary schematics and publish the Fruitvale comment-and-response record. If both appear, the hold lifts. If they do not, the Tribune's Cycle 97 headline stays wrong on the public record, and the project remains in design.

I have asked the Tribune's desk for a correction. I will report what the Cycle 118 packet shows and will not describe the hub as under construction until the tracker and a site record say so.
