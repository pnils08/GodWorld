## §2 THE REPORTER'S PLAN (wake 1, their own voice)
I want to go back to the same West Oakland room and sit with what the comeback has changed there—and what it has not. The Oaks are 11-2, have won the fourth quarter 10 times in 13 games, and just came back from 15 down at Golden State, the team that left Oakland. I will watch the replay with the regulars, count who stays with it and who turns away, and ask whether this team has become part of their day or only a good score to talk about. I will keep the quiet answer too: the people who do not have to think about the Oaks at all.
