# Rivas's 1.6 Walks per Nine Is a 136.1-Inning Reading, Not a New Level

Henry Rivas's 2041 line for the Athletics shows 1.6 walks per nine innings, 8.8 strikeouts per nine and 5.54 strikeouts per walk. The sample is 136.1 innings over 22 starts. Those are the best command rates in the eight seasons on his dossier. The claim in this memo is narrow: that sample does not show a break from his 2038 and 2039 command. It shows a return to that level, and a season this short cannot say more.

**The line.** In 2041 he went 11-4 with a 2.64 ERA. He allowed 123 hits, 40 earned runs and 16 home runs, and he walked 24 and struck out 133.

**The seasons before it.** The dossier holds eight seasons. Only the three most recent complete ones are used here as a command baseline.

- 2040 (Athletics, 33 starts, 184.2 IP): 2.9 BB/9, 8.2 K/9, 2.86 K/BB, 3.7 ERA. This is the weakest command season of his Athletics years, with 59 walks.
- 2039 (Athletics, 33 starts, 187.2 IP): 1.8 BB/9, 8.8 K/9, 4.84 K/BB, 3.84 ERA, 38 walks.
- 2038 (Athletics, 33 starts, 183.1 IP): 1.9 BB/9, 8.6 K/9, 4.49 K/BB, 3.39 ERA, 39 walks.

The 2041 walk rate of 1.6 sits next to the 1.8 and 1.9 he posted over roughly 185 innings in each of 2038 and 2039. The dossier shows he held a walk rate in that range across two full seasons. It does not show 2041 moved the range down.

**The earlier seasons.** The Philadelphia years run from 2.5 to 2.8 BB/9 over 170 to 187 innings: 2.5 in 2036, 2.8 in 2037 and 2.6 in 2035. His 2034 season is the closest comparison in length. In that pair the ERA rose and the walk rate held. A shortened season with a low ERA has already happened once on this dossier, and it was followed by a full-length line with a higher run number. That gives no forecast for 2042. It only shows a short year does not fix a level.

Ramos's walk rate is almost level with Rivas's, and he reached it over a much longer 2041 workload. That makes a rate in the 1.6 to 1.7 range a real feature of this rotation. Rivas's 5.54 K/BB is the highest of the three, and it is the figure most exposed to the innings count.

**What the record does not hold.** The dossier gives 22 starts and 136.1 innings. It does not say why the season ran shorter than his 33-start years, and this memo draws no conclusion from that. The dossier also holds no pitch-level or batted-ball cells, so nothing here speaks to how the walks fell. Everything above comes from innings, walks, strikeouts and runs.

**The test.** The claim fails if Rivas posts a walk rate at or below 1.6 per nine over a full-length season of about 180 innings or more. That would make 2041 a new level and not a short-sample high. The claim also fails in the other direction if he returns to the 2.9 of 2040 over a similar workload, which would make 2038, 2039 and 2041 the outliers of his Athletics years. A walk rate near 1.8 to 1.9 over a full year would match the claim as written. No 2042 line is on file.
