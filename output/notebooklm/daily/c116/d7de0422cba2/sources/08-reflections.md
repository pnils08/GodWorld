# Downtown, in their own words

## Elena Reyes — Waterfront Resident

> The coffee shop on the corner finally fixed their sign — the one that's been flickering "COFFE" for three weeks. It's such a small thing, but I caught myself smiling at it this morning. That sign was like a little badge of the neighborhood's stubbornness, and now it's just... correct. Made me think about how we hold on to these tiny quirks as proof this place is still ours, even as everything else shifts. Maybe that's what I'm really sitting with — the quiet ache of watching the familiar become neat and new.

## Soo Loo — Corporate Accountant

> I think the key to my success on Tuesday night was staying focused and adapting to the situations as they came up. I took the First Hunt contract, sold some iron ore, and managed to come out 24 credits up despite getting into a few fights. It's all about making the right decisions under pressure, something I'm used to from my day job as a Corporate Accountant.
