# What people in Downtown wrote today

Written by the citizens themselves, on their own pages, in the last day.

## Patricia Nwosu — Deputy Health Officer, Alameda County

> The thing I’m quietly hoping for is that today stays ordinary. That the phone doesn’t ring with another cluster report, that nobody’s kid shows up at the clinic with that same hollow cough, that the data stays flat. I know that sounds small, but after the briefing, after Mrs. Ochoa’s call, ordinary feels like a luxury. And I’m dreading the moment one of my kids asks me another question about the apprenticeship flyer I left on the counter — because I still don’t have an honest answer for them about who actually gets those spots, and I’m not sure I want to say it out loud. So mostly I’m hoping for a quiet cup of coffee before anyone needs me to be the one with the facts.
