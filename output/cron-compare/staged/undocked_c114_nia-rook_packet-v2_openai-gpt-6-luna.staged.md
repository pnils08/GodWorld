Lena Okafor’s Monday night didn’t take the top spot from Daphne Stafford. That’s the board’s clean headline; the more entertaining one is that Lena wasn’t flying as if she needed to. She kept going until the show ran out of time, and the standings were not what she says was on her mind.

The current board puts Daphne at #1, with 32.93 achievement points, two cycles led, and a current streak of two. The credits line is 0. There’s a little pressure built into that combination: a lead with a streak behind it feels like a story already gathering momentum. But Lena’s episode isn’t a chase scene. It’s a long, roaming night—Sol, Alpha Centauri, Tau Ceti, Electra, Mimosa, Ross 154, Arcturus, Timberline, Maplevale, Miaplacidus—with 11 iron ore and 6 copper ore mined, one fight, and credits even at the end.

I like the detail that the night simply stops when the show does. In Lena’s own account, “I just didn’t want to stop.” She says she’d had a good run, was on a roll, and wanted to do something for herself; the kids were doing well, her husband was supportive, and the standings and credits weren’t taking up much room in her thoughts. That makes the even-credit finish feel less like a verdict than a closing beat: the tally settles, but the appetite doesn’t.

There’s history behind Daphne’s name at the top, too, though not a tidy rivalry arc. An earlier approved C113 episode record has Daphne moving through Sol, Alpha Centauri, Tau Ceti, Timberline, Arcturus, Ross 154, Mimosa, Electra, Miaplacidus and Maplevale, with no combat recorded. That’s a striking overlap with Lena’s route—not evidence of a contest between them, but a familiar stretch of the map showing up in two different kinds of night. Daphne’s record is the board’s steady pulse; Lena’s is the pleasure of carrying on because the flight still feels good.

I’m with the second, while still keeping one eye on the board. Lena didn’t claim the lead tonight, and nothing in this episode says she was trying to.
