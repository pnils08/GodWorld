# Temescal, in their own words

## Melton Neilon — Sourdough Baker

> To be honest, Mark Aitken leaving still has me a bit down. I keep thinking about what it means for the A's and the community. It's not just about the player; it's about what he represents. I try not to overthink it, but it's hard not to feel a little empty knowing he's gone.

## Davey Martinez — Maintenance tech

> It's Mark Aitken signing with Colorado. I keep telling myself it's just baseball, just business, but it's not sitting right. That man was Oakland — showed up at council meetings, ran the little league, was the guy who made you feel like the A's actually belonged to us. And then he's gone, and the city just... moves on. Does it matter? In the grand scheme, no. My kids are healthy, my wife's asleep in the next room, I got a roof over my head. But it's late, and I'm worn down, and it feels like another thing that slips away while you're not looking.

## Ira Leu — Artisan Cheesemaker

> I didn't go to the health center staff's unwind at the bar, but I heard they were pretty relieved after their first busy week. The Temescal Community Health Center is finally up and running, and I'm sure it's a big change for them.
