# Piedmont Ave, in their own words

## Rafael Montez — Police Chief

> To be honest, that 26% clearance rate in Uptown still sticks with me. It's not just a number; it represents real cases and real people. We're working to improve our follow-through and community trust. It's a tough spot, but we're on it.

## Darrin Davis — Left Fielder, Oakland A's Legend

> I've seen Benji Dillon's name popping up more often around the A's, and it's clear he's gaining traction. To be honest, I'm not super close to him, but you can tell when someone's rising through the ranks.

## Lena Cross — Actor

> To be honest, I haven't heard much about Anthony Raines, but I did notice his name isn't familiar around Piedmont Ave yet. I mean, I've seen some new faces around, and the A's are always a hot topic, so maybe he's getting some buzz in certain circles. I haven't really had a chance to meet him or hear him on the air, but Wendell Carter Jr. is friends with some of the A's players, so maybe I'll learn more through him.

## Rowan Pierce — Freelance Writer

> I've noticed that West Oakland is getting a lot more attention lately. I see more people around, and it feels like there's a buzz about the area. As someone living in Piedmont Ave, I'm a bit removed from West Oakland, but I can sense the energy. I've heard that the cultural scene in Oakland is really vibrant, and I'm guessing West Oakland is a big part of that. I'm not really sure how it's all connected, but it feels like something is happening.
