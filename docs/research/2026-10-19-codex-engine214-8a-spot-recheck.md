## New hunt B — T5 visibility, signs, and weight stacking

The existing T5 loop matches `source:neighborhood`, `source:prevEvening`, and several other source tags, **not** `source:nbhdState` (`generateCitizensEvents.js:3320-3341`). Merely assigning the plan's three reaction weights does nothing without a new branch. Reusing `source:neighborhood` would also invoke the archetype neighborhood multiplier (`:3293-3317`) and T5 outabout multiplier, not the requested sociability/composure/warmth mapping. Give reactions distinct tags and apply exactly one specified dial multiplier to each. `getCitizenDialBands_` provides the 0.5–1.5 `mult` values with null fallback (`utilities/compressLifeHistory.js:1245-1273`); the plan's null behavior is feasible.

There is also a tag-sign collision: `state:trajectory-down` maps every decay entry to outabout-down, and `state:trajectory-up` every growth entry to outabout-up (`generateCitizensEvents.js:1054-1059`). `gradedTagFor_` gives `dial:outabout:up/down` explicit tags precedence over state facts (`:1104-1128`), so B must specify those for decay humble/defend and growth complaint, retain the real trajectory tag as context, and coordinate A's pulse sign with the effective grade. “Each line's sign through its own fact tag” (`plan:202`) does not describe an existing distinct signed reaction fact tag.

Weights multiply in order: archetype, T5, grief, then one 0.85 continuity factor **per** matching tag (`generateCitizensEvents.js:3293-3362`). Current `source:nbhdState` is not an archetype neighborhood match (`:3303-3307`) or a grief public/support match (`:134-155`), but added category tags could make those paths stack. Continuity already can stack. The `pw` cap of 6 applies **before** these modifiers (`:1696-1699`), so a ×1.5 T5 reaction can reach 9 even without other boosts. Specify final reaction cap or an explicit accepted range, and prove offered effective mass and drawn sign by dial band; no post-modifier pin exists today.

## New hunt C — previous streak and per-row sports fact

The previous streak is `ps.streak` for each BIZ_ID at loop entry (`applyBusinessDynamics.js:525`, `:574-580`); this Cycle's `d.streak` is computed at `:619`, then the close branch and shed decision run at `:647-695`. Thus recovery is implementable at the row collection point, but the floor must be fixed from the cited distribution *before* comparing previous and current states. “Likely ≥2 or shed threshold” (`plan:203`) is not a reproducible rule; the configured shed threshold and the proposed quiet-line floor need an explicit relationship. If C collects before `:690-693`, it will miss this Cycle's local shed result.

The row's `barTerm` is local and available before drift (`applyBusinessDynamics.js:597-619`). It is calculated for `bizIsBar_`, which includes **all food-class rows and food/hospitality sectors**, not only bars (`:281-285`). `sportsBarTerm_` combines home venue share and away-game nightlife watch-party reach, multiplied by the **signed result** (`utilities/sportsWeekRecord.js:345-360`). Therefore `barTerm > 0` does not establish a home game or a packed bar; it can describe a positive away-game term at a restaurant. Gate a home-game/bar claim on actual per-game venue and a real bar classification plus attendance evidence, or use wording limited to the signed sports uplift the row actually has.

`d.disrupted` is a seeded one-Cycle **growth shock**, not a recorded closure, repair, or reopening (`applyBusinessDynamics.js:451-454`, `:478-485`); actual closure takes the separate streak/revenue branch and is excluded (`:647-688`). The proposed “closed for repairs this week” remains unsupported even when conditional and even when the pulse closure regex is avoided (`plan:203`; `utilities/neighborhoodPulseMap.js:75-79`). `declines[id]` is a number of shed tracked-equivalent jobs, not evidence that a particular “usual face behind the counter” is gone (`applyBusinessDynamics.js:690-695`). `ps.streak≥floor` to `d.streak===0` proves the decline streak ended, not measured customer traffic. Narrow the lines to disruption/layoff/growth facts, or supply the missing repair, employee, and traffic records. Rendered-name regex tests guard an accidental vitality pulse but do not establish the claimed event.

Cut C's stable selection and per-hood receipts are substantial improvements (`plan:203`), yet the older live directives at `plan:190-197` still prescribe `win`, unconditional repairs, old lines, and a different bench. Mark them explicitly superseded or replace them so the build has one executable Step 8 contract. 8b likewise needs a POPID-aware `pickVenue_`: its present signature knows only neighborhood and draws from a generic hood texture list (`generateCitizensEvents.js:1393-1396`), with four call paths (`:913`, `:3442`, `:3509`, `:3526`). A 2-of-3 own-spot choice is conditional on a venue call; it does not imply that two thirds of ordinary citizen lines will name the spot. Measure both denominators and preserve `ctx.rng` use for the 2-of-3 choice.
