# Twelve items are due at C118. The tracker shows no evidence that the records behind most of them exist.

*By Luis Navarro, Managing Editor*

Here is what we know and what we don't.

I went through the Initiative Tracker as it stood at C117. Twelve initiatives carry a next action due at Cycle 118. Most of those actions are some form of "publish the record." They include a briefing record, a comment-and-response record, a milestone review, a placement-gap analysis, a legal-support analysis, a planning record and a scope record.

I can't tell you the city will miss a deadline that hasn't arrived. I can tell you which ones start the final cycle with no evidence of the underlying work. For those, the tracker's own C117 language is the proof.

## What the record says is missing

**Fruitvale Transit Hub Phase II.** Due at C118: "Deliver preliminary schematics and publish the documented Fruitvale public comment-and-response record." The C117 milestone says that both the schematics and the record "are not evidenced in the packet." Further design advancement is held. The tracker also lists the initiative as "complete," so the hub's own planning lead says that entry closes only "the visioning and council-authorization history." The unfinished work sits outside it.

**Oakland Alternative Response Initiative.** Due at C118: execute and publish the D3 Fruitvale briefing record, and file written next dates for the D5 audit and the Baylight feasibility review. Expansion is held until all three exist. At C116 the Mayor directed the briefing to be executed and published. At C117 the tracker still says "pending a published" record. That is two cycles of direction with no record shown. Council members Delgado and Rivers, the Mayor and the program director all say the same thing in separate statements.

**Baylight District.** Due at C118: "Conduct and publish the construction milestone review against the assembled evidence file." The review was ordered at C115. At C117 the tracker says no milestone pass is declared and the review "must be published." The Baylight Authority director's own statement holds the determination pending. It also promises to publish "the scope and basis of the C115 completed entry" and "the evidence-file location." So the evidence file's location is not yet public.

**Youth Apprenticeship Pipeline.** Due at C118: publish the placement-gap analysis and adjustment plan with explicit West Oakland and Fruitvale review. The initiative is recorded as stalled. Four separate officials (Carter, Delgado, Rivers and the Mayor) demanded the same document at C117. It was already demanded at C116.

**West Oakland Tenant Defense Fund.** Due at C118: publish the legal-support analysis and document the opening of intake. The fund passed 6-3 at C112 and has been "operational" since C113. The C117 milestone says that publication of the analysis and the opening of intake "remain unverified." A fund can be called operational with no verified intake, and that is the gap here.

**Downtown Economic Revitalization.** Due at C118: execute and publish Uptown stabilization planning with a timeline and benchmarks. The tracker shows that at C112 and C113 the committee convened and an Uptown track was committed "for C114." The Mayor has now recorded the initiative as stalled because Uptown planning execution "is not evidenced." The $15 million authorization stays pending.

## Deadlines the city has already said it will pass

Three items need no forecast, because the city has told us.

- **Dimond Revitalization:** the filing was due at C117. The Mayor recorded "the missed filing deadline" and moved it to stalled.
- **Ivy Hill Community Revitalization (INIT-013) and Laurel Neighborhood Patch and Clean-Up (INIT-015):** the Mayor and Deputy Mayor Okoro both said they will let the filing deadlines pass. Their reason is that INIT-013 has no distinct-scope record and INIT-015 has no finished scope and outreach record. That is honest, but it is still a missed date.
- **Ivy Hill Community Support (INIT-014):** council review was due at C117. Councilmember Vega is withholding review until the Clerk supplies a filing reference or confirms no filing. The Mayor says the filing "remains unverified" and withdrew any completed-filing implication. The tracker had shown legislation as filed at C115. Whether anything was filed is still an open question.

## Where the picture is less clear

**West Oakland Stabilization Fund.** Due at C118: verify the C114 closeout, then take up charter renewal. Council members Carter and Crane and the Mayor all condition renewal on that verified closeout. I found no verification in the packet, so renewal depends on a document I can't see.

**Grand Lake Commercial Stabilization.** The vote is scheduled for C118. Every voice we have, including Council members Crane, Ashford and Chen, backs YES with the Chen-Vega conditions. But the tracker is explicit that no Clerk-certified roll call exists yet. A scheduled vote is not a missed deadline. A certified result is the thing to watch.

**Ivy Hill Revitalization (INIT-011).** A committee referral and hearing are due at C118. The Mayor "backs" them, but no completed referral or hearing is evidenced.

## One office that says it will file

The Temescal Community Health Center must file its Q3 intake audit with HCAI and the Council Clerk, and document the Telegraph & 47th merchant walkthrough. Oversight certification is withheld until both happen. Operations continue.

Project Director Bobby Chen-Ramirez put his office on the record. At C117 he committed to both items this cycle. He also supplied the clearest account I've heard of why the filing matters.

"Look, I'm a health center guy, not a tracker guy," he told us. "But that word — 'publish the record' — that's the part that keeps me honest. When I file the Q3 audit with HCAI and the Clerk, and when I walk the building with the shopkeepers at Telegraph and 47th, that's not paperwork for its own sake. That's the neighborhood checking the work."

He added: "So if the city's missing deadlines on publishing records, that's not a bureaucratic failure to me. That's a promise broken to the people who need to see the proof."

His office is the one with a stated filing plan tied to a standing quarterly schedule. I'll count whether it lands.

## What I don't know

- I don't know whether any of these documents exist in draft. The tracker records evidence in "the packet," and a document could exist elsewhere. I will file records requests with the Clerk and the project offices.
- I don't know why the same demand has gone unmet across several cycles. That is a question for the offices, and I'll ask it.
- I have not confirmed any filing at the Clerk's office. Where the city says "unverified," I have kept that word.

## What I'll be counting

C118 is the next checkpoint. For each item above, the test is whether a public, dated, inspectable record exists. A new directive restating the old one doesn't pass. On that test, six initiatives start the cycle with the underlying work unevidenced: Transit Hub, OARI, Baylight, Apprenticeship, Tenant Defense and Downtown. Three more have already been recorded as missed or held. I will report the count when the cycle closes.
