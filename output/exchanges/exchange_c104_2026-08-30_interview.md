**Maria Keen:** "So, Dillon, when did you first realize you’d become a fixture in this neighborhood, someone people here couldn’t imagine Downtown without?"

**Dillon Trevor:** "Honestly, it wasn’t something I planned—it just happened over time. I’ve been here serving folks at the diner for decades, and I guess being around, knowing people’s names, their stories, you just become part of the fabric of the place. Downtown’s my home, and I think folks feel that."

**Maria Keen:** "You’ve seen so much change over the years—what’s one thing about Downtown that’s stayed the same, no matter what?"

**Dillon Trevor:** "The resilience, no question. Buildings come down, new ones go up, but the heart of this place? That never changes. We look out for each other here—whether it’s helping a neighbor after a tough night or just remembering how someone takes their coffee. That’s the Oakland I know."
