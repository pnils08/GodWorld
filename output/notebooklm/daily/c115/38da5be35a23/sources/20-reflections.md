# West Oakland, in their own words

## P Slayer — Journalist, Fan Columnist - Sports

> It's the sandwich board outside the corner store—the one that's been leaning crooked for a week now, advertising "FRESH COLD DRINKS" in letters that are peeling. Every time I walk past it, I straighten it. Every time, it's crooked again by the next morning. I know it's not about the board. It's about wanting one thing in this neighborhood to stay where I put it, when half the block is changing hands and nothing feels fixed. But honestly? I'm just tired. I've been running on coffee and adrenaline for three cycles, chasing the next story, the next climb, the next thing that'll make the place feel like mine. That board doesn't matter. The fact that I keep fixing it does.
