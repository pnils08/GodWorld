## 4. Comprehensive Evaluation of Flagged Drafts

### 4.1 Draft Audit: "Fruitvale Transit Hub: The Tribune Said Construction Had Started. The Record Says It Has Not."
*   **Author:** Luis Navarro, Managing Editor, Investigations  
*   **File Reference:** `output/cron-compare/flagged/civic_c117_luis-navarro_packet-v2_anthropic-claude-haiku-5-5.md`

#### What Was Reported
Navarro’s draft alleged that *The Tribune’s* Cycle 97 article ("Ground Breaks in Fruitvale") erroneously reported that construction had physically started on Phase II of the Fruitvale Transit Hub (INIT-003), contrasting it with Cycle 101 reporting ("Fruitvale's Bargain Comes Due") which stated the project remained in construction-planning. Navarro cited the city initiative tracker showing INIT-003 remaining in the design phase following Cycle 86 visioning completion, with no active construction milestones or payments. 

Navarro quoted Planning Lead Elena Soria Dominguez stating construction had not begun, quoted Councilmember Rose Delgado regarding the council hold on design schematics pending a public review on the first Thursday of next month, and cited Mayor Avery Santana’s directive that design work remain paused until comment logs are published. Navarro also highlighted an internal vote discrepancy, noting he had not reconciled the Cycle 87 report of a 7-1 council vote against the C86 tracker's "projected 6-3" authorization count. Finally, Navarro included transit figures attributed to Soria Dominguez showing Fruitvale station ridership at 8,007 (down from 9,343) and on-time running at 0.85 (up from 0.83).

#### Gate Finding & Flag Reason
*   **Recorded Blocker Trigger:** `deterministic world-contamination blocker failed`

#### Cross-Source Evaluation & Fact-Checking
A cross-examination of Navarro’s draft against published primary sources confirms significant factual errors and unverified assertions:

1.  **Site Preparation vs. Construction:** In the verified primary source (*Citizens in their own words — Fruitvale*), Elena Soria Dominguez explicitly states:  
    > *"The machines on that lot were clearing and grubbing under the old C86 authorization — that's site prep, not design work."*  
    Navarro’s draft claimed Soria Dominguez denied all physical on-site activity and asserted the project existed only "on paper." Navarro failed to distinguish between authorized site preparation (clearing and grubbing under C86) and full Phase II construction, creating an internal factual contradiction between his text and the direct source quote.
2.  **Scope of Design Hold:** Soria Dominguez clarified in primary records that the held scope applies specifically to *design advancement, schematics, and the comment-and-response record*, not physical site clearing authorized under C86. Navarro conflated site clearing with premature construction, misrepresenting the scope of the council hold.
3.  **Unverified Transit Metrics:** Navarro explicitly admitted in his text that he imported Soria Dominguez's transit metrics (8,007 riders; 0.85 on-time running) without secondary verification against official system records (*"These are her figures, given on the record. I have not checked them against the transit metrics sheet"*).

#### Conclusion on Gate Decision
The gate decision **is fully justified and holds up under audit**. Navarro’s draft contained factual mischaracterizations regarding site activity (clearing/grubbing site prep under C86 vs. Phase II construction) and incorporated unverified data points without secondary checking. The gate desk correctly triggered the `deterministic world-contamination blocker` to prevent unverified and contradictory copy from being published.
