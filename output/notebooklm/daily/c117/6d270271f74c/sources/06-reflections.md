# Fruitvale, in their own words

## Manjit Singh — Senior Pastor / Faith Leader

> The first thing I did in my new place was unpack my grandmother's old tea set; my friends from the community helped me move. I decided to prioritize getting the water heater fixed over a weekend trip because I wanted to settle in comfortably.

## Vladimir Gonzalez — Right Fielder, Oakland A's

> Man, when I saw that number on the screen—$119 million over five years—I had to sit down for a second. That's not just a contract; that's the organization telling me they believe in what I've been building. Coming up through the minors, you dream about this, but you never really know how it's gonna feel until it's real. The way it worked out with Mark heading to Colorado, and me getting that same money... it's a little surreal. I know this game is a business, but it's hard not to take it personally in the best way. Paulson didn't just hand me a raise; he handed me the keys to right field for the next five years. That's trust. I've been grinding my whole career to get to this point—staying late, studying pitchers, cutting down on those strikeouts. Last season finally felt like everything clicked. The power was there, the average was there, and I proved I can be a complete player. I know there are guys like Desmond and Frank pushing for time, and that's good for us as a team. But I plan to make it impossible for Paulson to take me out of that lineup. Now I've got the security to really settle in, buy a place in Fruitvale for good, and keep doing what I love. The money's life-changing, sure, but what it really means is stability—for me, for my family back in the DR, and for my future here in Oakland. I'm ready to earn every dollar of it.

## Marcus Whitfield — Youth Basketball Coach

> The court at Cesar Chavez Park, before anyone else shows up. The one with the chain nets that rattle when the ball goes through clean. I like it when the fog’s still burning off and the only sound is my sneakers squeaking and the ball bouncing. No drills, no kids yelling, no parents on the sideline. Just me and the hoop. I’ll shoot free throws for a while. Not thinking about the season, or the new baby, or the rent. Just the routine. Bounce, breathe, shoot. The net does that thing where it snaps and then hangs still for a second. That’s the closest I get to quiet these days.

## Elena Soria Dominguez — Transit Hub Planning Lead

> The machines on that lot were clearing and grubbing under the old C86 authorization — that's site prep, not design work. The held scope is the design advancement, the schematics and the comment-and-response record, and that hasn't moved an inch until we answer every Fruitvale comment in the language it came in.

## Rose Delgado — City Council District 3

> The Fruitvale community review is set for the first Thursday of next month at the Fruitvale Senior Center, 6 p.m., with the design team in the room — and yes, the held design covers the site work, because we're not letting construction outrun the community's say.
