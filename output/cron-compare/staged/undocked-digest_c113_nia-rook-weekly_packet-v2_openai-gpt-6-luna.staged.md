# UNDOCKED: Cristina Booker’s double-digit burst couldn’t carry the whole week

Three pilots flew this week, but the board’s loudest story belongs to the gap between Cristina Booker’s best night and her next one. Eric Taveras remains the name at the top: rank #1, 20 achievement points, 1,170 credits, three cycles led, and a current streak of three. Cristina’s Monday made a case for a climb; Thursday left that case hanging.

In the hallway, that is the kind of split that turns a recap into an argument. Monday, Cristina went through Alpha Centauri, took the Copper Requisition and Welcome to Sol Central contracts, and earned two five-point achievements: First Steps and Maiden Jump. She came out even on credits and flew until the show’s time ran out. Ten points is a real burst. But on Thursday, she never got the ship moving. That is not a week that settles where she belongs on the board; the supplied record gives no current rank for her.

Father Miguel’s week had a steadier shape, though not a bigger points return. On Tuesday he traveled through Lacaille 9352, Sirius, Sol and Alpha Centauri, took the Copper Requisition contract and earned Maiden Jump for five points. Friday brought a different kind of work: he mined two aluminum ore and one platinum ore, but earned no new points. Both nights ended even on credits. His own read is apt: “I guess you could say I'm more consistent, but she's more explosive when she gets it right.” He describes himself as 22nd out of 24, with five points so far.

Daphne Stafford also came away with five points, earning First Steps on Wednesday, but her ship never got moving that night. She described the board as barely budging around her: “I'm 23rd out of 24 on the leaderboard, and I only got 5 points last week.” The week’s record gives her a point-making moment, not a launch into the standings.

There is an older UNDOCKED entry worth keeping beside the current summit: C111 records Eric Taveras earning 1,170 credits on a night that included Sirius. That earlier episode record is not a new C113 flight, and it does not answer how the others rank today. It does underline the scale of the leader’s listed credit standing while this week’s three pilots each had to make their own case in the chair.

So the board leaves two questions open: can Cristina turn a ten-point night into consistency, and can either Father Miguel or Daphne Stafford convert a modest points return into movement? For now, Eric holds the summit, while the week’s cast leaves the next rung unsettled.
