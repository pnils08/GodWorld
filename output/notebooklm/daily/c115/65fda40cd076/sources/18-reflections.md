# Rockridge, in their own words

## Benji Dillon — Pitcher, Oakland A's Legend

> To be honest, I've noticed people recognizing me more often around Rockridge and at the grocery store. It feels a bit surreal, but I'm trying to stay grounded. My neighbor even taped an article about me to our building's front door, which was a surprise. I'm just trying to keep doing what I do and not let it change me too much.

> Anthony Raines is a friend, and I'm happy to see him covering the A's. He's got a good eye for the game, and his writing does justice to the team. It's nice having him around Rockridge; we chat about the A's whenever we run into each other.

## Anthony Raines — Journalist, Lead Beat Reporter - Oakland A's

> Benji Dillon's a Rockridge icon, and his influence on the local culture is huge. I've seen it firsthand - people around here still talk about his games, his style. He's one of us, you know? The neighborhood loves him, and he loves it back. I've had conversations with him about the community, and he's always been passionate about giving back. His presence here isn't just about baseball; it's about being a part of the fabric of Rockridge.

> As a rising name in Rockridge, I've gotten used to people knowing who I am and what I do. It's not something I think about much anymore, to be honest. I just do my job and try to be a good neighbor. I've lived here long enough that it's just part of the fabric of my life. People will sometimes stop me at the corner store or mention something about my stories, and that's nice. It's a good feeling to be recognized for the work you do, especially when it's for something you care about as much as I do about the A's. Benji Dillon's still the big name around here, though - he's a Rockridge icon. I've had some great conversations with him about the community and the team.

## Elijah Brown — Electrician

> I flew on UNDOCKED Thursday night and went through a bunch of systems until the show's time ran out. I ended up even on credits. My standing is 3rd of 30, with 38.35 points and 0 credits, so it didn't seem to hurt me.
