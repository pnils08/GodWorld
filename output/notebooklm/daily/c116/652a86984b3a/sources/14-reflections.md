# Piedmont Ave, in their own words

## Adash Stanley — PG / The Oaks

> *leans back in the chair, letting the screen glow fade for a second* Yeah. That Denver game. That's the one we've been building toward. 147 points, 80 percent from the field — I've been around this game a long time, and I've never seen a box score that looks like that. Five guys on our team didn't miss a shot. I was one of them. Six-for-six, three-for-three from deep. That's not a hot night, that's a statement. And then San Antonio came in and reminded us we're still human. First home loss. That one stings, I won't lie. But you know what? It happened in a Cup game, we were tied going into the fourth, and we just went cold. That's basketball. You don't hang your head over it when you're 9-2 and sitting at number one in the power rankings. The thing I keep coming back to — that Denver game — that's the ceiling. That's what this group can do when everything clicks. Wilson's got us playing with a pace that teams can't match, T. Jerome's been a flamethrower off the bench, and the ball movement? Forty assists on sixty-three makes. That's not isolation basketball, that's a team that trusts each other. The mood in the room is confident, and it should be. We just got punched in the mouth for the first time at home, and now we get to see how we respond. That's what separates the good teams from the ones that make a real run.

## Wendell Carter Jr. — C / The Oaks

> Man, I ain’t gonna lie — that Denver game was one of those nights you just live for. Everything we ran, everything we shot, it all felt like it was on a string. 147 points, 80 percent from the floor, 40 assists and only 5 turnovers? That ain’t just a good night, that’s a statement. I went 8-for-8, grabbed 9 boards, and honestly felt like I could’ve kept going. That’s the kind of rhythm you chase your whole career. But that Spurs loss — that one’s still sitting heavy. We let one slip at home in front of our own crowd, and that’s not who we are. We got cold in the fourth, left points at the line, and turned it over 14 times after only 5 in Denver. That’s the difference between a great team and a championship team. We can’t get comfortable off one historic night. Tyrese has been keeping us alive, I’ll say that. 23 off the bench in 21 minutes, six threes, two steals — that’s the kind of spark that changes a game. But we can’t keep relying on one guy to bail us out when the starters go flat. Coach Shep’s got us at 9-2, and yeah, the mood in the room is frustrated. But that’s good. That means we care. We know what we’ve got in that locker room, and we know we’re better than what we showed in that fourth quarter. We’ll tighten it up. We always do.
