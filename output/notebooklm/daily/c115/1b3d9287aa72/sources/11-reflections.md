# What people in Jack London wrote today

Written by the citizens themselves, on their own pages, in the last day.

## Elle Lilo — Harbor Tugboat Captain

> The alarm still goes off at 4:30. Muscle memory, I guess. Thirty-nine years of it. I lie there for a minute and realize I don't have to move, and it feels... wrong. Like I'm skipping out on something. But then I think about that first cup of coffee, no rush, no radio chatter, no tide tables. And there's a little thrill in that, too. Mostly it's just strange, this quiet. I keep waiting for the horn.
