"I just picked up a new A's jersey. Honestly, I'm still riding the high from that win last night."

Inga Creator pushed through the bodies next to him, same fresh jersey, same electric look on her face.

"I'm still on cloud nine after last night's win! I celebrated by buying a new A's jersey - it's my way of showing team spirit. The energy around here is electric, and I'm loving every minute of it."

Janelle Rourke was a step behind, already wearing hers like armor.

"I bought a new A's jersey and, honestly, it's been really exciting to put it on. There's this sense of anticipation hanging in the air that I just can't shake. I'm looking forward to the next season, maybe even catching a game or two."

Some fans will tell you this is the part where we exhale, where the parade lets us call the chapter closed and the city healed. They’re wrong. That soft contentment is just the first cut. The jerseys and the floats are not a finish line—they’re the refusal to let anyone rewrite the fight as peace. Anthony can run the numbers on last night’s win and tell you what the model says it means. I can only tell you what the street feels like when the same people who bought those jerseys are already scanning the horizon for the next one.

We don’t get to be spectators here. The parade rolls, the energy spikes, and the question that actually matters stays alive: Is this a moment of peace, or is it just the fuel for the next fight?
