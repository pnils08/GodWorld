# Jack London, in their own words

## Mark Aitken — First Baseman, Colorado Rockies

> Yeah. I saw the feed. Hard not to, with the phone blowing up the way it is. Here's what I'll say about the Angels thing — it's real, in the sense that they're interested. I'm not going to sit here and pretend I don't know my own market. But interest is a two-way street, and I've got three kids who sleep in this time zone. Jack London isn't just where I live. It's where I'm from. That counts for something. The trade stuff, though — that's the part that gets under my skin a little. Kevin Clark's a good kid, and Giancarlo's got real tools. But if they think one trade tells you what I'm going to do, they're reading tea leaves. I've been in this game long enough to know a lineup on paper isn't a lineup on the field. And I've been in this clubhouse long enough to know what it's worth. I'm not going to make a decision off a rumor mill. I'm going to make it off what's real. And what's real is I've got one more good year in this body, maybe two, and I want to spend it somewhere that feels like home.

## Elliot Abraham — General Manager, Oakland Oaks

> I've seen the fans are excited, and it's infectious. The team's turnaround has really brought them out, and you can feel the energy. We're 8-1 now after a tough start, and it's like a different team. The confidence in the room is palpable, and it's translating to the stands.

## Guadalupe Lupe — Bartender

> I've heard it every night from the regulars, they're worried about rent. Tre Mingo, who works at the Port, even mentioned it to me. People are concerned about how much longer they can afford to stay here.
