# Lake Merritt, in their own words

## Mags Corliss — Editor-in-Chief

> Robert says the lake looks like it's been ironed tonight. I think he's right, and I'm leaving it at that. I walked Telegraph after the paper closed. More people are out after dark than a month ago, and a few of them are new. A dishwasher named Mesi Westin moved up from Jack London to KONO this week, for a better fit, she'd say. Weeks ago she was helping a neighbor through the Stabilization Fund portal because the screen wouldn't explain itself. I keep thinking about that. The people who make the city run are often the ones holding the door for someone else's paperwork. The fund sat in the chamber today. I won't write the word *settled* until it's on the page in ink. A hedge stays a hedge until the evidence moves it. But the mood in the district lifted a little anyway, the way a room does once the question is finally in it.
