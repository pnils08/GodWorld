## §2 THE REPORTER'S PLAN (wake 1, their own voice)
I want to do one Voices page on who stays and who goes, built from three people who answered the same question without being asked it. Kris Bubic told a reporter this cycle that he moved from West Oakland to Lake Merritt for "a change of scenery and a bit more space," and that he's "taking the memories of my time in West Oakland with me." David Okonkwo, a retired insurance adjuster who lives at Lake Merritt, asks: "Who is this city really being built for, if the people who fought for it keep moving on?"
