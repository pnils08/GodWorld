# pipeline.73 Step 1 wiring card — Codex review

**HOLD the Step 1 card at `docs/plans/2026-10-11-media-extension.md:203–225`.** The proposed tag rule misses real hospital cases, the once-per-event state key cannot represent two events for one citizen, and the Intake mint does not carry the promised MED flag. The seven verdicts below are source review, not live-Sheet acceptance. The named output is this file only.

1. **FIX — tag set versus writers (high).** Birth is a real parent event with a minted child (`phase04-events/generationalEventsEngine.js:556–567`, `:1163–1184`). A career `[Promotion]` reflects an income increase and `LastPromotionCycle` (`phase05-citizens/runCareerEngine.js:426–438`; `phase05-citizens/educationCareerEngine.js:367–384`). `LifeEvent-Promotion-Up-M` is a *held break*, not a completed promotion: the writer stamps the M line and mark, while an eventual raise uses the separate Promotion writer (`phase05-citizens/runCareerEngine.js:549–559`, `:579–586`). `LifeEvent-Promotion-Up-L` is not produced by this writer: the L branch promotes and `continue`s before constructing a LifeEvent tag (`phase05-citizens/runCareerEngine.js:570–580`). Retirement is a stamped milestone and possible power-vacuum arc, but this path does not change the citizen's Status or CareerStage; calling it a structurally completed retirement needs a separate proof (`phase04-events/generationalEventsEngine.js:625–633`, `:1579–1607`).

   `Hospitalized` and `Critical` can be real health transitions (`phase04-events/generationalEventsEngine.js:786–807`, `:872–903`), but chaos cars also stamps those tags for high-severity outcomes even when its status-change guard does not admit that citizen (for example a retired citizen); a tag alone does not prove a Hospital_Ledger stay (`utilities/chaosCarsConfig.js:203–211`; `phase04-events/chaosCarsEngine.js:443–465`, `:523–531`). `Recovery` is mixed: the health lifecycle emits it for a discharge *or* recovery from an injury (`phase04-events/generationalEventsEngine.js:835–880`), and the OARI intervention emits the same tag (`utilities/chaosCarsConfig.js:222–226`). `Recovering` is mixed: household wellness and low-severity chaos use it (`phase05-citizens/runHouseholdEngine.js:379–394`, `:565–574`; `utilities/chaosCarsConfig.js:179–183`, `:218–225`). The card's exact hospital sentence **does identify the current hospitalized→recovering writer** (`phase04-events/generationalEventsEngine.js:786–808`), provided matching is on the complete current-Cycle line, not a truncated tail. No second hospital-grade `Recovering` wording was found in the current writers.

   The proposed set still misses hospital-grade events under other tags. Severe `Health` is admitted as hospitalized, while moderate `Health` can be injured or serious-condition; the same tag also covers ambient ailments (`phase04-events/generationalEventsEngine.js:1439–1477`, `:654–687`, `:690–714`; `phase05-citizens/runHouseholdEngine.js:384–388`, `:565–568`). `Stabilized` can mean critical→hospitalized or serious-condition→hospitalized; `Setback` can mean recovering→readmitted, while other producers use those labels for non-hospital events (`phase04-events/generationalEventsEngine.js:824–855`, `:891–903`; `utilities/chaosCarsConfig.js:179–180`, `:218–225`). A Hospital_Ledger missed-admission reconciliation can add a stay from Status with **no corresponding new LifeHistory tag**, explicitly including sports injury (`phase10-persistence/buildCyclePacket.js:1041–1069`). Death is stamped, then the deceased exits the living ledger; this slot has no survivor tag or routing (`phase04-events/generationalEventsEngine.js:637–647`, `:1721–1736`; `lib/wakePerception.js:380–400`). The plan itself leaves the survivor policy open (`docs/plans/2026-10-11-media-extension.md:223–225`), despite the builder's death-included wake ruling at `:184–185`. **Bounded correction:** classify a current-Cycle line together with writer provenance or typed status/ledger transition; decide Death's living recipient explicitly. Do not treat the regex as a complete hospital/death definition.

2. **FIX — opt-in pool isolation (medium).** `buildPool()` currently defaults to `SHAPED_MIN=60`, `LIFE_MIN_CHARS=25`, and filters missing dials, life, name and hood (`lib/wakePerception.js:26–27`, `:376–412`). The current callers are `scripts/citizen-wake.js:288`, `scripts/citizenVoice.js:252`, `scripts/citizen-exchange.js:429`, `:440`, and `scripts/employerSuccess.test.js:530`; the plan's `:35/:49/:40` caller line numbers refer to imports or old locations. Voice/exchange already opt out of the shape/life floors with their own options, but none passes `livedCycle`. A strictly gated `livedCycle` branch can leave their returned arrays byte-for-byte unchanged, including object shapes and order. That is a required regression test, not an implemented fact: Step 1 is still a proposal.

   The card filters `livedOnly` from posture and rotation, yet forced selection and voiced selection occur **before** those branches and currently use the whole pool (`scripts/citizen-wake.js:164–182`, `:189–207`). Explicitly decide whether forced `--pop` may override the lived-only boundary and ensure voiced selection cannot accidentally consume that row. Also choose a current-Cycle line from the full LifeHistory *before* the 3-salient/2-texture tail is formed; the tail can drop a matching line (`lib/wakePerception.js:413–425`). Test both unchanged no-option callers and all four wake branches with a lived-only fixture.

3. **FIX — once per event and Cycle boundary (high).** `state.lived[popId] === cycle` is once **per person per Cycle**, not once per event. Career and generational events both run in Phase 5, with Career first; a citizen can acquire a Promotion and a Birth in the same Cycle (`phase01-config/godWorldEngine2.js:640–647`; `phase04-events/generationalEventsEngine.js:556–567`; `phase05-citizens/runCareerEngine.js:426–438`). A single `livedTag` and person/Cycle key will suppress one of those event lines. Conversely the existing voiced branch does not reject recently woken IDs, and forced selection ignores recency (`scripts/citizen-wake.js:164–182`); a citizen can wake by lived and later by voiced/forced, or wake by rotation and then by lived if only lived wakes set the new key. Use a stable event identity such as POPID + Cycle + normalized tag + full line or a durable source receipt, then define whether a prior wake for the *same event* satisfies it. Avoid representing multiple events with one tag field.

   The Cycle is resolved from `--cycle`, else `getCurrentCycle()` (`scripts/citizen-wake.js:41–45`, `:286–293`). That helper prefers the newest local `output/world_summary_cN.md`, then `base_context.json`; it does **not** read the live counter for each cron (`lib/getCurrentCycle.js:34–51`, `:64–106`). A Sunday engine fire between the three daily wakes, or a delayed summary build, can put the wake on either side of the Cycle boundary. Because the proposed pool matches `absCycleOf(line)` to only the passed `livedCycle` (`lib/citizenDials.js:89–95`), an old-Cycle event still pending at the rollover can be dropped permanently, while a stale resolved Cycle misses new events. Name the authoritative update/retention rule and test a fire between crons plus delayed summary. Source alone does not establish the live scheduler's exact interleaving.

4. **PASS dry-run guard; FIX durability claim (medium).** Today there is one `saveState(state)` call, after the `if (DRY) ... process.exit(0)` branch (`scripts/citizen-wake.js:373–377`, `:455–458`), so putting `state.lived` into this same state object and saving at that call keeps it off disk on dry runs. Tension and ripple saves are also after the guard (`scripts/citizen-wake.js:420`, `:453`). But `saveState` silently swallows write failure (`scripts/citizen-wake.js:81–82`), and the page append occurs before the state save (`:387–395`, `:455–458`). A failed local save or process exit after page append can cause a second wake for an already published reflection on a later cron. The proposed “once per event” guarantee needs an explicit save/readback or replay reconciliation with the page receipt; do not mark an event spent before a successful page write, since that loses it on failure.

5. **FIX — household dedupe and age floor (medium).** The married-parent Birth path requires a populated HouseholdId; the newborn copies it, so household+Birth can collapse parent and newborn (`phase04-events/generationalEventsEngine.js:528–548`, `:1072–1078`, `:1153–1183`). The second parent's `ChildrenIds` is updated, but that code does **not** stamp the second parent with Birth (`:1187–1207`). The rare solo path forms or reuses a household before stamping the parent (`:573–597`). For unrelated rows whose HouseholdId is blank, a literal empty-household+tag key collapses them all into one candidate. Dedupe by household only when nonblank; otherwise retain separate POPIDs. The task's POP-00510/POP-00801 blank-household observation was not independently read from live Sheets.

   `buildPool` derives `age` as `currentSimYear() - BirthYear`, else the empty string (`lib/wakePerception.js:423–430`). The Node helper obtains that year from local snapshot metadata first, then a world summary (`lib/citizenDerivation.js:27–41`), which may differ from `citizen-wake`'s separately resolved Cycle near rollover (`lib/getCurrentCycle.js:68–92`). Require a valid BirthYear and compare a numeric age calculated from the *same* Cycle used for tag selection. Do not let a blank/invalid age pass by JavaScript coercion or silently become an adult.

6. **FIX — mint door (high).** A row with a new nonempty name and no normalized-name collision goes to the new-citizen branch even without MatchPopId. An ambiguous duplicate is cleared/skipped; a unique name match updates the existing citizen instead; a family/household/owner payload changes collision handling (`phase05-citizens/processAdvancementIntake.js:690–745`, `:748–795`, `:795–805`). The new branch copies `ClockMode` (MEDIA if the header/value exists), authored RoleType, Tier capped at 4, validated/folded Neighborhood, Gender override, BirthYear, and CitizenBio (`:624–646`, `:701–716`, `:806–844`, `:899–901`). There is no general review-status gate in this pass; rows are processed and cleared (`:1005–1030`). The plan's character facts and actual pre-fire name checks remain outside this source proof.

   **The claimed MED flag does not mint through this door.** The intake reader does not resolve a MED column (`phase05-citizens/processAdvancementIntake.js:624–646`), and `newRow` starts blank and is assigned no `MED (y/n)` value before `ledgerRows.push` (`:827–845`, `:959–978`). A queued MED cell is ignored. The branch also keeps a carried EmployerBizId only if Business_Ledger has stated headcount room; otherwise it re-picks an employer or sentinel (`:914–956`; `:1491–1522`). Therefore `ClockMode=MEDIA` can land while MED stays blank and the requested Tribune EmployerBizId can reroute. This is **HOLD for the two proposed journalist rows** until the card specifies a supported MED write and verifies the employer's actual capacity, then reads back both minted rows. `EmployerBizId` should name the confirmed Tribune business, rather than infer it from the job title.

7. **FIX — slot order and budget (medium).** Forced and voiced remain first; adding lived after voiced and before posture gives lived precedence over both posture and rotation (`scripts/citizen-wake.js:164–207`). `docs/OPERATIONS.md:53–70` lists morning, midday and night wakes, so 21 is the documented weekly call count. The card's 6 lived + 7 posture + 4 voiced = 17 is a scenario, not a cap or reservation. If an eligible lived backlog is nonempty at every non-voiced call, rotation gets zero; if the backlog exceeds the remaining calls, posture can also get zero. The card has no fairness rule, per-Cycle quota, or aging policy to prevent it (`docs/plans/2026-10-11-media-extension.md:214`, `:219`). A Cycle rollover can discard pending lived candidates rather than drain them (verdict 3). Add a bounded lived allocation or explicit priority/fairness acceptance and test a >21 candidate week; report whether event coverage or rotation is allowed to lose in that case.
