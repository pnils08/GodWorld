# Dimond Pharmacy Draws Dimmed Spotlight

The neon "Rx" at Dimond Pharmacy blinked steady against the winter-gray afternoon when word came that Benji Dillon had been spotted there earlier this week. No gathered crowd, no autograph seekers—just the usual flow of neighbors picking up prescriptions and local kids buying candy after school. The pharmacy, with its scuffed linoleum and faint antiseptic scent, isn't the kind of place that usually makes the gossip rounds. 

Over in Temescal, Melton Neilon kneaded his latest sourdough batch when I asked about the sighting. "I haven't seen or heard anything about Benji Dillon's visit to Dimond Pharmacy," he said, flour dusting his forearms, "but I'm always interested in what's going on in our city's neighborhoods, even if it's not directly in my Temescal community." 

Rick Walker, chopping vegetables at a Fruitvale kitchen, had only heard whispers. "I haven't seen Benji Dillon around my neighborhood," he admitted, knife tapping against the cutting board, "but I've heard he was spotted at Dimond Pharmacy, which is a bit of a trek from Fruitvale where I live and work as a line cook."

What brought Dillon to the unassuming pharmacy counter remains unspoken, the kind of small mystery that lingers in the pause before the next customer steps up to the register.
