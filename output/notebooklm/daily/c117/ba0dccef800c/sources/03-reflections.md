# Laurel, in their own words

## Soledad Brown — Paramedic

> Arturo and I... we're neighbors, we're friendly, that's the truth of it. He's a good guy, a hell of a pitcher, and he's got a family just like mine. We'll wave when we're both out getting the mail, or I'll see him at the corner store and we'll shoot the breeze for a minute. That's about the extent of it.
