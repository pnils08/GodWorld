# engine.276 cut — adversarial review

**Scope:** source review of the unbuilt cut at `docs/plans/2026-09-21-care-and-justice-system.md:624-661`, commit `1b3b15e6`. No engine change or live/bench run. Severities assess the mechanism, not the chosen dial values or sim direction.

## Findings

1. **HIGH — The default mark will disappear in Phase 9.** `utilities/citizenMemory.js:256-270` is the deserializer the fold actually calls at `utilities/compressLifeHistory.js:564`; the fold writes with its *own* `serializeDialState_` at `utilities/compressLifeHistory.js:679,1302-1309`. The cut lists two whitelist lines in `citizenMemory.js` (`docs/plans/2026-09-21-care-and-justice-system.md:650,661`), but `serializeDialState_` does not carry `debtDefault`. On a normal fold RMW, it drops the mark on the default Cycle, so the 52-Cycle home bar and lifetime count do not survive. `emitPressureTag_` itself parses the current cell and writes the same object back (`utilities/citizenDialMap.js:423-440`); it does **not** clobber the mark. **Fix:** add `debtDefault` to both `deserialize_` and `serializeDialState_` (and `serialize_` for the shared round-trip contract), then test default → pressure tag → Phase 9 fold → next-Cycle readback with an actual fold-triggering life line.

2. **HIGH — The claimed level-6 ceiling omits active mint writers, including one after the money loop.** `deriveDebtLevel_` explicitly clamps to **10** (`utilities/citizenDerivation.js:317-331`). Bond spouse and lottery mints (`phase05-citizens/bondEngine.js:2409-2413,2602-2603`), promotion/intake mints (`phase05-citizens/checkForPromotions.js:392-393`; `phase05-citizens/processAdvancementIntake.js:846-876`), and the age-18 settlement (`phase05-citizens/educationCareerEngine.js:1221-1229`) can therefore write 7–10. The age-18 writer runs *after* `Phase5-GenerationalWealth` in both orchestrator paths (`phase01-config/godWorldEngine2.js:623-624,2319-2320`), so its new level cannot be normalized by this Cycle's loop. Existing 7–8 values also remain above 6 until a successful fall/default; a capped rise alone does not normalize them. Thus the cut's “writers stop at 6” inventory and “no level above 6 written” bench claim (`docs/plans/2026-09-21-care-and-justice-system.md:640,661`) are false for the whole Cycle. **Fix:** decide the debt scale at every live mint boundary, align the derivation/seed contract or explicitly preserve the 7–10 exception, and assert the chosen invariant after *all* Phase 5 writers, not just the money loop.

3. **MEDIUM — Default changes net worth after the wealth-level pass, leaving the stored face stale.** `processGenerationalWealth_` computes `WealthLevel` at step 2 (`phase05-citizens/generationalWealthEngine.js:146-152`) and only then runs the money loop (`:172-176`). `calculateCitizenWealth_` reads `NetWorth` to set the level (`:1115-1123`), while the proposed default sets `NetWorth` to zero inside the later loop (`docs/plans/2026-09-21-care-and-justice-system.md:650`). Step 5 mobility reads that already-written level (`phase05-citizens/generationalWealthEngine.js:187-190,1654-1660`). A default can therefore persist `NetWorth=0` beside its old `WealthLevel` and miss a same-Cycle mobility change. The same ordering applies to large shock/payoff changes already, but default makes the discrepancy a designed event. **Fix:** recompute the wealth level from final `NetWorth` before mobility/home/commit, or explicitly defer the derived face and its downstream claims until the next Cycle; cover a high-worth default in a Cycle-level test.

4. **MEDIUM — Same-Cycle debt consequences are read before the new debt move.** Both orchestrator paths run `Phase5-Maneuver` and `Phase5-Bonds` before `Phase5-GenerationalWealth` (`phase01-config/godWorldEngine2.js:596,620,623,2292,2316,2319`). Maneuver's retreat is decided from the old `DebtLevel` (`phase05-citizens/maneuverEngine.js:127-133,267`), and bond fitness copies and scores the old debt (`phase05-citizens/bondEngine.js:2073-2096,2020-2029`). The cut's default or rise can change the ledger and pressure this Cycle but cannot change those decisions until the next one. **Fix:** state the one-Cycle lag in the cut and test it, or move the debt move before these readers only after checking their other dependencies and RNG order. Do not use a 20-Cycle count of debt moves as proof of same-Cycle downstream effects.

5. **MEDIUM — The added rolls move the existing random stream, including shocks and home purchases.** The current loop draws one shock roll per eligible adult and extra draws only on a shock or windfall (`phase05-citizens/generationalWealthEngine.js:331-335,353-389`). A lean roll per adult, plus a default roll for top holders, inserted before or among these draws changes which random values every later citizen receives; `trackHomeOwnership_` then uses the same `safeRand_(ctx)` stream (`:1706,1742`). Casino settlement runs before the loop (`:158-176`) and keeps its earlier draws, but later Phase 5 decisions shift. The cut specifies “one roll a Cycle” for lean and an additional top-level default chance (`docs/plans/2026-09-21-care-and-justice-system.md:647,650`) without a draw-order contract. **Fix:** specify the exact per-row order and whether the lean roll is consumed at the line/cap and on absent hood data; compare a fixed-seed draw trace with the old path, then declare the intended downstream shift. A same-seed 20-Cycle aggregate cannot establish draw equivalence.

6. **MEDIUM — The proposed 20-Cycle bench cannot prove the lasting mark or long-run claims.** The mark lasts 52 Cycles (`docs/plans/2026-09-21-care-and-justice-system.md:650,653`); the cut's estimates cover one to four years, repeat defaults, and crime reach around C139 (`:655-657`). Twenty fires can show some defaults and immediate home skips, but cannot show the mark surviving to its expiry, a blocked then admitted purchase at the boundary, or the quoted annual/repeat-default outcomes. A purchase roll is rare and may never be eligible in that window (`phase05-citizens/generationalWealthEngine.js:1710-1742`). **Fix:** use deterministic local boundary tests for mark start/last barred Cycle/first unbarred Cycle and lifetime increment; use a longer controlled bench or label the long-run numbers as model estimates rather than bench-proven behavior. For the home test, force a rented, otherwise eligible household containing the marked member and verify the gate runs before its RNG draw.

## Checked claims and limits

- `careerRecordLayoff_` is called by custody dismissal, employer-success layoff, and headcount reconciliation (`phase05-citizens/runCareerEngine.js:349,461,1782`); `S.careerSignals` is initialized before these calls (`:647-661`). The proposed `laidOff` carrier is reachable if added at that shared helper. Employer-success is the live `LastPromotionCycle` writer (`:442-443`); `educationCareerEngine.js:529-533` explicitly does not stamp it.
- The existing crisis predicate is rent at least half of household income **and** savings below annual rent (`phase05-citizens/generationalWealthEngine.js:311-328`). The loaded hood rent rule derives rent from income (`phase02-world-state/loadNeighborhoodState.js:118-130`). The cut's C109 “0 of 532” is a live-data assertion, not independently verifiable from source; the predicate is not an unconditional `false` in code.
- Existing money-loop, casino, and custody settlement debits floor net worth at zero (`phase05-citizens/generationalWealthEngine.js:349,369,383`; `phase05-citizens/casinoLedgerEngine.js:325-334`; `phase05-citizens/judicialLifecycle.js:428-439`). The proposed payoff and default need to preserve that floor; no new negative-net-worth path is established by the cut itself. The home gate reads living household members from `Members` and rolls only after affordability checks (`phase05-citizens/generationalWealthEngine.js:1710-1742`), so the mark must be checked against those resolved member rows before the roll.
