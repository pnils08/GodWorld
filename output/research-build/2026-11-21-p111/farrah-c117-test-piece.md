# Nobody Decided Anything in Cycle 117, and Everyone Called It a Decision

Lay the week's city statements next to each other and a pattern shows. The word "hold" appears in nearly every one. Cycle 117 produced dozens of recorded decisions from the Mayor, the Deputy Mayors, eight council districts and the project directors. Very few of them decide anything. They are promises to produce a record later.

Here is the ledger.

**Baylight, $2.1 billion.** Director Keisha Ramos decided to "hold the construction milestone determination pending." She will publish the scope and basis of the C115 "completed" entry, the evidence-file location and the remaining determination work. Read that again. The tracker carried the C115 entry as complete, and this cycle the city said no milestone has passed. The Mayor's office said it twice: "declare no milestone passed." The review is due Cycle 118. So a $2.1 billion project was marked complete, and the basis for that mark is now something the Director promises to explain.

**Fruitvale Transit Hub, $230 million.** The Transit Hub lead says the "complete" tracker entry closes only the visioning and authorization history. The design work, the schematics and the comment-and-response record are still owed. The word "complete" can mean "the paperwork about the thing is finished." Residents will read it as "the thing is finished."

**OARI, $12.5 million.** Expansion is held. Three records stand between the program and growth: the D3 Fruitvale briefing record, a written next date for the D5 audit, and a written next date for the Baylight feasibility review. The Mayor, Council Districts 1, 3 and 5, and the program director all said the same thing. That is five voices for one hold. I don't count that as consensus. It means no one wants to be the person who produced the record.

**Dimond.** The filing deadline for the $2.5 million ordinance passed. The Mayor's office recorded the miss and moved INIT-012 to stalled. I'll give them credit for that. But the tracker still lists the next step, "File legislation," as due Cycle 117, and the cycle is over.

**Ivy Hill.** Three initiatives with overlapping names: INIT-011, INIT-013 and INIT-014. INIT-013 is stalled to avoid a duplicate filing. INIT-014 is listed as vote-scheduled, yet the Mayor's office says its filing "remains unverified" and has withdrawn any completed-filing implication. A measure can't be scheduled for a vote when no one can confirm it was filed. Someone has to explain how it got that status.

**Laurel, $6 million.** Announced. The deadline passed because scope and outreach records don't exist.

**Apprenticeship, $12.5 million.** Stalled. Four council members and the Mayor demand a placement-gap analysis. Nobody has said why placements fell short in West Oakland and Fruitvale, the two places the program was meant to reach.

**Temescal Health Center, $45 million.** It stays operational, but oversight certification is withheld until the Q3 audit is filed and the Telegraph & 47th walkthrough is documented. Both are due Cycle 118. This is my neighborhood, so I will be at that walkthrough.

**West Oakland Stabilization Fund, $28 million.** The Tribune's own headline this cycle says the fund paid out while "the closeout that gates its renewal is still unverified." The program director's line in that story says the closeout is "published." Published is not verified. Council members Carter and Crane both made verification a condition for renewal. If the closeout were verified, there would be nothing to wait for. Webb himself said the renewal isn't claimed "before the roll call."

I asked nothing of Deputy Mayor Brenda Okoro that she didn't already answer. She put it plainly: "I know how this reads from the outside — holds, deferrals, records that take another cycle to land. It can look like a pattern of stalling dressed up as process." Then: "That gap between a promise and a delivered record — that gap is where trust either gets built or gets broken." And: "when I say a record is coming, it comes."

I agree with every word. It is also a standard she can be measured against. Her statement is the test, and every item above has a due date of Cycle 118.

To be fair, there is a case for the city. A hold is better than a false "complete," and the Mayor's office deserves credit for withdrawing filing claims it couldn't back. Saying "no milestone passed" is honest. The Grand Lake vote shows this too: every official says YES, with the Chen-Vega conditions attached, and none claim an outcome before the Clerk certifies it.

My complaint is that honesty about a gap does not close the gap. Fifteen initiatives are on the tracker. In this cycle's record, most of their next steps are a document, a date or a verification. Residents can't live in a hold.

So here is my demand. By the close of Cycle 118, publish the Fruitvale briefing record, the Baylight evidence-file basis, the apprenticeship placement-gap analysis and the Clerk reference for INIT-014. If any of them slips again, someone with a name should say why. Then we can stop calling the waiting a decision.
