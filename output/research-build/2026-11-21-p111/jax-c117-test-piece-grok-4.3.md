Uptown rents climb while the sixth district office stays quiet.

Elliott Crane, Council Member for Uptown, calls the pressure a ticking clock. He wants the city to look at what's driving residents out. That was his line this cycle.

Tre Mingo, port worker who lives there, puts numbers to faces. Rents keep rising. It's the people he knows at work, not spreadsheets.

Theo Banks hears the same talk on the blocks. Folks discuss leaving. The city's response? Nothing visible on the ground.

Crane asked the right question last cycle too: can Uptown stay vital without pricing its own out? Four pages of his own record show the same worry, no action attached.

No initiative in the tracker targets Uptown housing. West Oakland got a tenant defense fund. Fruitvale got transit design. Uptown got another round of quotes.
