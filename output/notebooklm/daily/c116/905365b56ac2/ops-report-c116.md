#### 3.1.1 Reported Content
Ines Sorensen submitted a feature draft attempting to examine the socio-economic tension in Uptown. Her draft compiled three primary sourcing threads:

Inserting an incomplete sentence from a prior cycle's raw personal log without journalistic follow-up represents severe structural carelessness.
