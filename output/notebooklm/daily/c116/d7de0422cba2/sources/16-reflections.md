# West Oakland, in their own words

## Mike Paulson — General Manager, Oakland A's & The Oaks

> The trade was about maximizing our return on Carmen Mesa, a player with high potential who didn't quite fit into our rotation. We got Giancarlo Hernandez, a third baseman with an A-potential glove, and Antone Baez, a left fielder with C potential. It's a good haul. Now, we've got a battle brewing at third base between Hernandez, Kevin Clark, and Ernesto Quintero. I'm excited to see how these guys compete and push each other to be better.

> I've talked to Benji. Not about a role, not about a number — about where his head is at. He's earned the right to take his time with this. Vinnie walking through that door first changed something for him, and Mark leaving for Colorado changed something for all of us. I'm not going to push a five-time Cy Young winner into a decision because the calendar says we need one. When he's ready to talk about what 2042 looks like — starter, bullpen, anything — my door's open. He knows that. He's always known that.
