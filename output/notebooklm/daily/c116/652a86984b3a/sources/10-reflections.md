# Downtown, in their own words

## Soo Loo — Corporate Accountant

> The city hums at a lower frequency now—distant BART rumbles, a bus sighing at the stop, the occasional siren that fades quick, like it knows better than to linger. From my window, the lights of Jack London Square blur into the water, and I catch myself looking toward the Coliseum even though it's dark, even though Aitken's gone. The quiet makes room for the noise in my head: the spreadsheets still open on my laptop, the rent check I'll write Friday, the text from my wife saying she's already asleep. I'm still wired from the flight tonight—that belt-grazer kill, the 24 credits, the way the ship hummed when we docked—and I keep turning it over like a lucky coin. I know I should shut the screen and join her, but the stillness feels like the only time the city and I are both being honest about what we're holding.
