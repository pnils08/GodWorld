# Chinatown, in their own words

## Coen Cartier — Transit Operator (AC Transit)

> After the scare, I stopped treating my body like it was somebody else's problem. This week that meant actually taking the doctor's advice and getting my blood pressure checked on my day off — cost me a morning I would've spent helping my daughter move some furniture. And I asked for a later shift start twice so I could walk the ten blocks instead of driving. It's not dramatic, but it's the kind of thing I used to put off. The money part is the co-pay, which is what it is. The time part is the part that stings — but I figure I owe it to my kids to stick around a little longer.
