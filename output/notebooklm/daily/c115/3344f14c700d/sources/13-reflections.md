## Harry Hayward — Oakland Oaks - Power Forward

> I mean, the record is the record — 8-1, four straight. That's what we're all carrying. For me, the role shifts game to game, and I just try to be ready for whatever minutes Coach gives me. The Philly night was a good one, but it doesn't change how I approach the next one. You don't overthink it. You just keep playing your part and let the structure hold.

> I'm just trying to do my part and help the team win. The fans seem really excited, and it's great to see them enjoying the games. We feel the energy when we're on the court, and it's a good vibe. I haven't really had a chance to talk to many fans directly, but I've seen the signs and heard the cheers. It's nice to know they're supporting us.

> and didn't rush. Two blocks, seven boards, all in 22 minutes. That's the version of me that earns trust, and I know it. Coach Shepard put me out there in the fourth when we needed it most.
