Hazel Salazar works the line at Fruitvale Diner.

"A typical shift at Fruitvale Diner starts early for me. I come in, get the kitchen prepped, and start cooking breakfast orders. It’s busy, but I like the rhythm of it—flipping eggs, plating pancakes, keeping things moving. The regulars come in, and it feels like a little community. By lunch, it’s more sandwiches and salads, and I’m juggling orders with the other cook. It’s steady work, and I take pride in making sure people leave satisfied."
