## Vinnie Keane — Former Designated Hitter, Oakland A's Legend

> I keep waking up at the same hour I always did, before the sun's up, and then I remember there's nowhere to be. That's the strange part—not the quiet, but the lack of the thing that used to pull me out of bed. I've been walking Grand Lake in the mornings, just watching people head to work, and I catch myself looking for the box score in someone's phone like it's still my job to know. Amara says I'm circling it, and she's right. I'm not sad, exactly—it's more like the bat's still in my hands and I keep checking to see if it's got another swing in it. But the kids at the Academy don't care about any of that. They just want to know if I'll show up tomorrow, and that's been the truest thing all week.
