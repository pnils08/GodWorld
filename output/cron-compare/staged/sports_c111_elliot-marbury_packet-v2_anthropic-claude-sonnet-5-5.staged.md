# Richards Struck Out Less in 2039, 2040 and 2041 Than in Any Season Before Them

Martin Richards, the Athletics third baseman, hit .302 in 2041 with 27 home runs and a .551 slugging average over 463 at-bats. He struck out 70 times, once every 6.6 at-bats. His career line shows that rate has stood outside his earlier band for three straight seasons.

## The claim

The claim is narrow. In 2039, 2040 and 2041, Richards struck out less often per at-bat than in any of the five seasons from 2034 through 2038. The power did not shrink to pay for it.

From 2034 through 2038, his strikeout rate ran from one every 5.1 at-bats to one every 5.6. In the three seasons since, it has run from 6.6 to 7.9. No season on file crosses between those bands.

## The hinge, and what it is made of

The hinge is 2039. It is the best strikeout rate on file, and it comes from the thinnest full-time sample: 90 games and 348 at-bats, with 44 strikeouts. A season that short is variance until another one confirms it. Two did. The 2040 line (499 at-bats, one strikeout every 6.7) and the 2041 line (463 at-bats, one every 6.6) are both near the size of his 2034 through 2038 seasons.

The better evidence is those two seasons, not 2039. The rate moved from 7.9 to 6.7 to 6.6, so the 2039 figure has not held. It has settled lower, and the band is still well clear of the 5.1 to 5.6 that preceded it.

The home-run rate runs with this. His 2038 season, at one every 16.9 at-bats, is the closest earlier match to 2041's one every 17.1. In 2038 he also struck out once every 5.1. In 2041 the home-run rate is about the same and the strikeouts are fewer.

## What the line does not show

Two columns run against the contact reading. His walks fell from 62 in 2040 to 35 in 2041, and his on-base average fell from .377 to .355. That is the lowest on-base figure since 2034's .342. The strikeout improvement did not arrive with more on-base value in 2041. His slugging average of .551 is the highest on file, so the 2041 gain came from power. This memo does not reach whether the walk drop is a pattern or one season.

The line also says nothing on cause. The career record does not say why the rate changed in 2039, and no one has been asked.

## What would kill the claim

If Richards logs a full season of about 460 at-bats or more in 2042 and strikes out once every 5.6 at-bats or more often, he is back inside his 2034 through 2038 band. The 2039 through 2041 stretch would then be a three-year dip, not a change. A rate at 6.5 or above across a full season would extend the claim to four seasons.
