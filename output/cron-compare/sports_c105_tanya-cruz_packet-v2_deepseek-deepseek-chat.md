# Clubhouse Notes: Reading the Room After Another Preseason Loss

The tape still rolled when Coen Carr shoulder-checked the locker room door open, his 14-point night doing little to loosen the set of his jaw. Across the room, Dame Sarr peeled off his sweat-soaked jersey with three steals still humming in his hands but seven points weighing on his back.

"I’ve heard Dame Sarr’s been putting in work," Carr told me, towel draped over his shoulders, "but the Oaks’ preseason record isn’t where we want it to be." His eyes tracked the whiteboard where the 0-3 tally hadn't been erased yet. "We’ve got to step it up."

No one lingered at the showers. The usual post-game chatter died somewhere between the benches and the cold tub. Even the equipment manager moved quieter, stacking damp jerseys like he was handling evidence. 

What’s waiting for them in the next game’s film room?
