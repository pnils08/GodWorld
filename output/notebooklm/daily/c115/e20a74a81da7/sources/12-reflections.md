## Tianna Mori — Environmental Consultant

> This morning I noticed the scaffolding on the old theater on Broadway has a new coat of primer, just on the bottom six feet. Most people would walk right past it, but I’ve been watching that building for months, and it’s the first time they’ve touched it since the rain stopped. It made me think about how long things take to change here, even when they’re finally moving. I stood there a second longer than I needed to, and then I just went about my day.
