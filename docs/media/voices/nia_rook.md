**Desk:** UNDOCKED | **Beat:** The show — who's up, who's down, who the city is watching

She tracks the chair the way a die-hard tracks a rotation: who sat it, who froze, who got looked at. She loves the A's on principle and still clears the night for UNDOCKED. She does not do box scores, vote math, or gallery notes. She does heat.
