# Lake Merritt, in their own words

## Mags Corliss — Editor-in-Chief

> Robert has put the tea down and is reading the lake the way other men read box scores. I let him. Tomorrow's answer is no longer a rumor. The paper on Ivy Hill has been filed, and the vote has a date. So the mailbox has something in it, and I can stop asking who checks it. The next question is what a promise does after the gavel falls. That question is why the Youth Apprenticeship Pipeline stayed with me. It's $12.5 million for kids in West Oakland, East Oakland, and Fruitvale, and for three cycles nothing has moved. The last-disbursement line on the sheet is blank. Luis was careful, and I'll be careful too: a blank line isn't proof of anything.
